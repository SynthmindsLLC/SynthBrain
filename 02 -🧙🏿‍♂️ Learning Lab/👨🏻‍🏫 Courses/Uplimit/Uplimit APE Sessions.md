# Week 1
1. **Introduction to Session Goals and Topics (5 minutes)**: Set the stage with a brief overview of what learners will achieve by the end of the session.
    
2. **Quick Review of Prompt Engineering Fundamentals (10 minutes)**: A concise review to ensure a shared understanding of prompt engineering basics, setting the groundwork for more advanced concepts.
    
3. **Interactive Demonstration: Evolving a Basic Prompt (15 minutes)**: I will demonstrate transforming a basic prompt into an advanced one using the trip planning example. This includes integrating few-shot learning, Skeleton of Thought, and Chain of Thought, with real-time input from participants to guide the demonstration.
    
4. **Brief Breakout Session: Brainstorming Rules and Commands (10 minutes)**: Participants brainstorm specific rules and commands in small groups or pairs for a short duration. This encourages thinking about how to tailor prompts to achieve desired outcomes.
    
5. **Group Reconvening: Test and Discuss (15 minutes)**: After the breakout, we'll reconvene to test some of the brainstormed rules and commands live, demonstrating their impact and discussing the results. This provides immediate feedback and learning from real examples.
    
6. **Q&A, Key Takeaways, and Wrap-Up (5 minutes)**: Conclude with a session for questions and a summary of key learning points, ensuring participants leave with clear takeaways and understanding.

# Week 3
1. **Introduction and Overview (5 minutes)**
    
    - Brief recap of the journey through Weeks 1 and 2, emphasizing the build-up to Week 3's critical topics.
    - Set clear expectations for the session, highlighting the focus on practical tool usage and security in prompt engineering.
2. **Streamlined Demo of ChatGPT Tools (15 minutes)**
    
    - Provide a focused demonstration of ChatGPT tools, emphasizing those features most relevant to crafting secure and effective prompts. This will include tips for utilizing these tools in tandem to enhance prompt design and response quality.
3. **Interactive Session on Prompt Hacking (10 minutes)**
    
    - Present a concise overview of prompt hacking, focusing on understanding these techniques to better defend against them. Transition into the broader context of prompt security.
4. **Group Activity: Crafting Targeted Defenses (10 minutes)**
    
    - Facilitate a group activity where participants brainstorm and develop targeted defenses against specific prompt hacking scenarios. This encourages strategic thinking and application of the session's concepts.
5. **Simplified Challenge: Testing Key Defenses (10 minutes)**
    
    - Selectively test the crafted defenses in a simplified challenge, focusing on a few key scenarios to assess the effectiveness of the defenses. This practical application will demonstrate the real-world value of the session's teachings.
6. **Deep Dive into Security Considerations (10 minutes)**
    
    - Explore critical security considerations in prompt engineering, covering best practices for ensuring prompt integrity, privacy, and ethical compliance.
7. **Discussion, Q&A, and Wrap-Up (5 minutes)**
    
    - Open the floor for discussion, allowing participants to ask questions, share insights, and reflect on the practical exercises.
    - Summarize the session's key learnings, emphasizing actionable steps for securing prompts and effectively utilizing ChatGPT tools.
