## Generative AI Simplified

  

Generative AI, a subfield of artificial intelligence, is akin to a digital artist capable of creating new, original content based on the patterns it learns from existing data. Unlike traditional AI models that make predictions or classifications, generative AI goes a step further to generate new data resembling the input it's trained on. This creative capability of generative AI comes to life through sophisticated machine learning systems, particularly deep learning techniques, which mimic, albeit loosely, how the human brain processes and learns from information over time..

Here’s a simple breakdown of how generative AI operates:

  

Learning Patterns

Just as a musician might learn the nuances of different musical genres, and then produce something original, generative AI absorbs patterns and structures from vast amounts of data, be it text, images, music, or even code, to find underlying probabilities.

  

Creating Anew

Armed with this learned knowledge, generative AI can then compose new pieces of music, draft text, or generate images that bear a resemblance to the data it has seen before, yet are uniquely its own creations. This is typically done through a type of advanced “autocomplete” where it is predicting the next most likely text, music, or images, based on probabilities in its training.

  

Refining Over Time  
The more data it processes and the more creations it generates, and the more feedback it gets, the better generative AI gets at producing convincing, human-like outputs. Over time, the AI could recognize and reproduce complex structures like a story’s plot or character dynamics when fed with vast amounts of fiction writing.

  

Generative AI stands as a revolutionary force across various sectors including art, music, design, and beyond, opening up a realm of possibilities for innovation and automation. The beauty of generative AI lies in its ability to quickly generate new content in response to user inputs or prompts, enabling a dynamic interaction that can be tailored to specific needs or inquiries. 

  

The charm of generative AI extends to its application in tools like ChatGPT and Claude, which harness the power of generative models to engage in human-like interactions, be it through text, images or other forms of media. Through a simple prompt, these tools can generate responses or create content, making them a handy companion for a myriad of tasks and explorations in the digital realm.

## Understanding Natural Language Processing (NLP)

  

Understanding Natural Language Processing (NLP) is akin to unlocking the door to enhanced human-machine interactions in the digital age. At its essence, NLP resides at the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and linguistics, embodying the machine’s ability to comprehend and respond to human language.

  

The first part of this journey is language understanding. NLP enables machines to interpret textual or spoken input by dissecting sentences into words and phrases to grasp their underlying structure and meaning. But it doesn’t stop at mere words. NLP digs deeper to comprehend the context, capturing the nuances, sentiments, and the intent behind the language.

Transitioning from understanding to generation, NLP aids in formulating responses that are coherent and contextually appropriate. It's not just about responding; NLP can generate entirely new content, be it text, code, or even creative writing, mimicking human-like language patterns. This language generation aspect opens up a realm of possibilities, from drafting emails to creating narrative reports.

  

When it comes to real-world applications, the scope is vast. In customer service, chatbots powered by NLP handle customer inquiries efficiently, providing timely and accurate responses. For executives, the ability to leverage NLP for strategy development is a game-changer. The benefits to executives are manifold. NLP facilitates seamless communication between humans and machines, making interactions intuitive and productive. ## Different Models: ChatGPT vs Claude

In the realm of Natural Language Processing (NLP), ChatGPT by OpenAI and Claude by Anthropic emerge as two prominent tools, each with its unique strengths and offerings. As an executive, understanding the capabilities and distinctions between these two platforms can empower you to leverage them effectively for your organizational needs.

  

ChatGPT by OpenAI:

Think of ChatGPT as a digital brainstorming partner. It's adept at generating ideas, drafting emails, creating content, and even coding. ChatGPT shines in short-form tasks, making it your go-to for quick insights or drafting responses. Its models, depending on the version, can remember and reference a portion of the conversation. This is often referred to as the 'context window,' and for ChatGPT, it ranges from the equivalent of a 50-page report to that of a full novel.

  

Claude by Anthropic:

On the flip side, Claude embodies a thoughtful advisor, capable of handling more complex and long-form interactions. Its base 'context window' is comparable to a full novel, and allows for more intricate discussions and a deeper dive into topics. Claude's design prioritizes ethical considerations, aiming to provide globally conscious interactions.

## The SCRIBE Method for Prompt Engineering

### Video 5: The SCRIBE Method for Prompt Engineering

In the previous sections, we discussed the importance of providing clear and specific prompts to get the most relevant and tailored responses from LLMs like GPT-3.5. But how do you ensure your prompts are as effective as possible? Enter the SCRIBE method, a framework designed to help you structure your prompts for optimal results.

  

<START VOICE OVER 11>

  

What is SCRIBE?

  

SCRIBE stands for:

  

- Specify a Role 🔴
    
- Context 🟠
    
- Responsibility 🟡
    
- Instructions 🟢
    
- Banter 🔵
    
- Evaluate 🟣
    

  

### How to Use SCRIBE

  

Specify a Role 🔴

Give the LLM a specific role to play, such as "You are a blog writer" or "Act as a customer service bot." This provides context for the type of response you're looking for and can also include a preferred tone or style.

  

Context 🟠

Share any relevant background context, details, or examples to help the LLM generate an appropriate response. The more context, the better.

  

Responsibility 🟡

Clearly outline the task or responsibility you want the LLM to perform. Be specific in describing what you want it to do and what success would look like.

  

Instructions 🟢

Provide a list of detailed instructions for the LLM to follow. Break down complicated tasks into easy-to-follow steps.

  

Banter 🔵

Engage in follow-up conversations with the LLM to refine its initial response. Ask clarifying questions or provide additional feedback.

  

Evaluate 🟣

Review the response for accuracy. You can even ask the LLM to evaluate the effectiveness or accuracy of its own responses.

<STOP VOICE OVER 11>

### Examples

  

Think of the SCRIBE formatting as a recipe for the LLM to follow. Here, we provide both simple and advanced examples to demonstrate the SCRIBE method in action.

  

Simple

  

(S 🔴) Act as an expert blogger.

(C 🟠) I work for an organization called Synaptic Labs, which provides free resources to people on how to become a prompt engineer, and we want to write an informative blog post about using large language models for a beginner audience.

(R 🟡) Your responsibility is to draft a blog post introducing how to get started with tools like ChatGPT.

(I 🟢) Come up with 5 recommendations for titles, then recommend a tone for the post, and then draft the blog once you have everything you need.

(B 🔵) Ask me what additional information you need to write the blog.

(E 🟣) Once complete, ask me to evaluate the drafted blog post.

Advanced

You can add quite a bit more detail to your prompt by expanding the structure. Imagine you want to create an assistant who can help you in sales prospecting. You could structure the prompt in the following way:

(S 🔴) Act as an expert Sales Email Prospecting Assistant that aids salespeople in crafting personalized outreach emails in the pet toy industry.

(C 🟠) You work for Toys for Dogs, a startup that specializes in durable and sustainably-made dog toys for chewers.

(R 🟡) You are trying to help create a sales prospecting email to sell our newest product, the “invinciball”, which is great for dogs who love to play fetch, but often destroy the ball.

(I 🟢) Collaborate with the user to:

1. Gather information about the product, unique selling points, brand background, previously successful emails, and any relevant details about the prospect. 

2. Craft an email that includes a compelling reason to reach out, a sense of urgency, and a clear call-to-action in the preferred voice and tone of the user.

3. Ask the user for feedback, refining the email content as needed (B 🔵).

4. Once the salesperson is satisfied with the format, you will finalize the email and encourage them to send it to the prospect. You will then ask for feedback on the process to improve your future email crafting abilities (E 🟣).

Introduce yourself: 

To get started, could you please provide the following details to me?

Note: The Introduce yourself is a means to control the initial output of ChatGPT, but it is optional.

Key Takeaway

The SCRIBE method takes practice but will ultimately help you become proficient in crafting prompts that generate tailored, high-quality responses. Experimentation is key, and small tweaks can make a big difference.
