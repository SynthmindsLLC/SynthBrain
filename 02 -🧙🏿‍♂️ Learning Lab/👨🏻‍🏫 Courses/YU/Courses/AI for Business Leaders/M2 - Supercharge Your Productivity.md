### Identifying Your Pain Points

Before you can leverage AI to generate ideas that solve problems, you need to know what those problems are. As an executive, you're likely juggling multiple responsibilities, from strategic planning to team management and stakeholder communication. ## 2.2 AI for Meeting Summaries

### Video 3

Meetings are a necessary part of executive life, but they can also be a significant time sink, especially when it comes to summarizing key points and action items. Fortunately, AI can come to the rescue, offering efficient ways to capture the essence of your discussions. Whether you're looking for an integrated solution that handles everything from transcription to summarization, or you prefer a more hands-on approach using Large Language Models (LLMs), AI has got you covered. In this section, we will explore two approaches to using AI for meeting summaries: one through specialized integrated tools like Fireflies.ai and another using manual summarization with LLMs.

  

### <START VOICE OVER 4>

### Using a Dedicated Summary Tool

  

When transitioning from meeting to meeting, there is often little time to jot down notes, let alone summarize them. Maybe you have someone who takes notes for you, or you capture a transcript, but those can also be difficult to navigate or create consistency around. You need a solution that's as busy as you are, one that takes care of the mundane so you can focus on strategy. Luckily, there are a variety of existing AI-fueled tools that can do this all for you.

  

First, you'll want to pick the right tool for the job. Do a quick search and you'll find a few options, but let's say you go with Fireflies.ai. You sign up and integrate it with your go-to video conferencing platform—be it Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet. This is usually a straightforward process, and you only have to do it once. From then on the AI will be invited to your meetings, and will capture a recording and the transcript to be fully summarized.

  

When the meeting wraps up, you'll find a complete transcript waiting for you along with time stamps, a summary, and even the ability to “chat” with the transcript. Different tools have different capabilities, the ability to “chat” with the transcript will allow you to ask AI questions about the content of the meeting, request that it create lists of tasks for you or your team members from the content of the meeting notes, and even provide constructive feedback on effective communication techniques. It is still important to take a few minutes to review it. The AI is good, but it's always a good idea to make sure everything is accurate.

  

Finally, you can share this invaluable summary with your team, along with the generated list of action items. And there you have it—a streamlined, efficient way to handle meeting summaries, all thanks to AI.

  

Depending on what you already have available, you will want to consider using the tools you already have incorporated into your workflow. Zoom recently released their own integrated summary tool, and Microsoft and Google are beginning to offer their own AI-infused tools to do the same within their existing ecosystems. These all often come at an additional cost, and will vary in quality and user-friendliness, so it’s important to take these tools for a test drive before committing fully.

### Using an LLM Directly

When it comes to summarizing meetings, you have two main routes you can take. The first we described, which is to use specialized tools designed for this purpose, like Fireflies.ai, which can transcribe, summarize, and even help you interact with your meeting notes. These tools are fantastic for those who want an all-in-one solution.

  

The second route is a bit more manual but offers greater flexibility. You can use Large Language Models (LLMs) to do the summarizing for you. While GPT-4 is a popular choice, Anthropic's Claude is particularly well-suited for this task due to its extended context window of about 75,000 words. This makes it ideal for taking a full meeting transcript and distilling it into concise notes and action items.

  

Imagine you've just finished a critical meeting, which you’ve recorded using Microsoft Teams - a function that allows you to also capture the transcript. You have the transcript, but it's lengthy and filled with tangents. You need the core insights and next steps. Here's how you can use Claude to get what you need:

  

1. Gather the Transcript: Make sure you have the full transcript of your meeting. This could be from a recording or manual notes.
    
2. Access Claude: Navigate to the [Anthropic's Claude platform](https://claude.ai/login).
    
3. Input the Transcript: Paste the entire transcript into Claude's text box.
    
4. Instruct Claude: Ask Claude to summarize the meeting, focusing on key points and action items. For example, "Please summarize the key points and action items from this meeting."
    
5. Generate and Review: Hit 'Generate' and wait for Claude to produce the summary. Always review for accuracy and context.
    
6. Draft Communications: You can even take it a step further by asking Claude to draft emails or other communications based on the summary and action items.
    
7. Share: Once you're satisfied, share this summary with your team in the format that works best for you.

### Using an LLM Directly

While there are specialized SaaS tools designed to streamline these tasks, foundational Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT or Claude offer a more flexible, customizable approach. These models are the force that power tools like Writesonic.

  

The key to unlocking the full potential of these foundational LLMs lies in prompt engineering and maintaining a strong prompt library, which is a repository for all your internal prompts as an organization, which we will discuss more deeply in Module 6. By crafting precise prompts, you can guide the AI to generate content that aligns closely with your specific needs.

  

Let’s walk through an example of how you might use a foundational LLM tool to write a prospecting email.

  

Step 1: Prepare Your Prompt

The first step is to prepare your prompt. As we’ve discussed, the prompt serves as a set of instructions that guide the AI in generating the content you need. Below is a sample prompt designed to act as an "Expert Sales Email Prospecting Assistant" agent. 

  

Act as an expert Sales Email Prospecting Assistant for [company] that aids salespeople in crafting personalized outreach emails for any industry or business type. You adapt to the specific details provided by the user regarding the product, brand, and prospect.

  

Collaborate with the user to:

1. Gather information about the product, unique selling points, brand background, previously successful emails, and any relevant details about the prospect. 

2. Craft an email that includes a compelling reason to reach out, a sense of urgency, and a clear call-to-action in the preferred voice and tone of the user.

3. Ask the user for feedback, refining the email content as needed.

4. Once the salesperson is satisfied with the format, you will finalize the email and encourage them to send it to the prospect.

  

Variables:

[Company] =

[Industry] =

[Tone]=

[Audience]=

[Values/Mission] =

  

Introduce yourself:

"Hello! I am your personal expert Sales Email Prospecting Assistant. I specialize in crafting compelling prospecting emails in [tone] for [audience] to engage potential customers for [company] in [industry] with [Values/Mission]. To get started, could you please provide the following details to me?

  

1. What are the details about the product you're selling?

2. Tell me about the unique value add of your product, or the problem it solves for the prospect.

3. What relevant information is there about the prospect you're reaching out to? 

4. Optionally, can you share an example of a successful email you or a colleague has used in the past?

  

Once I have this information, we can begin the email crafting process together." and wait for the user to respond.

  

Step 2: Customize the Prompt with Detailed Company Information

  

In this step, you'll be adding text next to all the variables with specific, detailed information about your organization and needs. This is a one-time investment of effort that will pay dividends across all future prompts you use, since it will act as a variable that the LLM will use as context to tailor its responses, making them more aligned with your company's specific needs, culture, and objectives.

  

Elements to Include:

  

1. Company Name: Replace with your company's name. This is straightforward but essential for personalization.

  

2. Industry: Briefly describe the industry you operate in. This helps the AI understand the market dynamics and regulations you might be dealing with.

  

3. Tone: Describe the tone and style of your company's communications. Are you formal and professional, or casual and relatable?

  

5. Audience: Who are your primary customers? What are their pain points and needs?

  

6. Company Values and Mission: What does your company stand for? What are its long-term goals?

  

Copy and paste the prompt into your word processor of choice, and fill out all the variables before continuing.

#### Example

  

Instead of just replacing `[company]` with "TechSolutions," you could expand it as follows:

  

TechSolutions is a SaaS company specializing in cybersecurity solutions. Our USP is our patented AI-driven threat detection system. We pride ourselves on our customer-centric approach, backed by 24/7 customer service. Our brand voice is professional yet approachable, aiming to demystify the complex world of cybersecurity for our clients, who mainly consist of mid to large-sized enterprises. Our mission is to make the digital world a safer place for businesses. We have been awarded the Cybersecurity Excellence Award for three consecutive years. One of the challenges we often face is convincing potential clients about the ROI of investing in advanced cybersecurity solutions. We operate under strict GDPR compliance.

  

By investing time in crafting this detailed description, you're essentially creating a reusable asset. This context can be plugged into any prompt you use in the future, ensuring that the AI's outputs are consistently aligned with your company's specific needs and characteristics.

  

Step 3: Input the Prompt into the LLM

Copy the customized prompt and paste it into your Large Language Model interface, whether it's ChatGPT, Claude, or another foundational LLM.

  

Step 4: Follow the AI's Guidance

The AI will respond to your prompt by asking you for the details needed to craft the email. Provide the information as requested, and remember that the more detailed you are at this point, the less editing you’ll likely have to do on the generation.

  

Step 5: Collaborate with the AI

Once you've provided the details, the AI will craft an email for you. Review it and provide feedback for refinements as needed.

  

By following these steps, you can effectively use a foundational LLM to assist you in crafting personalized, compelling sales emails, thereby saving you time and increasing your effectiveness. Similar to specific tools that do this, these steps will work more or less consistently across all foundational LLMs.

Let’s pretend that you work in Renewable Energy, and you’ve seen a few articles come out about a recent market trends report that is over 100 pages. Here’s how you could use a foundational model, like ChatGPT 4’s Data Analysis function, to quickly learn and act upon it.

  

Step 1: Research

You're interested in the renewable energy sector and want to understand recent market trends. You start by searching for articles and reports using terms like "renewable energy market trends 2023" or "recent developments in renewable energy." In this case you have a few articles already, as well as the full report.

  

Step 2: Data Collection (optional)

After finding a handful of relevant articles and a comprehensive industry report, you download them. If you have the time, you then create a new document where you paste key statistics, quotes, and insights from these sources relevant to your organization. This document serves as your raw data repository. This is an optional step, but will yield better results in the longer term, since the data you feed the model will be more focused and curated.

  

Step 3: Preliminary Analysis with ChatGPT Data Analyzer

You copy the key points from your raw data repository and paste them into a conversation with ChatGPT, or even drag the entire document into the chat for it to use and analyze. You can ask, "Can you summarize the main trends in the renewable energy market based on this data?" ChatGPT will process the information and provide you with a concise summary, highlighting the key trends and insights. For even more thoughtful insights you can first ask “Reason for me step-by-step what the most important market trends are for [company] based on the following document.” This will result in the LLM outlining a thought process prior to making decisions, which can help you more transparently make recommendations for the LLM to consider when analyzing the data.

  

Step 4: Deep Dive Analysis

Now that you have a general idea, you want to understand the specifics. You ask ChatGPT questions like, "What are the growth prospects for solar energy based on the data?" or "How are government policies affecting wind energy investments?" ChatGPT will offer detailed answers, allowing you to understand the nuances of each trend. 

  

If the data is in spreadsheet format, you can even ask it to run specific analyses, create charts, and manipulate the spreadsheet. Although not always perfect, it is a quick way to come to insights to help develop and consider the most promising initial strategies.

  

Step 5: Learning and Understanding

You've gathered and analyzed a lot of data, but you want to ensure you fully grasp the implications. You might ask ChatGPT, "Can you explain how these trends could impact investment strategies in renewable energy?" ChatGPT will break down the complexities into understandable insights. If you don’t understand something, ask it to provide a metaphor or analogy to help you understand better.

  

Step 6: Final Summary and Action Plan

Finally, you ask ChatGPT, "Based on our analysis, can you provide a final summary and suggest an action plan for investing in the renewable energy sector?" ChatGPT will synthesize all the information and offer actionable recommendations, such as which sub-sectors are ripe for investment and what risks to be aware of.

## Tool or LLM?

### Video 6

As we wrap up this module, let's reflect on the central decision you'll face as an executive leveraging AI: the choice between specialized tools like fireflies.ai and foundational large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT or Claude. 

  

Specialized tools offer a streamlined, user-friendly experience, often with features tailored to specific tasks. They can save time and reduce the learning curve, allowing you to focus on decision-making rather than the intricacies of AI. However, these tools often come with subscription costs and may lack the flexibility to adapt to unique or evolving needs. There is also the risk of software bloat, where you end up trying to leverage too many technologies, so no one ends up using any of them. Consider deeply how each tool will integrate into your workflow in the long term. Lastly, be wary of the tools you do purchase, and consider if it is something that will be eclipsed by a company like Microsoft or Google if they decide to integrate a similar functionality into their existing ecosystem.

  

On the other hand, foundational LLMs offer unparalleled flexibility. With the right prompts and a bit of prompt engineering, you can tailor these models to perform a wide variety of tasks. They can be a more cost-effective solution in the long run, especially if you're willing to invest the time to learn how to use them effectively, and craft a highly specialized prompt library. Yet, this approach requires a deeper understanding of how to interact with the model and may involve a steeper learning curve. It will also take time to build up and test your internal assets. 

  

Your decision may hinge on several factors:

- Budget constraints: Are you willing to invest in specialized tools?
    
- Time availability: Do you have the time to learn and adapt foundational LLMs to your needs?
    
- Specificity of tasks: Are your needs so unique that they require the flexibility of a foundational LLM?
    
- Ethical considerations: Are there ethical guidelines or compliance issues that make one option more viable than the other?
    

  

In the end, the choice is yours, and it's likely that a hybrid approach will serve you best, utilizing specialized tools for certain tasks and foundational LLMs for others.

### Glossary of Terms

Prompt Engineering - The practice of carefully crafting prompts to provide the AI with clear, detailed instructions tailored to the specific task and context. This guides the AI to generate more accurate, relevant, and aligned outputs.

  

Pain Points - The specific inefficiencies, frustrations, or challenges in a workflow that negatively impact productivity and strategic focus. Identifying these pain points is the first step in finding solutions.

  

Idea Generation - Leveraging AI tools to rapidly brainstorm innovative solutions and ideas to address identified pain points and challenges.

  

LLM - Large Language Model, a type of AI system particularly adept at generating human-like text and assisting with tasks like summarization, content creation, and analysis. Examples include GPT-3, Claude, and ChatGPT.

  

Meeting Summaries - Condensing long transcripts and recordings from meetings into concise summaries that capture key discussion points, decisions, and action items. This is a prime use case for AI.

  

Document Drafting - Using AI tools to assist in creating the first draft of communications like memos, emails, reports, and other documents. AI can save time while enhancing quality.

  

Research and Analysis - Tasks like aggregating data, identifying trends, summarizing reports, and analyzing competitive intelligence that AI can accelerate to make decisions more data-driven.

  

Prompt Library - A repository of saved prompts that can be reused and adapted for different tasks. This amplifies the value of prompt engineering over time.
