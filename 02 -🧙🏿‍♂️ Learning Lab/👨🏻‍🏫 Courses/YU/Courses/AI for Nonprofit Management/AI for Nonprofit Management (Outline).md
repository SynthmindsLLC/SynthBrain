### Module 1: Program Development (Logic Models)
**Learning Outcomes:**
1. Understand the basics of nonprofit program development.
2. Learn how to use generative AI to ideate and design new programs.
3. Master the creation of logic models using generative AI to define program outcomes, activities, and resources.

**Necessary Content:**
- Introduction to program development in nonprofit management.
- Overview of generative AI tools and their application in ideation and design.
- Steps for creating a logic model: defining problem statements, objectives, inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact.
- Case studies and examples of successful program development using generative AI.

**Generative AI Use Cases:**
- Ideation: Using AI to brainstorm and refine program ideas based on community needs and organizational goals.
- Design: Developing program structure and activities through AI-generated templates and examples.
- Logic Model Creation: Utilizing AI to articulate and visualize the logical flow from program resources (inputs) to the desired impact (outcomes).

### Module 2: Fundraising
**Learning Outcomes:**
1. Understand the fundamentals of fundraising in the nonprofit sector.
2. Learn how to use generative AI to identify and engage potential donors.
3. Develop skills in creating persuasive fundraising materials using AI.

**Necessary Content:**
- Overview of fundraising strategies and best practices.
- Introduction to generative AI tools relevant to fundraising, like donor segmentation, personalized communication, and content creation.
- Techniques for crafting effective fundraising appeals, campaigns, and donor stewardship programs using AI.

**Generative AI Use Cases:**
- Donor Research: Employing AI to analyze and segment donor data for targeted outreach.
- Campaign Creation: Generating engaging content for fundraising campaigns, including emails, social media posts, and grant proposals.
- Personalization: Using AI to tailor communications and build relationships with donors, enhancing donor engagement and retention.

### Module 3: Stewardship

**Learning Outcomes:**

1. Understand the importance of donor relations and stewardship in nonprofit management.
2. Learn to leverage generative AI in developing personalized stewardship strategies.
3. Gain skills in automating donor communication and feedback processes using AI.

**Necessary Content:**

- Fundamentals of effective donor stewardship and relationship management.
- Using generative AI for donor profiling, personalized outreach, and engagement.
- Techniques for automating thank-you messages, updates, and impact reports to donors.

**Generative AI Use Cases:**

- Personalized Outreach: Crafting tailored communications for different donor segments using AI, to strengthen relationships.
- Feedback Automation: Implementing AI tools to gather and analyze donor feedback, ensuring responsive and adaptive stewardship practices.
- Impact Reporting: Utilizing AI to create detailed and visually appealing reports on the impact of donations, enhancing transparency and donor trust.

### Module 4: Grant Writing/Reporting

**Learning Outcomes:**

1. Understand the process and requirements of grant writing and reporting in the nonprofit sector.
2. Learn to use generative AI to research grant opportunities, and draft and refine grant proposals.
3. Develop skills in creating effective and comprehensive grant reports using AI.

**Necessary Content:**

- Key elements of successful grant writing and the grant lifecycle.
- Overview of generative AI capabilities in researching funding opportunities, generating proposal drafts, and streamlining the reporting process.
- Best practices for data collection, outcome measurement, and reporting to funders.

**Generative AI Use Cases:**

- Opportunity Research: Leveraging AI to identify and analyze potential grant opportunities matching the nonprofit’s mission and needs.
- Proposal Drafting: Using AI to assist in writing clear, compelling, and customized grant proposals.
- Reporting Enhancement: Automating the creation of detailed, data-driven reports showcasing the program's impact and effectiveness.
