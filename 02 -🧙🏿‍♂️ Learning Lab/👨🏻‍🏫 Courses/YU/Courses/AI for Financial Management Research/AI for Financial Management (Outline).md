##### Module 1: AI in Data Analysis and Financial Reporting

#### Learning Objectives:

- Understand Generative AI's Role in Financial Data Analysis: Grasp how Generative AI like ChatGPT can enhance data collection and analysis.
    
- Use AI Tools for Insights and Analytics: Learn practical skills in leveraging Generative AI for generating insights and support the development of future predictions in finance for your company.
    

#### Content and Structure:

- Introduction to Generative AI in Data Analysis:

- Content: A comprehensive overview of the evolution of AI in financial data analysis. Begin with early applications of AI in finance and progress to current state-of-the-art technologies and trends distinguishing between classical AI and Generative AI.

- Hands-on Training with ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis:

- Content: A step-by-step tutorial on using ChatGPT for financial data analysis, including uploading data, interpreting results, and troubleshooting common issues.

- Financial Modeling:

- Content: Exploration of how to use Generative AI for financial modeling, focusing on the different analyses to run, efficiency, accuracy, and predictive analyses to try.
    
- Fictional Case Study: Analyze an  example where generative AI was used to financially model something. Discuss both successes and challenges.

- Creating Reports:

- Content: Instruction on how to use Generative AI tools to generate financial reports, based on their analyses for different audiences emphasizing clarity, conciseness, and relevance.

#### Assessment:

- Task: Create a comprehensive financial report using an AI analytics tool on a provided dataset.

### Module 2: Budgeting and Forecasting with AI (Revised)

#### Learning Objectives:

- Master AI Techniques for Dynamic Budgeting and Forecasting: Acquire skills to apply AI in financial planning with a focus on budgeting and forecasting.
    
- Apply Advanced Data Analysis with ChatGPT+: Use ChatGPT+ and its Python capabilities for data-driven budgeting and forecasting.
    

#### Content and Structure:

- Tutorial on AI in Budgeting:
    

- Content: A concise tutorial outlining the basics of AI in budgeting and forecasting. The tutorial will focus on how AI can automate, enhance, and make these processes more dynamic.
    
- Format: Step-by-step video walkthroughs, focusing on the practical application of concepts in AI budgeting.
    

- Practical Exercise with ChatGPT+ for Budgeting:
    

- Content: Guided exercises where learners use ChatGPT+ to create and analyze budgets. This will include entering budget data, asking ChatGPT+ to interpret and make predictions, and understanding the output.
    
- Support Materials: Step-by-step guides and FAQs to assist learners in navigating ChatGPT+ features and Python code usage.
    

- Fictional Case Study: Creating a Budget with AI:
    

- Content: Develop a fictional case study that simulates a real-world budgeting scenario. Walk learners through the process of using AI tools to create a budget for this scenario.
    
- Format: A series of written exercises and tutorials, demonstrating each step of the budgeting process using AI.
    

- Forecasting Models Using ChatGPT+ and Python:
    

- Content: Instruction on various forecasting models (like linear regression, time series analysis) and how to implement these using the natural language processing and Python capabilities of ChatGPT+.
    
- Format: Written guides and screen-captured video tutorials showing the implementation of these models on example datasets.
    

- Individual Project: Developing a Forecast Model:
    

- Content: An assignment where learners develop a forecast model for the fictional case study. They will use ChatGPT+ to process data, generate forecasts, and interpret results.
    
- Support: Include templates and example codes to assist learners in their project.
    

#### Assessment:

- Task: Development and presentation of a budget and forecast for the fictional financial scenario using AI tools.
    
- Criteria: Evaluation based on the accuracy of the budget and forecast, application of AI techniques, and clarity of explanation in the accompanying report.
    
- Submission Format: Written report with accompanying data files and code (if applicable).

### Module 3: AI in Risk Management

#### Learning Objectives:

- Comprehensive Risk Assessment Using AI: Equip learners with skills to use AI for thorough risk analysis in finance.
    
- Identification and Prediction of Financial Risks: Teach how to effectively identify and predict different financial risks using AI tools.
    

#### Content and Structure:

- Fundamentals of AI in Risk Management:
    

- Content: A detailed exploration of how AI is transforming risk management, covering various types of financial risks such as credit, market, and operational risks.
    
- Format: Written materials and succinct video tutorials explaining core concepts and AI applications in risk management.
    

- Hands-on Sessions with ChatGPT:
    

- Content: Practical exercises using ChatGPT to identify and analyze risks. These exercises will simulate real-world scenarios, encouraging learners to apply AI tools to uncover potential risks.
    
- Support Materials: Step-by-step guides on using ChatGPT for risk analysis, including examples of effective prompts and interpreting responses.
    

- Case Study Analysis:
    

- Content: Analysis of fictional case studies where AI plays a critical role in mitigating financial risks. Each case study will represent different aspects of risk management, such as credit risk, market risk, and operational risk.
    
- Format: Written case studies accompanied by guided questions to help learners apply theoretical knowledge to practical scenarios.
    

- Simulation Exercise:
    

- Content: Interactive simulation exercises where learners conduct risk assessments for fictional financial institutions. These simulations will be designed to mimic the complexities and unpredictabilities of real-world finance.
    
- Format: Interactive, text-based scenarios that learners can navigate to make decisions and see the outcomes of their risk assessment strategies.
    

#### Assessment:

- Task: Analyze and present a risk management strategy for a hypothetical financial institution using AI tools. This assessment will require learners to apply the knowledge and skills gained throughout the module.
    
- Criteria: Evaluation based on the comprehensiveness of the risk assessment, the application of AI tools, and the effectiveness of the proposed risk management strategy.
    
- Submission Format: A detailed report outlining the risk assessment process, findings, and recommendations, supported by AI-generated data and analysis.

### Module 4: AI for Compliance and Regulatory Reporting

#### Learning Objectives:

- Understand AI in Compliance and Reporting: Gain a comprehensive understanding of how AI is applied in compliance and regulatory reporting.
    
- Monitor Transactions with AI: Learn to use AI tools for monitoring transactions and ensuring adherence to financial regulations.
    

#### Content and Structure:

- Introduction to AI in Compliance:
    

- Content: An overview of the role of AI in compliance, focusing on tools, methodologies, and the impact of AI on regulatory practices.
    
- Format: Written materials outlining key concepts, supplemented with case studies illustrating the application of AI in compliance.
    

- AI Compliance Training:
    

- Content: Interactive sessions teaching how to use AI for compliance monitoring. This would include identifying potential compliance issues using AI tools.
    
- Support Materials: Step-by-step guides or interactive tutorials on using specific AI compliance tools.
    

- Implementing AI for Transaction Monitoring:
    

- Content: Workshop-style content on setting up and using AI systems for detecting anomalies in financial transactions. This will include practical examples of how AI can identify unusual patterns or potential compliance breaches.
    
- Format: Written guides and walkthroughs, showing how to implement and use AI monitoring systems.
    

- Individual Project: Compliance Report Development:
    

- Content: Instead of a group activity, learners will develop a compliance report for a simulated financial scenario using AI tools. This project will allow learners to apply their knowledge in a practical setting.
    
- Scenario: Provide a detailed, fictional scenario that includes various transactions and financial activities, and ask learners to identify and report any potential compliance issues using AI tools.
    

#### Assessment:

- Task: Prepare a comprehensive compliance report for the case study organization, utilizing AI tools to identify and report any irregularities.
    
- Criteria: Assessment based on the thoroughness of the compliance report, accuracy in identifying irregularities, and effective use of AI tools.
    
- Submission Format: A detailed written report, possibly including charts or data visualizations, outlining the compliance analysis and findings.
