## Relevant Info
- Windward Heights is a large-scale project exceeding 1 MW 
- GreenTech Solutions subcontracted part of the construction work to a third-party contractor who, unbeknownst to GreenTech, has not been paying the prevailing wage to its workers.
- GreenTech Solutions intended to source steel from a U.S. manufacturer to qualify for this bonus. However, due to supply chain disruptions, the manufacturer could not deliver the materials on time, and the project team sourced steel from an overseas supplier to avoid construction delays.
- To secure the PTC, GreenTech Solutions had to prove that construction on Windward Heights began before the January 1, 2025 deadline. The company relied on the safe harbor provision, having paid for 5% of the project's total cost before the end of 2024. However, due to an accounting error, the actual amount paid was slightly below the required 5%
