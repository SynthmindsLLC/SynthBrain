Are you struggling to keep track of all the interesting content you come across daily? Fear not, as Readwise might just be the solution you've been looking for to build your second brain. In this post, I'll walk you through how to use Readwise to effortlessly import highlights into your Obsidian Vault.

## What is Readwise?

Readwise is a paid service (with a free trial) that simplifies the process of capturing and organizing information from various sources. It's a tool I've found invaluable, and it's worth every penny for the frictionless experience it provides.

## Setting Up Readwise

### Importing Highlights

Readwise allows you to import highlights from a multitude of platforms such as Kindle, Instapaper, Pocket, and more. To start importing:

1. Sign in to Readwise and navigate to the **Import** section.
2. Connect your preferred platforms and add any books or articles you wish to import highlights from.

### Exporting to Obsidian

Readwise can export your highlights to various note-taking apps, including Obsidian. Here's how to set it up:

1. In Readwise, go to the **Export** section.
2. Choose Obsidian and configure the export settings to your liking, such as custom file names and how the highlights will appear in your vault.

## Using the Readwise Highlighter Extension

To capture content from the web:

1. Install the Readwise Highlighter extension from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Highlight text on any webpage and add tags or comments as needed.
3. The highlights will sync to your Obsidian Vault automatically.

## Pricing

While Readwise is a paid service, it offers an education discount for those affiliated with a university. The standard price is around $8 per month, but the value it provides can be well worth the investment.

## Integrating Readwise with Obsidian

To connect Readwise to Obsidian:

1. Open Obsidian and go to **Settings** > **Community Plugins** > **Browse**.
2. Search for and install the **Readwise Official** plugin.
3. Configure the plugin settings, such as the folder path for your highlights and sync frequency.

## Additional Features

### Daily Review

Readwise offers a Daily Review feature that helps you revisit and reinforce your captured knowledge. You can also share quotes in a visually appealing format on social media.

### Readwise Reader App

For mobile users, the Readwise Reader app is a game-changer. It allows you to:

- Save articles for later reading.
- Highlight and tag content within articles.
- Convert YouTube video transcripts into text and highlight as you watch.

## Conclusion

Readwise is an incredibly powerful tool for content capture and integration with Obsidian. Although it requires a subscription, the ease of use and time saved can be invaluable for those looking to build a comprehensive and accessible second brain.
