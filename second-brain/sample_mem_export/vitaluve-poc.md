# VitaLuve — POC Enclosure Decision

## Key Decisions
- Selected the IKEA Milsbo Tall glass cabinet as the proof-of-concept enclosure.

## Reasoning
We chose the Milsbo because it is cheap, widely available, and already a proven
form factor in the houseplant community, so a Kickstarter audience recognizes it
instantly — that trust lowers the perceived risk of a smart conservatory.
