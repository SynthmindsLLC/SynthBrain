# PBTV — MOA v4 (active)

The Memorandum of Agreement governing the PBTV ↔ Phytosynth partnership.

## Key Decisions
- Locked a 70/30 plant consignment split (PBTV / Phytosynth).
- Chose a 60/40 split on 3D-printed garden products.
- Rates: $300/hr design, $150/hr fulfillment.

## Reasoning
We went with 70/30 on plants because Monica carries the storefront risk and
foot-traffic cost at Olde Mistick Village, so the larger share to PBTV reflects
who absorbs the inventory exposure. The 60/40 on printed goods flips toward
Phytosynth because the IP and machine time are ours.
