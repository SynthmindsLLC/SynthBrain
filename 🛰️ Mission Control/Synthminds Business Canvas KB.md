#### History

Synthminds was conceived with a vision to harness the transformative power of AI. Founded by Wes, who now serves as the CEO, the company initially embarked on a journey to build bespoke AI solutions. This phase was marked by innovation and a deep exploration into the potentials of artificial intelligence. Over the past year, as an LLC, Synthminds carved out a niche in developing tailored AI applications, gaining valuable insights and expertise in the field.

In this period of growth and learning, Synthminds has not only honed its technical acumen but also crystallized its core values and purpose. Recognizing the broader impact and potential of AI, the company is in the midst of a pivotal transition. Moving from an LLC to a C Corp, Synthminds is realigning its focus, drawing on its rich experiences in AI solution development.

#### Current Focus

The new chapter for Synthminds is defined by a strategic shift towards AI education and responsible integration. This direction stems from a profound understanding that the future of AI lies not just in creating solutions but in empowering individuals and organizations to understand, integrate, and ethically use AI in their workflows and daily lives.

Under the leadership of Wes, and with the dynamic contributions of Goda, a renowned YouTube influencer, as well as Joseph and Daniel, experienced AI educators, Synthminds is poised to redefine its trajectory. The company now aims to become a beacon in the AI landscape, focusing on educating a diverse audience about AI and helping them integrate these technologies responsibly.

This strategic pivot is more than a business decision; it's a reflection of Synthminds' commitment to its values – collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit. By demystifying AI and making it accessible, Synthminds is not just adapting to the evolving technological landscape but actively shaping it.
