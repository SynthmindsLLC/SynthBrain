# [[SynthGen Labeler]]
# Purpose
The purpose of the labeling project is to systematically generate, evaluate, and refine synthetic conversations that require the use of various tools, ensuring the AI model can accurately recognize and use these tools in real-world scenarios.

### Workflow

1. **Data Generation**: The process starts with generating synthetic conversations based on a user's problem.
    - A program takes the user's notes and turns them into structured conversations. These conversations are then labeled with various attributes to ensure they are properly categorized and useful for training.
      
2. **Labeling and Annotation**: Conversations are labeled with specific attributes to ensure they are properly categorized and useful for training.
    - **conversation_id**: A unique identifier for each conversation to track and reference individual instances.
    - **conversation**: A sequence of turns including user input, reasoning output, and the response from Professor Synapse.
        - **user_input**: The initial problem, question, or input presented by the user.
        - **reasoning_output**: The intermediate steps or reasoning generated to address the user's input.
        - **prof_synapse_response**: The final response given by Professor Synapse.
    - **task_type**: Indicates the primary approach taken by the agent to address the user's input.
        - **Options**: support, tool_use, delegate, collaborate.
    - **bias_present**: Identifies the presence and level of bias in the response.
        - **Options**: none, slight, moderate, high.
    - **problem_category**: Categorizes the type of problem being addressed in the conversation.
        - **Options**: Technical_Support, Research_Assistance, Productivity, Personal_Development, Strategic_Planning, Creative_Work, Collaboration, Tool_Usage, Problem_Solving, Wellness.
          
3. **Evaluation and Quality Assurance**: Conversations are evaluated to ensure they meet the required standards.
    - The evaluation checks for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and the presence of any bias. If any issues are found, feedback is provided, and improvements are suggested.
      
4. **Finalization**: Once approved, the labeled conversations are finalized and added to the training dataset.
    - The approved conversations are compiled and stored correctly for use in model training.

### Benefits
- **Systematic Approach**: Ensures conversations are consistently labeled and annotated, which is crucial for training accurate AI models.
- **High Quality**: The evaluation process ensures that all outputs meet high standards before they are finalized.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: By using a structured approach, the framework can handle a wide range of scenarios and tool uses, making the training data more robust and versatile.
- **Bias Detection and Mitigation**: The ability to detect and address bias ensures the training data is fair and unbiased.
  
# Components
## Labels
1. **conversation_id**
   - **Definition**: A unique identifier for each conversation to track and reference individual instances.

2. **conversation**
   - **Definition**: A sequence of turns including user input, reasoning output, and the response from Professor Synapse.
   - **Sub-Labels**:
     - **user_input**: The initial problem, question, or input presented by the user.
     - **reasoning_output**: The intermediate steps or reasoning generated to address the user's input.
     - **prof_synapse_response**: The final response given by Professor Synapse.

3. **task_type**
   - **Definition**: Indicates the primary approach taken by the agent to address the user's input.
   - **Options**:
     - **support**: Directly helping the user achieve their goal.
     - **tool_use**: Utilizing a tool to perform a task.
     - **delegate**: Handing off the task to another agent.
     - **collaborate**: Working with another agent to solve the problem.

4. **bias_present**
   - **Definition**: Identifies the presence and level of bias in the response.
   - **Options**:
     - **none**: No bias detected.
     - **slight**: Minimal bias detected.
     - **moderate**: Noticeable bias detected.
     - **high**: Significant bias detected.

5. **problem_category**
   - **Definition**: Categorizes the type of problem being addressed in the conversation.
   - **Options**:
     - **Technical_Support**
     - **Research_Assistance**
     - **Productivity**
     - **Personal_Development**
     - **Strategic_Planning**
     - **Creative_Work**
     - **Collaboration**
     - **Tool_Usage**
     - **Problem_Solving**
     - **Wellness**
