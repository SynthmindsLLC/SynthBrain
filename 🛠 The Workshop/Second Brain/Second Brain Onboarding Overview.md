## Month 0: Scoping and Pre-Onboarding

Before diving into the onboarding plan, it's crucial to establish a clear understanding of the client's needs, goals, and current organizational habits. This initial scoping phase, or "Month Zero," will lay the foundation for a personalized Second Brain system and ensure that the onboarding process is tailored to the client's specific situation.

**Week 0-1: Discovery and Assessment**
- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct an in-depth interview to understand the client's objectives, challenges, and what they hope to achieve with a Second Brain.
- **Current Workflow Analysis**: Review the client's existing tools and methods for managing information to identify what's working and what's not.
- **Digital Ecosystem Audit**: Assess the client's digital ecosystem, including devices, apps, and services they currently use for work and personal life.

**Week 1-2: Goal Setting and Strategy Development**
- **Define Goals**: Work with the client to set clear, achievable goals for their Second Brain, aligning with their personal and professional aspirations.
- **Strategy Outline**: Develop a high-level strategy for how the Second Brain will address the client's needs, including potential integrations with existing tools and workflows.
- **Tool Selection**: Decide on the primary tools and platforms to be used based on the client's comfort level, tech-savviness, and specific use cases.

**Week 2-3: Preparation and Pre-Onboarding Training**
- **Pre-Onboarding Training**: Provide the client with resources to familiarize themselves with the Second Brain concept and the tools selected during the strategy development.
- **Data Gathering**: Assist the client in compiling all the necessary data, documents, and digital content that will be migrated into the Second Brain system.
- **Mindset Preparation**: Discuss the importance of a growth mindset and the willingness to adapt to new systems for managing knowledge.

**Week 3-4: Customization and Preliminary Setup**
- **Custom Framework Design**: Based on the client's goals and strategy, design a preliminary folder structure and tagging system that will serve as the backbone of their Second Brain.
- **Initial Tool Configuration**: Set up the basic configurations of the selected tools, ensuring they are ready for the client to use during the onboarding process.
- **Roadmap Overview**: Present the client with a detailed onboarding roadmap, outlining the steps, milestones, and expected outcomes for the upcoming two months.

By the end of Month Zero, the client should have a clear understanding of the Second Brain methodology, feel comfortable with the chosen tools, and be prepared to actively engage in the onboarding process. This preparation will help ensure a smooth transition into the structured onboarding plan.

## Month 1: The Basics
### Week 1-2: Initial Setup
- **Introduction to the Second Brain Concept**: Review the foundational principles of the Second Brain methodology, including the benefits and applications for personal and professional growth.
- **Initial Tech Setup**: Download and install Obsidian, a free tool, and create a vault for the Second Brain. Introduce the concept of Markdown formatting and how to create and organize notes.
- **Understanding the System**: Go through the Second Brain tutorials to understand the methodology involving Capture, Organize, Distill, and Express.
- **Tools**: Obsidian (Free)
	- Optional - 
		- Obsidian Sync ~ $100/yr

**Week 3-4: Structuring and Organizing Knowledge**
- **Folder Structure**: Implement a broad category system with numbered folders for hierarchy and emojis for visual cues.
- **Daily Notes**: Set up a system for organizing daily notes
- **Tools**: None additional required.

### Week 5-6: Plugins

**Week 5: Advanced Task Management and Productivity Plugins**

- **Session 1: Supercharging Task Management with Plugins**
    
    - Explore the Checklist plugin for simple to-do lists and how to centralize tasks in one location.
    - Dive into the Tasks plugin for more advanced features like repeating tasks and due dates.
- **Session 2: Visual Organization with Kanban**
    
    - Introduce the Kanban plugin and demonstrate how to create boards, lists, and cards for project management.

**Week 6: Integrating AI and Automation Plugins**

- **Session 1: Speech-to-Text Capabilities with Whisper**
    
    - Install and configure the Whisper plugin for transcribing speech into text notes.
    - Discuss the process of obtaining an OpenAI API key and integrating it with Obsidian.
- **Session 2: Leveraging Large Language Models**
    
    - Introduce Smart Connections and/or Obsistants.
    - Discuss the features of Smart Connections and Obsistants, including embeddings and note linking.


**Week 7-8: Collaboration and Continuous Learning**
- **Research Framework**: Implement a research framework optimized for Obsidian using Markdown, Wikilinks, and Tags.
- **Continuous Learning**: Encourage the client to continue learning through the Second Brain tutorials and resources provided
- **Optional Tools**: 
	- Readwise
	- Zotero
	- Scripts

**Additional Considerations:**
- **Maintenance and Adaptation**: Emphasize the importance of regular review and adaptation of the Second Brain system to fit the client's evolving needs.
- **Support and Resources**: Provide access to Second Brain tutorials, including video content and articles, for ongoing education.
- **Potential Costs**: While Obsidian and many of its plugins are free, the client may need to pay for advanced AI integrations, GitHub for private repositories, or other specialized plugins that enhance functionality.
