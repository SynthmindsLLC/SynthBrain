Need More Support?
==================

Get in touch with our friendly support team for personalised assistance and guidance

We use [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) to handle all support inquiries. Please head to your Leonardo.Ai [home page](https://app.leonardo.ai/) and contact support by clicking on the Intercom widget button at the bottom right corner of the page.

![](https://files.readme.io/813b39b-Screenshot_2024-02-05_at_3.49.51_pm.png)

In this widget, select 'Ask a question' and you'll be able to send us a new message, file bug reports, feature requests or give us any feedback. For API specific troubleshooting or questions, select the _API Help_ option.

![](https://files.readme.io/f4f9c9f-Screenshot_2024-02-23_at_11.24.39_am.png)

If our Support team is unavailable, or if you're not on Leonardo.Ai when our team replies, we'll send a copy of your conversation to the email address used to log into your Leonardo.Ai account. Feel free to respond using the Intercom widget or reply via email -- whichever method is more convenient for you!

Alternatively, feel free to drop us an email anytime at [support@leonardo.ai](mailto:support@leonardo.ai). Our friendly support teams are all ears and ready to help with any questions, feedback, or concerns you might have.

The more details you share in your message, the better we can assist you and the quicker we can get you up and running again!
