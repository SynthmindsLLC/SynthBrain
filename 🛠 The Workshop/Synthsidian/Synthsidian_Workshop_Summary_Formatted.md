T
his document encapsulates the key activities, insights, and strategic steps derived 
from our recent design thinking workshop. The purpose of this workshop was to 
harness collective creativity and strategic thinking to drive the development of 
Synthsidian, a product poised to become the gold standard in AI interaction. 
Purpose of the Workshop 
Design thinking is a user-centered approach to innovation that draws from the designer's 
toolkit to integrate the needs of people, the possibilities of technology, and the 
requirements for business success. Our workshop brought together a diverse group of 
stakeholders to collaboratively explore and define the pathway for Synthsidian's 
development.

STICKY STEPS METHODOLOGY 
T
he "Sticky Steps" methodology is designed to break down the path to our ultimate 
goal into manageable, actionable steps. By starting with the end goal and working 
backwards, this approach helps in identifying every necessary action required to 
achieve success. This method transforms seemingly unachievable or difficult goals into 
tangible realities by providing a clear, step-by-step plan.

creating a plan to address them eﬀectively.

HYPOTHESIS STATEMENT 
T
he "Hypothesis Statement" methodology is used to transform assumptions into 
testable hypotheses. This approach helps in removing subjectivity and focusing 
the team on gathering feedback from users and customers to validate 
assumptions. 
For initial customers and users, the group observed a need for a private knowledge base 
to support creative eﬀorts among small independent producers. They believed that other 
people and organizations in the creative space also required this level of service but 
didn’t know where to start. The hypothesis was framed as follows: "Because we saw a 
need for a private knowledge base for the purpose of creative eﬀorts among small 
independent producers, we believe there are other people/organizations in the 
creative space that require the same level of service but don’t know where to start." To 
validate this hypothesis, they planned to create a feedback loop within existing 
customers and monitor the number of people who sign up for the waitlist. Additional 
metrics such as the number of tools called, conversations, messages sent, active users, 
stickiness ratio, churn rate, and retention rate were identified as crucial indicators. 
Diverse feedback channels, including interviews and surveys, will be used to gain a 
comprehensive understanding of user needs and track engagement over time. 
Regarding how to reach clients, the hypothesis was based on the interest gained through 
existing media channels like YouTube, LinkedIn, and Discord. The group believed these 
channels might be ideal for reaching potential clients. The hypothesis was stated as: 
"Because we saw interest gained through our existing media channels (YouTube, 
LinkedIn, Discord), we believe these existing channels may be the ideal way to reach 
potential clients." Validation criteria included qualitative feedback from interactions on 
these channels and quantitative feedback, such as the number of downloads, waitlist 
sign-ups, ad conversions, and contact points generated. The scope will be broadened to 
include emerging platforms, and A/B testing with diﬀerent content and outreach 
strategies within each channel will be conducted to identify the most eﬀective approach.

For understanding how the product is used, the group hypothesized that Synthsidian 
would be used for research on projects, brainstorming, and supporting creative 
processes. They believed the product's benefit was in supercharging the creative process

for the waitlist, and additional qualitative and quantitative measures. Direct observation 
sessions or user testing will be included to understand how users interact with the

The monetization strategy hypothesis was built on the assumption that existing 
customers showed interest in paying for setup and continued maintenance. The group 
believed that a multi-tiered approach with team setups and maintenance plans would be 
the most appropriate strategy. The hypothesis was: "Because we saw existing 
customers demonstrate interest in paying for setting up as well as continued

would be the appropriate strategy." To validate this, they planned to gather qualitative 
feedback from potential customers and measure quantitative indicators such as the 
number of maintenance plan purchases, referral clients, and changes in key performance 
indicators (KPIs). A/B testing with diﬀerent pricing models will be conducted to identify 
the most eﬀective strategy, and feedback on perceived value and willingness to pay will 
be gathered to refine the pricing tiers. 
Lastly, the hypothesis for important features centered around the assumption that having 
AI-driven auto features would be highly valuable for users. The group believed that this 
capability would lead to brand recognition, user adoption, loyalty, and referrals among 
small independent producers. The hypothesis was framed as: "Because we saw the need 
for AI to make features that matter for the user, we believe this will achieve brand 
recognition and awareness as thought leaders and innovators, user adoption, loyalty, 
and referrals among small independent producers." Validation would come from 
market feedback, quantitative measures like user adoption rates, and qualitative insights 
from user testimonials and feedback sessions. Usability testing will be integrated to refine 
feature development based on real user interaction data, ensuring that the features are 
not only desired but also eﬀectively used.
