# Agenda
#### **1. Presentation of the Written Agenda and Workflow (10 minutes)**
- **Objective**: Offer a clear roadmap of the day’s activities, cementing a transparent workflow from research ingestion to creative exploration.
- **Activity**: Hand out a written agenda at the beginning, outlining:
  - The workflow for integrating and organizing provided materials.
  - The session’s activities, including structured Q&A times.
  - The pacing and sequence of the day.
#### **2. Incorporation of ANVL Materials (10 minutes)**
- **Objective**: Assure guests of the comprehensive inclusion of their provided materials.
- **Activity**: Present a summary screen/graphic or document detailing all the ANVL materials ingested by the AI, emphasizing the volume and variety (books, papers, transcripts, etc.) to underscore the depth of research considered.

#### **3. Interactive Live Test Using Proposed Materials (30 minutes)**
- **Objective**: Showcase AI's capability to handle live input and generate relevant, insightful outputs through a text based game
- **Activity**: Guests will go on a guided tour through the history and their books, choosing from a variety of characters, demonstrating real-time data handling, analysis, and output generation.

#### **4. AI Art Generation Demos (20 minutes)**
- **Objective**: Visualize the era and its narratives through AI-generated art.
- **Activity**: Using DALLE or similar tools, prepare 5 to 6 prompts based on the day's discussions or directly from the ANVL materials to create art pieces. These visuals serve as a vibrant complement to the textual and analytical data, enriching the storytelling aspect.

#### **5. Printing Demonstrations and Outputs (10 minutes)**
- **Objective**: Offer tangible takeaways from the live test interactions.
- **Activity**: Print out the AI’s responses, analyses, or generated narratives from the live test for guests to review, providing a physical record of the AI’s capabilities and insights drawn from their materials.

### **Preparation Tips**: 
- Prioritize a setup where transitioning between activities and handling questions is seamless.
- Prepare a selection of topics, questions, or scenarios in advance for the live test, ensuring they are relevant and engaging.
- Ensure the technical setup supports both the interactive and demonstration parts of the session smoothly, with a dedicated station for printing outputs.

## Research & Knowledge Organization
- Introducing guests to the expansive realm of historical research via AI begins with the aggregation of books, transcripts, and other relevant documents. 
- This foundational work fuels the AI’s knowledge base, which, once enriched, can be seamlessly organized using a templater bot within Obsidian. 
- The process not only simplifies the discovery of interconnected themes but also highlights the potential of AI in enhancing our understanding of history.

## Knowledge Graph Exploration
- Navigating through the organized data via backlinks and tags reveals a vast knowledge graph that underscores the interconnectedness of historical events, figures, and concepts. 
- This exploration serves as a powerful demonstration of how AI can transform a collection of disjointed data points into a coherent, accessible narrative.

## Creative Role-Playing Introduction
- Transitioning from research to creative exploration, guests are invited to embody characters from the period in question, including figures on either side of the colonial narrative. 
- This role-playing venture, backed by a text-based game format, offers an immersive experience that not only educates but also entertains, providing a unique lens through which to understand complex historical dynamics.

## Waiting Time Activities
- To keep guests engaged while AI processes their interactions within the RPG, ask them to share what they expect the AI to reply with based on their knowledge. 

## Debrief and Reflection
- Concluding the session with a reflective conversation allows participants to share their experiences, insights, and how they envision integrating AI into their future projects. 
- This debrief is essential for understanding AI’s potential in revolutionizing historical narrative construction and storytelling.

## Best Practices, Tips, and Tricks

### Best Practices
- **Start Small**: Begin with basic, easily understandable demonstrations and gradually introduce more complex AI interactions as comfort grows.
- **Interactive Demonstrations**: Ensure that each part of the session is interactive, giving guests the chance to engage directly with the technology.
- **Clear Instructions**: Provide clear, simple instructions and support throughout the sessions to prevent confusion and enhance learning.
- **Relate to their Expertise**: Tailor the content and AI interactions to connect directly to their historical expertise, making it more relevant and engaging.

### Tips
- **Regular Breaks**: Plan short breaks between activities to allow guests to absorb the information and discuss among themselves.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Encourage feedback throughout the session to adapt and tailor the experience to the guests' preferences.
- **Use of Visual Aids**: Employ visuals such as diagrams, screenshots, or real-time visualizations (like those from DALLE/Leonardo for image generation) to aid understanding and add an element of visual interest.

### Tricks
- **Pre-loaded Content**: Have certain queries and their complex answers pre-loaded into the AI system to ensure smooth demonstrations without waiting for processing.
- **Shortcut Commands**: Utilize shortcut commands in Obsidian to streamline navigation and manipulation of the AI tools, making it easier for the guests.
- **Highlight Interconnectivity**: Showcase the interconnectedness of various historical events and figures through AI’s ability to link data, enhancing the depth and narrative of the experience.
