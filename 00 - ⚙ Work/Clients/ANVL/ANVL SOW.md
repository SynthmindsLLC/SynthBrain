**Statement of Work (SoW) Between Synthminds AI, Inc. and ANVL Productions LLC**

**1. Introduction**
This Statement of Work (SoW) is entered into by Synthminds AI, Inc. ("Service Provider") and ANVL Productions LLC ("Client") to outline the services provided, the terms of engagement, and the financial agreement for the period specified.

**2. Term of Engagement**
The term of this SoW commences on April 16th and will continue for a period of 12 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms set forth herein.

**3. Financial Arrangement**
The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider a monthly fee of $3,500, totaling $42,000 for the 12-month period. This represents a reduced rate from the Service Provider's normal fee of $5,000 per month.

**4. Scope of Services**
The Service Provider will provide the following services to the Client:
- Maintenance and ongoing enhancement of the Obsidian Vault.
- Development and integration of AI solutions, specifically Obsistants, into the Client's workflows.
- IT support to troubleshoot and resolve any operational issues.
- Access to a variety of AI educational resources to support the Client's team.

**5. Exclusivity Agreement**
Joseph Rosenbaum, representing the Service Provider, will engage exclusively with the Client in regards to integration services. Mr. Rosenbaum will not undertake additional engagements without express permission from the Client. Other members of the Service Provider's company are not included in this Exclusivity Agreement.

**6. Product Development**
The Service Provider agrees to postpone the open-source release of Obsistants onto Github publicly until August 2024 at the latest to maintain strategic advantage and alignment with the Client's goals.

**7. Termination and Renewal**
This SoW may be terminated by either party with written notice. Upon expiration, this SoW may be renewed or renegotiated based on mutual agreement.

**8. Signature**
This SoW is considered effective when the Client makes their first payment.
