# **Introduction** 
In the digital era, artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping how we live, work, and learn. However, the rapid advancement of technology has widened the digital divide, leaving many behind. Recognizing the urgency to bridge this gap, Synthminds and AI for Education propose a collaborative effort with Verizon to celebrate AI Literacy Day on April 19th. This initiative aims to demystify AI for the younger generation, fostering a future where technology is accessible and empowering for all.

# **Objectives** 
Our nationwide AI Literacy Sprint is designed to ignite curiosity and impart fundamental knowledge of generative AI among students across the United States. Through a compelling, story-driven course, we aim to:

- Enhance AI understanding and literacy.
- Encourage critical thinking about AI's role and impact.
- Stimulate interest in AI careers, particularly among underrepresented communities.

# **Celebrity Involvement** 
Leveraging the star power of Terry Crews and Stevie Wonder, this initiative will transcend traditional educational boundaries. Their stories and messages will be interwoven throughout the course, serving as a beacon of inspiration and engagement. Their involvement will not only draw attention but also highlight the importance of inclusivity in technology.

# **Course Overview** 
The sprint will feature a concise, interactive course suitable for a single class period. It will introduce students to the basics of generative AI, including how chatbots operate, with a focus on storytelling to foster a deeper connection and understanding. This scrappy, initial version will serve as a foundation for a more robust, interactive, and personalized learning experience to be developed post-AI Literacy Day.

# **Short Term**
For AI Literacy Day, we will launch a 45-60 minute GenAI literacy curriculum, complete with a literacy bot, tailored for two age groups: grades 4-7 and 8-12. This curriculum will be teacher-led, hands-on, pedagogically sound, and engaging to ensure a super fun learning experience. Hosted on a web application that respects privacy by not recording student and teacher data, we will measure the initiative's impact through the number of individual users, completion rates for the curriculum, and a short assessment to gauge understanding.

- **Terry Crews** steps into the role of a modern-day Prometheus, an ingenious creator with the warmth and charisma akin to Tony Stark and the determination of Frankenstein. Terry embarks on a visionary project to demystify AI for the young minds, crafting an "AI entity" with the intention of serving humanity. His narrative will guide students through the creation process, emphasizing the importance of ethics, creativity, and inclusivity in technology.
    
- **Stevie Wonder**, embodying the insightful and soulful companion akin to Igor, brings a unique perspective to the narrative. His musical genius and life experiences offer a metaphorical lens through which AI's potential to enhance human creativity and overcome challenges is explored. Stevie's involvement will not only draw attention to the importance of accessibility in technology but also inspire students to think critically about how AI can be used to create a more inclusive world.
    
- **Robert Downey Jr.**, channeling his iconic role with a twist, becomes the AI itself - a creation that evolves, learning from its interactions with the world. This "AI monster", far from being a creature to fear, represents the potential and challenges of AI technology. Through RDJ's portrayal, students will learn about the capabilities of AI, the importance of responsible usage, and the potential impact on society.

The below is an example of how this course could be created for students.
### **Act 1: The Vision (6 minutes)**

- **Video 1 (2 minutes)**: Terry Crews, the visionary inventor, explains to Stevie Wonder, his insightful friend, the concept of building a Large Language Model (LLM) assistant. They discuss how this AI will understand and generate human-like text, aiming to help people in creative and practical ways.
- **Activity (4 minutes)**: Students will participate in activity to show how LLMs work in terms of predicting the next word based on the context that preceded it.

### **Act 2: Bringing AI to Life (8 minutes)**

- **Video 2 (2 minutes)**: Terry and Stevie successfully activate the LLM assistant, with Robert Downey Jr. lending his voice to the AI, expressing its eagerness to learn and help. The AI demonstrates its capabilities by answering questions and creating stories.
- **Activity (6 minutes)**: Students interact with a basic LLM through a guided interface to ask questions and request creative outputs, learning how AI processes inputs to generate responses.

### **Act 3: The Challenge (10 minutes)**

- **Video 3 (3 minutes)**: The AI, now with a persona voiced by RDJ, starts to "hallucinate" - generating incorrect or nonsensical answers. Terry and Stevie face the challenge of addressing these unexpected outcomes, highlighting the limitations and ethical considerations of AI.
- **Activity (7 minutes)**: A problem-solving activity where students are presented with examples of AI "hallucinations" or errors and work in groups to discuss why these happened and propose solutions or safeguards.

### **Act 4: Responsible Use and Resolution (12 minutes)**

- **Video 4 (3 minutes)**: The team implements improvements and teaches the AI about responsible use. They discuss the importance of understanding AI's limitations and using it as a tool to augment human creativity and problem-solving, not replace it.
- **Activity (9 minutes)**: Students brainstorm and present scenarios where AI can be used responsibly and creatively, focusing on augmenting human efforts rather than replacing them. They discuss how to mitigate risks and ensure ethical use.

### **Wrap-Up and Reflection (9 minutes)**

- **Video 5 (2 minutes)**: All three stars reflect on the journey, emphasizing the lessons learned about AI, its potential, and its pitfalls. They encourage students to continue exploring and learning about AI with curiosity and responsibility.
- **Activity (7 minutes)**: A reflection and discussion session where students share their thoughts on the story, what they learned about AI, and how they envision using AI responsibly in the future.

**Medium Term**
Following AI Literacy Day, we plan to extend the curriculum/bot to include an individual student version for ages 13-18, with parental permission. We will refine the curriculum based on feedback and introduce new extension activities. Additional training and support will be provided for teachers and caregivers, along with new accessibility features such as text-to-voice and voice-to-text capabilities.

### **Framework for the Home-Based Curriculum**

1. **Interactive Video Modules**: Develop short, interactive video lessons where students can choose different learning paths. These videos feature the celebrities explaining AI concepts, with built-in decision points that allow students to explore topics that interest them most.
    
2. **Voice-Activated Learning**: Utilize voice recognition technology to enable students to ask questions and receive answers from the AI, voiced by the celebrities. This can be integrated into an educational app or web platform, making learning both accessible and conversational.
    
3. **Personalized Learning Journeys**: Incorporate AI to tailor the learning experience based on the student's progress, interests, and learning style. The system can suggest topics to explore next, activities to deepen understanding, or even adjust the difficulty level of the content.
    
4. **Interactive Activities and Quizzes**: After each video or voice interaction, present students with interactive activities, quizzes, or challenges that reinforce the lesson's content. These can be designed to feel like games, with points, badges, or rewards to motivate and track progress.
    
5. **Community Engagement**: Create a safe, moderated online community where students can share projects, discuss ideas, and ask questions. Incorporating moderated video or text interactions with AI representations of Terry Crews, Stevie Wonder, and RDJ can enhance the sense of community and engagement.
    
6. **Accessibility Features**: Ensure the curriculum is accessible to all students, including those with disabilities. This includes voice-to-text and text-to-voice capabilities, high-contrast visuals for the visually impaired, and simplified interfaces for those with cognitive or learning disabilities.

**Long Term**
We envision developing a comprehensive suite of GenAI literacy learning modules covering topics such as responsible GenAI use in schoolwork, understanding large language models (LLMs), prompt engineering, digital citizenship in conjunction with GenAI, and using GenAI to augment learning. We will also establish an enterprise infrastructure for schools and systems to sign up for the curriculum and track progress, ensuring compliance with COPPA and FERPA. Customization options will be available for schools and districts to align with their GenAI literacy and academic integrity plans. Ongoing training will be released for teachers and caregivers to support the fidelity of implementation.

We would develop a comprehensive set of learning modules for GenAI literacy like:

- How do LLMs work
- How to Prompt Engineer

- Using GenAI to augment my learning
