# Tools

1. **Thinkific:** Our Learning Management System (LMS) of choice. Thinkific is where our courses live, breathe, and are accessed by students. It serves as the main platform for course delivery, student engagement, and the tracking of progress.

2. **GPT Trainer:** A cutting-edge tool for creating dynamic, responsive bots within our courses. These bots serve to enhance the learning experience, providing interactive support and personalized learning paths for our students.

3. **Obsidian:** Used extensively by the course creation team, particularly Joseph Rosenbaum and Tomer Zahir. Obsidian aids in the research and development phase, allowing for effective organization of ideas and course materials before transforming them into comprehensive course content.

4. **Articulate:** Our go-to software for instructional design, Articulate empowers us to craft engaging and visually appealing course content. Combined with Reach 360, it serves both as a creative platform and a simplified LMS for specific purposes.

5. **Reach 360:** While Thinkific houses our primary course offerings, Reach 360 offers a more streamlined, aesthetically pleasing environment for demo purposes and B2B client use, where extensive LMS features are not necessary.

6. **ChatGPT:** Beyond individual use, we are on the brink of integrating an organizational API for enhanced capabilities. This move will further augment our ability to leverage AI in our operations.

7. **HubSpot:** The nerve center for our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and the backbone of our website. HubSpot enables us to maintain a pulse on our audience engagement, streamline communications, and manage our course catalog effectively.

8. **Microsoft Teams:** The main channel for internal communication within Yeshiva, Teams enables swift, seamless interactions across departments. It's critical for facilitating dynamic discussions and swift decision-making.

9. **Zapier:** Our newest addition, offering the ability to automate workflows between Thinkific and HubSpot. This tool simplifies the data transfer process, ensuring that our student information and interaction records are consistently up-to-date.

# Tech Support
At YU Global, while our operational procedures are ever-evolving to meet the dynamic needs of our community, our approach to troubleshooting maintains a core principle of agility. Initially, issues may arise in various ways, prominently through direct instructor contact or support queries sent to our designated email — a contact point Jordan will be closely linked with.

**Getting Started:**
- **Point of Contact:** The primary method of issue reporting is via our coursework platform, Thinkific, supplemented by direct instructor feedback. Information regarding the support email will be prominently available in all courses.
- **Initial Setup:** A 'book a time' feature via Microsoft Outlook will be established for Jordan, simplifying the scheduling process for those requiring assistance.

**Process Flow:**
1. **Receipt of Query:** Upon receiving a query, the first step is an attempt to resolve it through email communication. This approach allows for the efficient handling of straightforward issues without needing synchronous interaction.
2. **Detailed Troubleshooting:** For more complex issues that can't be resolved via email, Jordan will schedule a session to diagnose and work through the problem alongside the individual requiring support.
3. **Escalating the Problem:** If still struggling, Jordan will escalate to Joe.
4. **Documentation:** After the problem is resolved, create a brief description of the problem, and how it was resolved.
   
**Primary Tools for Support:**
- **ChatGPT:** An invaluable resource for instant guidance and potential solutions to Thinkific-related queries. Jordan is encouraged to leverage ChatGPT for swift problem resolution.
- **[Thinkific Documentation](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030352674-Troubleshooting-Guide-for-Student-Issues):** Essential reading material that provides a comprehensive understanding of the LMS. It is advisable to familiarize oneself thoroughly with this documentation.
