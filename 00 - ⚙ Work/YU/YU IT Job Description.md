**Position Summary:**  
This role is crucial for maintaining and advancing the technological infrastructure of YU Global's asynchronous and self-paced certification programs. The ideal candidate will not only manage and optimize our learning management systems (LMS), but also support the creation and development of innovative course content. The candidate will curate engaging course descriptions and visuals, oversee community engagement through platforms like Slack, and provide IT-related support to our adult learners. Additionally, this position involves collaborating closely with the Director of Education at YU to ensure that the technological and educational aspects of the programs align with the organization's strategic goals. This role requires a unique combination of technical skills, creativity, and excellent communication abilities to enhance the learning experience and support our global community of learners.

**Position Responsibilities:**  
- Manage and administer the LMS platforms, ensuring a seamless learning experience.
- Assist in the creation and development of course content, working in tandem with instructional designers and subject matter experts.
- Develop and update course descriptions and cards, aligning with program objectives and learner needs.
- Oversee and moderate community channels (e.g., Slack) to foster engagement and support among learners.
- Serve as the first point of contact for IT-related queries from learners and facilitators, providing timely and efficient helpdesk support.
- Collaborate with instructional designers, faculty, and the Director of Education to implement technical enhancements, content development, and integrations within the LMS.
- Monitor and report on the usage and performance of the learning platforms, recommending improvements as needed.
- Work under the supervision of the Director of Education to align technology initiatives with educational strategies and goals.

**Experience & Educational Background:**  
- At least two years of experience in Computer Science, Educational Technology, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience in managing LMS platforms.
- Experience in managing community platforms, providing IT support, and assisting in the development of course content.

**Skills & Competencies:**  
- Strong technical proficiency with LMS platforms, IT support tools, and content creation software.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to interact effectively with a diverse group of learners and colleagues.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team, managing multiple priorities with attention to detail.
- Creative problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to addressing challenges and facilitating learning.
- Familiarity with adult learning principles, online education trends, and content development processes is preferred.
