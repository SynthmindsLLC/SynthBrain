## Early Life and Education
- **Birth and Upbringing:** Marc Lowell Andreessen was born on July 9, 1971, in Cedar Falls, Iowa, and raised in New Lisbon, Wisconsin.
- **Family Background:** His parents, Patricia and Lowell Andreessen, worked for a seed company.
- **Education:** Andreessen earned a bachelor's degree in computer science in December 1993 from the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign (UIUC).
- **Early Career Steps:** He interned twice at IBM in Austin, Texas, and worked at the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) at UIUC.

## Career Milestones
- **Mosaic Web Browser:** Co-author of Mosaic, the first widely used web browser with a graphical user interface, developed while at NCSA.
- **Netscape Communications:** Co-founded Netscape, known for its browser Netscape Navigator. Netscape's IPO in 1995 significantly raised Andreessen's profile.
- **Opsware:** After AOL acquired Netscape, Andreessen co-founded Opsware (originally Loudcloud), which was later sold to Hewlett-Packard for $1.6 billion.
- **Andreessen Horowitz:** In 2009, co-founded the venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz, investing in companies like Facebook, Twitter, and Skype.

## Contributions and Recognition
- **Influence in Tech and Investment:** Andreessen has been a significant figure in technology and investment, ranked highly in various lists of influential people in tech.
- **Philanthropy:** Pledged to donate half of his lifetime venture capital income to charitable organizations.
- **Recent Endeavors:** Published a "Techno-Optimist Manifesto" in 2023, advocating the importance of technology in civilization's progress.

## Personal Investments and Interests
- **Ning and Personal Investments:** Co-founded Ning and has invested in LinkedIn and other companies.
- **Advisor Roles:** Advisor to companies like Asana and a proponent of Bitcoin.
- **Futuristic Projects:** Involved in Neom, Saudi Arabia's plan to build a futuristic megacity.

## Personal Life
- **Marriage:** Married Laura Arrillaga, founder of the Silicon Valley Social Venture Fund, in 2006.
- **Political Views:** Supported different political candidates across the spectrum in various elections.
- **Real Estate:** Made headlines for purchasing a property in Malibu for $177 million in 2021, the highest price for a California property at that time.

Marc Andreessen's journey from a young software engineer to a prominent figure in technology and venture capitalism exemplifies the impact of innovation and entrepreneurship in shaping the digital world. His contributions to web browsing technology and his strategic investments in numerous tech startups have made him a key figure in the development of the internet and the tech industry.
