## Liv Boeree: A Diverse Journey from Poker to Science and Philanthropy

### Introduction
[[Liv Boeree]]'s career spans across professional [[poker]], science communication, and philanthropy, making her a unique and influential figure in multiple fields.

### Early Life and Education
Born in [[Kent]], England, Boeree's early life was marked by a keen interest in science, earning a degree in [[Physics]] with [[Astrophysics]] from the [[University of Manchester]], alongside pursuits in music and modeling.

### Poker Career
Boeree's poker journey began with the show "Ultimatepoker.com Showdown," leading to significant accomplishments including winning the [[European Poker Tour]] and the [[World Series of Poker]]. Her skills earned her the title of European Female Player of the Year thrice, and she was a prominent member of [[Team PokerStars Pro]].

### Science Communication and Public Speaking
Post-poker, Boeree focused on science communication, creating popular YouTube videos and engaging in public speaking. Her appearances include TEDx talks and discussions on various scientific and philosophical topics at prestigious forums.

### Philanthropic Efforts
Boeree co-founded [[Raising for Effective Giving]], advocating for effective altruism. Through her efforts, she has significantly contributed to charitable causes, demonstrating a commitment to using her platform for social good.

### Critique
Boeree's diverse career showcases her ability to excel and make impactful contributions in different realms, from the competitive world of poker to the fields of science communication and philanthropy.
