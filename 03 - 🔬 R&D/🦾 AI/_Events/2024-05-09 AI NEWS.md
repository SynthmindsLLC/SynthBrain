## Potential AI Model Collapse Debated
- A group of 14 academics argue that AI model collapse, the degradation of quality from models recursively training on their own output, is not inevitable if synthetic data is accumulated alongside real data rather than replacing it entirely.[1] 
- However, authors of another paper titled "Model Collapse Demystified: The Case of Regression" disagree that synthetic data can be added to model training without consequence.[1]

## Turbulence in AI Stocks 
- AI stocks, which have been driving the market rally over the past year, are experiencing a reality check this earnings season, with several prominent companies gapping down after reporting quarterly results.[2]
- The market appears to be reassessing lofty expectations and repricing these high-flying stocks based on actual performance and future guidance rather than just hype.[2]

## OpenAI to Announce Google Search Competitor
- OpenAI plans to announce its AI-powered search product on Monday, May 13, raising the stakes in its competition with Google, according to sources.[3] 
- The search product is an extension of ChatGPT that enables it to pull direct information from the web and include citations.[3]
- The announcement timing comes a day before Google's annual I/O conference, where the tech giant is expected to unveil its own AI-related products.[3]

## AI Enhancing Air Travel and Airports
- AI is helping the aviation industry operate more efficiently to handle increasing passenger demand without the need for immediate physical infrastructure expansion.[4]
- Applications include AI-driven scheduling software for baggage handling, predictive maintenance, optimizing gate turnaround times, real-time digital twin models for route planning, and more.[4]
- Rather than automating jobs, AI is empowering airport and airline workers to be more effective and resilient.[4]

## Other AI News
- A New York Times columnist shares his experience cultivating AI companions over the past month, suggesting AI's true impact may lie in offering empathy, advice and emotional support rather than just groundbreaking utilitarian achievements.[5]
- Reddit has issued a public content policy requiring anyone looking to use its public data, likely for AI training, to make a deal with the company first.[7]
- AI and online gaming are highlighted among the top cyber threats to kids this summer.[8]
