## Anthropic Introduces Customizable AI Bots
Anthropic released a new "tool use" feature for its Claude AI chatbot, allowing users to create custom AI assistants for tasks like email, shopping, recommendations and more. These bots can integrate with APIs to analyze data and images. Beta tests show promise for applications like personalized AI tutors.

## Opera Integrates Google's Gemini AI 
Opera added Google's high-performance Gemini AI models into its Aria AI browser extension. This enables enhanced conversational abilities, text-to-speech, and AI image generation across all Opera browsers. It highlights the growing trend of advanced AI in everyday tools.

## New York Passes Law on AI in Elections
To combat potential AI deception, New York enacted legislation regulating the use of AI in political communications. This was prompted by a recent incident involving a convincing but fake AI-generated audio clip of a party chairman. The law aims to set boundaries for AI in campaigns.

## OpenAI Launches GPT-4o in Unfinished State
OpenAI unveiled its latest ChatGPT model, GPT-4o, without some of its promised features like a human-like voice and real-time camera analysis. Reviews found the chatbot improved in some areas like photo analysis and translation, but was not a major leap over the previous GPT-4.

## Opting Out of AI Training Data
Users' personal chats and data help train AI models for companies like OpenAI and Google. However, there are now options for people to opt out of having their data used this way, giving them more privacy and control. The choice involves a tradeoff between privacy and AI progress.
