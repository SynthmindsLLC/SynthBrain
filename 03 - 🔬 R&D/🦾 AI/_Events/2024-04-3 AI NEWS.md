## AI in Healthcare

- **Pharma Pulse 4/3/24**: AstraZeneca has capped the cost of its inhaled respiratory portfolio at $35 for eligible asthma and COPD patients, which is expected to reduce racial disparities in asthma treatment. The DIA 2024 Global Annual Meeting in San Diego will discuss the ethical use of AI in healthcare. Additionally, medications like Ozempic may improve a woman's chances of conceiving if she has insulin resistance or obesity[1].
- **Doctors vs. AI**: A study led by Dr. Andrei Brateanu of the Cleveland Clinic Foundation found that human physicians outperform AI in providing accurate medical advice. AI tools like ChatGPT-4 and Google Bard were tested against doctors, with Bard performing slightly better than ChatGPT-4. However, both AI programs had relatively low levels of accuracy, emphasizing that AI should complement, not replace, medical professionals[2].
- **FDA Approves AI-Driven Test for Sepsis**: Prenosis has developed an AI tool that predicts patients' risk of developing sepsis. The FDA-approved diagnostic tool classifies a patient’s risk of sepsis into four categories, from low to very high, based on 22 parameters including blood-based measures and vital signs[5].

## AI in Marketing

- **OM Strategic Forecasting**: The company has unveiled digital marketing services for the AI era, focusing on strategic forecasting and AI-driven solutions to enhance operational models. It aims to bridge the gap between business planning and marketing investments, offering services like OM Digital Strategy Suite and OM Marketing Strategy Excellence[3].

## AI in Investment

- **VC Investment Dropped**: Despite the excitement over AI, venture capital investment in the U.S. has dropped by 30% in the first quarter of 2024. This indicates a slow start for startup funding in the country[4].
