The world of artificial intelligence continues to rapidly evolve, with major developments and trends emerging across industries. Here are some of the top AI news stories from May 25, 2024:

- **Nvidia's Blockbuster Earnings Fueled by AI Demand**: Nvidia reported record earnings, with data center revenue jumping 427% driven by strong demand for its GPUs from cloud providers for AI workloads. However, this is straining data center infrastructure and energy consumption[1][2].

- **AI Predicted to Displace 50% of Jobs by 2027**: Top China VC Kai-Fu Lee says his 2017 prediction that AI will displace half of all jobs, especially white-collar ones, by 2027 is proving to be "uncannily accurate" with the rise of generative AI. He advises developing human skills AI can't replicate[3].

- **Billionaires Buying AI Stock**: Major hedge funds like Bridgewater, Tiger Global and Soros have been loading up on shares of Alphabet, seeing it as a major AI player through efforts like Google Cloud AI and search summarization, even as the stock climbs[4].

- **AI Unlocking Secrets of Plants**: New AI techniques are providing an unprecedented detailed view into plant biology and interactions with the environment. This could help develop more resilient crops and agricultural practices to adapt to climate change[5].

- **Elon Musk Planning AI Supercomputer**: Elon Musk told investors his AI startup xAI is planning to build its own supercomputer to train next-generation models, following the compute-intensive approach of OpenAI and Anthropic[8].

The acceleration of AI progress shows no signs of slowing, with transformative impacts and opportunities emerging across sectors from enterprise tech to agriculture. But challenges around infrastructure, energy use, and job displacement are growing as well.
