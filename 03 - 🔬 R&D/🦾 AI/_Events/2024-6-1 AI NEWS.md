Here are the key AI news stories from June 1-2, 2024:

## Apple's WWDC 2024 Event

Apple has sent out invitations for its annual Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC) on June 10th. The colorful circular invite hints at potential AI-related announcements, with rumors suggesting iOS 18 will include enhanced AI capabilities like:

- More conversational Siri with improved language understanding
- On-device AI for automatic transcription of Voice Memos
- AI photo editing and enhancements
- AI improvements to Safari browser

However, some AI features may be limited to iPhone 15 Pro and higher-end models. Apple is expected to unveil the next versions of its operating systems (iOS, iPadOS, macOS, etc.) and potentially make a bigger move into AI and generative AI tools. [1]

## Nvidia Announces Next-Gen AI Platform "Rubin" for 2026 

At Computex 2024 in Taiwan, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang announced the company's roadmap for future AI hardware:

- Blackwell Ultra chip coming in 2025
- Next-generation "Rubin" AI platform arriving in 2026, using HBM4 high-bandwidth memory

Huang emphasized Nvidia's accelerated computing capabilities to handle the rapidly growing computational demands of AI, claiming 98% cost savings and 97% less energy usage compared to traditional methods. He positioned Nvidia as critical for companies across industries to embrace AI capabilities. [2]

Other chip giants like AMD, Qualcomm, Intel, and Arm also outlined their AI hardware plans at Computex, underscoring Taiwan's central role in supplying advanced semiconductors for AI applications. [3]

## AI Adoption Lagging Despite Hype

Despite the AI buzz, a recent study revealed that most people are only using AI tools like ChatGPT sparingly, with a small percentage being daily active users. A significant number hadn't even heard of ChatGPT, highlighting the gap between the AI hype and actual adoption rates among consumers and businesses. [1]
