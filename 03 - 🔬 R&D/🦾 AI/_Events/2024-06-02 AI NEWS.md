Here are the key updates on AI news from June 2, 2024:

## Democratic National Committee's Proposed AI Guidelines Fail

The Democratic National Committee (DNC) proposed draft guidelines for the ethical and responsible use of AI in political campaigns, but the proposal faced resistance from Democratic campaign committees. The guidelines advocated for verifying AI outputs, combating biases, and not using AI to spread misinformation. However, concerns were raised about potential restrictions on using AI and displeasing donors with AI industry ties. The failure highlights divisions within the party on leveraging AI for campaigns. [1]

## Nvidia Unveils New AI Chips Rubin and Blackwell 

At Computex 2024, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang announced two new AI chip architectures - Rubin and Blackwell. Rubin will incorporate new GPUs and a "Vera" central processor, following just months after unveiling Blackwell. The rapid release cycle underscores Nvidia's accelerated innovation amid intense competition from AMD, Intel, and tech giants like Microsoft and Google in the AI chip market. [4]

## Nvidia Showcases Broad AI Innovations at Computex

Beyond new chips, Nvidia unveiled a range of AI innovations at Computex 2024:

- New GeForce RTX AI laptops with advanced AI hardware and "Copilot" AI features [5]
- RTX AI Toolkit to help developers integrate AI capabilities into Windows apps [5]  
- Spectrum-X Ethernet network optimized for robust GPU-to-GPU connectivity in AI factories [5]
- Nvidia NIM microservices to efficiently deploy and run large AI models for inference [5]
- Omniverse platform integration with industrial technologies for AI simulation and digital twins [5]

## Google Temporarily Halts AI Search Overviews 

Google has temporarily disabled its new AI-powered "AI Overviews" search feature, which provides summary overviews for some queries, due to errors. The company is working to minimize inaccuracies before relaunching the feature. [6]

The AI news highlights the rapid advancements and growing pains in deploying AI responsibly across various sectors, from politics to consumer tech and enterprise solutions. Key players like Nvidia, Google, and the DNC are grappling with AI's opportunities and challenges.
