This week's AI news roundup covers a range of topics, from new AI-powered features and investments to concerns about regulation and the impact of AI on jobs and innovation.

- **Truecaller and Microsoft partner to enable AI-generated voices for answering calls**, allowing users to create personalized AI versions of their voices. This showcases the potential for AI to enhance customer interactions and experiences. [[AI Update, May 24, 2024: AI News and Views From the Past Week]][1]

- **Google introduces ads in AI Overviews in Search**, presenting a new advertising opportunity for marketers. However, users are finding ways to avoid these AI Overviews, which sometimes present incorrect or copied content. [[AI Update, May 24, 2024: AI News and Views From the Past Week]][1]

- **Google invests 1 billion euros in its Finnish data center to support AI business growth in Europe**, leveraging renewable energy resources and aligning with sustainability goals. This underscores the growing demand for AI capabilities and the importance of sustainable practices. [[AI Update, May 24, 2024: AI News and Views From the Past Week]][1]

- **AI analysis of plant biology papers reveals research priorities and trends across countries.** The study found that the evolution of plant science is often driven by maturing technologies and cost reductions, with China overtaking the U.S. as the top publisher in the field. [[AI uncovers how plant science evolved]][2]

- **Elon Musk predicts jobs will become optional in the future thanks to AI**, envisioning a "benign scenario" where AI investment yields universal high income and an abundance of goods and services. However, he also acknowledges the potential risks and the need for governance and regulation. [[Tesla CEO Elon Musk predicts jobs will become hobbies thanks to AI]][3]

- **A Commerce Department-backed deal with Emirati AI giant G42 raises concerns in Congress** about the potential transfer of cutting-edge AI technology to a country with close ties to China. Lawmakers are scrutinizing the deal and its implications for U.S. foreign policy and AI dominance. [[Commerce-backed deal with Emirati AI giant sets off alarm bells in Congress]][4]

- **Big Tech executives express concerns about Europe's new AI law**, warning that it could harm innovation. Meta's AI chief and Amazon's CTO caution against overregulation and emphasize the need to balance innovation with risk management in different sectors. [[Big Tech execs say Europe's new AI law could harm innovation]][5]

- **Artist and musician Holly Herndon explores the potential for collaboration between artists and AI** to create generative, thought-provoking, and transcendent masterpieces. Herndon and her team are working on solutions to enable artists to "consent" to their work being used as training data for AI models. [[Opinion | 'Artificial Intelligence'? No, Collective Intelligence.]][6]
