Here's a summary of key AI news and developments from July 18, 2024:

1. Google and AI in Olympics Broadcasting:
Google announced it will integrate AI into the U.S. broadcast of the 2024 Paris Olympics. Sports commentators will use Google's AI to explain competitions and provide insights. The company will be named the official search AI partner of Team USA [1].

2. Meta Suspends AI Development in EU and Brazil:
Meta has suspended AI development efforts in the European Union and Brazil due to regulatory scrutiny over data usage concerns. This impacts Meta's multimodal AI models and generative AI tools in these regions [8][14].

3. AI Drone Development in Ukraine:
Ukrainian startups are developing AI systems to enhance drone capabilities for warfare. This includes visual systems for target identification, terrain mapping, and software enabling drone swarms to operate in interconnected networks [10].

4. UK Government's Approach to AI Regulation:
The new UK Labour government outlined plans to focus on regulating only the largest and most powerful AI models used for generative AI, taking a more cautious approach to AI legislation compared to expectations [12].

5. KraneShares Launches AI-Focused ETF:
Investment firm KraneShares launched AGIX, an Artificial Intelligence & Technology ETF providing investors exposure to companies across the AI ecosystem [13].

6. AI in Marketing and Advertising:
A report suggests 80% of consumers expect a future where marketers will target AI shopping assistants rather than humans directly. This could potentially transform the advertising industry [15].

7. New AI-Powered Marketing Tools:
Several companies released new AI-powered marketing tools, including SugarCRM's customer intelligence features, Sinch's customer engagement platform, and Crixeo's content generation platform [15].

These developments highlight the growing integration of AI across various sectors, from sports broadcasting to military applications, as well as the evolving regulatory landscape surrounding AI technologies.
