Several significant AI news stories broke on June 20, 2024:

## OpenAI Co-Founder Ilya Sutskever Launches New AI Safety Startup 
- Ilya Sutskever, former OpenAI chief scientist, announced the launch of Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI), a new company solely focused on developing safe and powerful AI systems.[4][7][14][18]
- SSI aims to pursue "safe superintelligence" through revolutionary breakthroughs by a small team, insulated from commercial pressures and product cycles.[4][7] 
- Sutskever previously led OpenAI's efforts on AI safety and AGI development. His departure followed disagreements with OpenAI's leadership on prioritizing safety.[7][14]

## Tech Giants Unveil New Enterprise AI Solutions
- Aetina and Tupl partnered to enhance AI vision solutions for factories, combining Aetina's NVIDIA Jetson modules with Tupl's AI engine.[6]
- Advantech launched the AIR-520 edge AI server optimized for deploying generative AI solutions in enterprises.[6]
- Lenovo introduced the ThinkSystem V4 portfolio powered by Intel Xeon processors to make AI accessible for businesses of all sizes.[6]

## Anthropic Claims New AI Model Outperforms OpenAI's GPT-4
- Anthropic released Claude 3.5 Sonnet, an upgraded version of its AI model, claiming it surpasses OpenAI's GPT-4 on industry benchmarks.[12]
- The rapid pace of new model releases by Anthropic, OpenAI and others highlights the fierce competition in the AI industry.[12]

## Concerns Grow Over AI's Impact on Jobs and Energy Usage
- A survey found 46% of large U.S. firms plan to implement AI within the next year to automate tasks previously done by humans.[10] 
- The tech industry's reliance on power-hungry AI is straining electrical grids, leading to increased fossil fuel usage despite companies' clean energy commitments.[15]

## Other AI News
- BYU professor and partners created the first enterprise risk framework to help organizations safely adopt generative AI.[9]
- Microsoft announced Windows 10 support will end in October 2025, urging businesses to upgrade to Windows 11 to leverage new AI capabilities.[11]
- Accenture forecasts strong 2024 revenue growth, bolstered by surging demand for AI services.[16]

The rapid advancements in AI are driving both excitement and concern as the technology becomes increasingly integrated into business and society. As AI's capabilities grow, questions around safety, ethics, and governance will only become more pressing.
