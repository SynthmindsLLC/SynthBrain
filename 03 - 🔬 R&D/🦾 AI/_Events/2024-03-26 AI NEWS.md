Recent developments in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) have been making headlines, showcasing the technology's expanding influence across various sectors. Here's a roundup of the key AI news from March 26, 2024:

- **Intel, Qualcomm, and Google Form Alliance Against NVIDIA**: Intel, Qualcomm, Google, and over a hundred startups are reportedly forming an alliance to challenge NVIDIA's dominance in the AI sector[8].

- **AI's Role in Legal Sector Transformation**: Holland & Knight's CEO, Robert Grammig, discussed on a podcast how AI is expected to bring a 'sea change' to the legal industry, highlighting the transformative potential of AI technologies[7].

- **Tether Expands AI Focus**: Tether Operations Limited, a leading company in the cryptocurrency industry, announced a strategic expansion of its AI focus, welcoming top talent to fuel innovation[6].

- **CDC Applies AI to Public Health**: The CDC's data office is leveraging AI to advance public health goals, demonstrating the federal agency's commitment to IT modernization and the potential benefits of AI in public health initiatives[5].

- **EU Regulation on AI Deepfakes**: The European Commission has requested major social media platforms like Facebook and TikTok to identify and label AI-generated deepfakes, especially in the context of political advertising, to safeguard the integrity of upcoming European elections. This move follows the approval of the landmark AI legislation, the Digital Markets Act[4].

- **AI in Cybersecurity**: Reinforcement learning is highlighted as a promising path for AI integration into cybersecurity, offering a method that mimics human learning through experience. This approach is seen as crucial for addressing the volume of data and alerts that cybersecurity professionals must manage[3].

- **AI's Impact on Tech Stocks**: A CNBC video discussed how AI continues to serve as a significant tailwind for certain tech stocks, indicating the growing importance of AI technologies in the financial performance and valuation of tech companies[2].

- **WHO on AI in Pharmaceutical Development**: The World Health Organization (WHO) acknowledged the potential benefits and risks of using AI in pharmaceutical development and delivery. A discussion paper has been developed to examine AI's application in the development and deployment of medicines and vaccines[1].

These developments underscore the diverse applications of AI across industries, from tech alliances and legal transformations to public health and cybersecurity. Additionally, regulatory efforts, particularly in the EU, aim to address the challenges posed by AI, including the management of deepfakes and the integrity of electoral processes.
