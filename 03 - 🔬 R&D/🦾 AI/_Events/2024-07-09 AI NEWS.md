## Major Funding and Investment News

- AI startup Hebbia raised $130 million in funding to help firms efficiently parse and interpret complex data[2].
- AI startup funding more than doubled in Q2 2024, reaching $24 billion according to Crunchbase data[4].
- Five out of six billion-dollar funding rounds in Q2 went to AI firms, including Elon Musk's xAI raising $6 billion[4].

## AI Adoption and Development

- China is leading the world in adoption of generative AI, with 83% of surveyed Chinese respondents reporting use of GenAI tools compared to a 54% global average[5].
- Google's DeepMind introduced a new AI training method called JEST, which is 13 times faster and 10 times more power-efficient than previous methods[2].
- Sam Altman and Arianna Huffington launched a new AI-powered health app providing personalized health insights[2].

## AI Regulation and Governance 

- The U.S. Justice Department held an interagency convening on advancing equity in artificial intelligence[8].
- French antitrust regulators are preparing to charge Nvidia with anti-competitive practices as part of EU efforts to regulate the AI industry[3].

## AI in Defense and Security

- The U.S. and allies announced taking down a Russian "bot farm" powered by AI that was spreading propaganda on social media[7].
- Palmer Luckey's company Anduril is developing AI-powered weapons like drones being used in Ukraine[10].

## Corporate AI Developments

- Microsoft and Apple withdrew from observer positions on OpenAI's board amid increased regulatory scrutiny of Big Tech's influence over AI startups[11].
- Amazon, Tesla, and Microsoft are making significant investments in AI-powered robotics[2].

This roundup highlights the continued rapid growth in AI funding and adoption, alongside increasing regulatory attention and use of AI in sensitive domains like defense. The developments underscore AI's expanding impact across industries and geopolitics in 2024.
