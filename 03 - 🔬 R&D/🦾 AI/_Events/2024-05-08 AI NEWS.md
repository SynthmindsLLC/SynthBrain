Here are the top AI news stories from May 8, 2024:

- **Microsoft Announces $3.3B AI Investment in Wisconsin:** Microsoft is investing $3.3 billion to build an AI data hub in Wisconsin, which aims to train workers on using AI and help manufacturers integrate the technology. The project will create thousands of jobs. President Biden visited the site to contrast it with a failed $10B Foxconn investment previously backed by Trump.[1][5]

- **Microsoft/LinkedIn 2024 Work Trend Index on AI:** A new report surveyed 31,000 people globally and found 75% of knowledge workers now use AI at work. However, many fear AI could replace their jobs. The demand for AI skills is surging, but companies are lagging in providing AI training to employees.[2][3]

- **Google Unveils AlphaFold3 AI for Predicting Human Molecule Behavior:** Google's DeepMind introduced AlphaFold3, an AI system that can predict the 3D structures and interactions of proteins, DNA, RNA and other molecules in the human body. This could significantly accelerate drug discovery efforts and our understanding of diseases.[4]

- **Apple's Controversial "Crush!" iPad Ad Triggers AI Fears:** Apple released a new iPad ad showing a machine crushing creative tools like musical instruments and cameras. The ad's destructive imagery in promoting the iPad sparked backlash, with some seeing it as a metaphor for AI displacing human creativity.[6][7]

- **Texas Seeks to Set National Agenda on AI Policy:** The Texas legislature is pushing new bills to regulate artificial intelligence development. Elected officials aim to build on the state's previous leadership in data and cybersecurity policy to influence the national AI agenda.[8]
