## 2024-03-17 News in AI

### AI and Accounting
- **Books Reviewed**: A selection of new books on AI and accounting were reviewed, including titles such as *Artificial Intelligence: Law & Litigation* by Mark Davies and *Artificial Intelligence for Audit, Forensic Accounting, and Valuation* by Al Naqvi[1].

### Prompt Engineering in Generative AI
- **Best Practices**: An article discussed the importance of prompt engineering when leveraging imported text in generative AI, emphasizing the need for practice and understanding the context window size limits of generative AI applications[2].

### Elon Musk's Grok Chatbot
- **Open Source Initiative**: Elon Musk announced the decision to open source the code behind Grok Chatbot, marking a significant move in the ongoing AI technology competition[3].

### AI Security Concerns
- **Security Warning**: Developers and data scientists are urged not to overlook security in the rush to build and ship AI products, highlighting the importance of incorporating security measures from the start[4].

### AI's Impact on the NFL and Nvidia
- **Market Speculation**: Discussions around whether the AI market is a bubble and Nvidia's role in potentially transforming the NFL through technology were highlighted. Goldman Sachs analysts commented on the AI market's current state, suggesting it is not a bubble yet[5][6].

### AI-Driven Marketing for SaaS Companies
- **The Unmanned Agency**: AP Digital launched a new service aimed at boosting SaaS companies' ARR through AI-driven marketing, focusing on enhanced lead generation and conversion rates[7].

### Deepfake in Politics
- **Legal Action**: The League of Women Voters filed a lawsuit over a deepfake robocall of Biden in New Hampshire, marking one of the first major uses of AI to interfere with a U.S. presidential election[8].

These highlights from March 17, 2024, reflect the diverse and evolving landscape of artificial intelligence, touching on topics from accounting and legal implications to security, marketing, and even political interference.
