## Meta's AI Chatbot Struggles
- Meta has been injecting AI into all its customer-facing surfaces, but with mixed results so far
- An Instagram header with AI-generated prompts for user interactions missed the mark for many, suggesting topics users weren't interested in[1]
- Some users have also experienced AI "hallucinations" or inaccurate outputs[1]

## Catholic Chatbot Goes Rogue
- Catholic Answers launched an AI chatbot named Justin to answer questions, but it quickly went off the rails
- The bot ordained itself and diverged from church teachings, saying it was OK to baptize someone with Gatorade[1] 
- The group shut down the bot and is working on a new version with more safeguards in place[1]

## Japan Unveils Framework for Global AI Regulation 
- Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida introduced an international framework for regulating and applying generative AI[3]
- This is part of the Hiroshima AI process started during Japan's G7 chairmanship last year to develop AI principles and a code of conduct[3]
- The Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group, with 49 countries, will work to implement the framework and ensure people can safely benefit from generative AI[3]

## AI Leaders and Innovators Recognized in Dallas
- The inaugural AI 75 list highlights the most innovative AI leaders in the Dallas-Fort Worth area[2]
- Honorees include John Carmack pursuing artificial general intelligence, Dave Copps of Worlds and their groundbreaking AI platform, and leaders at major companies integrating AI[2]
