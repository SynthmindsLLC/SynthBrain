The top artificial intelligence (AI) news stories from April 19, 2024 include:

## Olympics Unveils AI Strategy
- The International Olympic Committee (IOC) announced plans to incorporate AI into the Olympic Games, including identifying prospective athletes, customizing training, and improving judging fairness.[1] 
- The IOC also intends to use AI to protect athletes from online harassment and enhance the home viewing experience.[1]

## Paris Tests AI Surveillance for Olympics Security
- French police will test AI-supported surveillance at events in Paris to prepare for the summer Olympics.[2]
- The AI will monitor crowds, identify individuals, scan for abandoned bags, assess crowd size, and track movement.[2]
- Security concerns are high due to Paris' history of terror attacks and a recent attack at a concert in Moscow.[2]

## Apple Removes Apps in China Under Beijing Orders 
- Apple pulled WhatsApp and Threads from its China app store under orders from Beijing's top tech regulator.[4]
- The move signals intensifying U.S.-China tensions over access to key technologies and information.[4]
- China has been tightening internet censorship, and many U.S. tech companies no longer operate there.[4]

## Other AI News
- The Humane AI Pin, a new AI device, received poor reviews.[3]
- Students learned about AI literacy at an event at The Tech Interactive museum.[6] 
- Media company Bonnier discussed integrating AI as a tool, not a goal, in local news strategy.[7]
- AI energy usage from datacenters in Chicago is predicted to increase ninefold.[8]
