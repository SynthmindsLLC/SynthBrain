## Microsoft Build 2024 Highlights AI Innovations
- Microsoft unveiled new AI features integrated into Windows, Copilot, and the Edge browser at its annual Build developer conference[20].
- Copilot AI agents will act as virtual assistants to automate mundane business tasks without replacing jobs[20]. 
- The Phi-3-vision multimodal AI model was introduced, capable of processing both text and images[20].
- Microsoft Edge is getting real-time video translation between multiple languages[20].

## Google I/O 2024 Focuses on Gemini AI and Android 15
- Google is expected to provide an update on its advanced Gemini AI model, potentially powering a new Google Assistant codenamed Pixie[15].
- Numerous demonstrations will showcase how Google's AI is enhancing existing products like Maps, Docs, Chrome, Gmail, and search[15].
- Android 15, currently in beta, will have its key features recapped for the developer audience[15].

## DHS Warns of AI Threats to 2024 Election Integrity
- The Department of Homeland Security issued a warning about the potential use of AI to create fake content and manipulate voters in the 2024 US elections[9][10].
- AI-generated deepfakes could amplify false claims of electoral fraud and conspiracy theories[10].
- The rapid advancement of AI capabilities in recent years raises concerns about its use by malicious actors to disrupt elections[10].

## FCC Proposes AI Transparency Rules for Political Ads
- The FCC unveiled a proposal mandating disclosure of AI use in broadcast TV and radio political ads to combat voter manipulation[16].
- Broadcasters would need to verify with advertisers if their content was AI-generated, but specifics like disclosure methods are still to be determined[16].
- The FCC's jurisdiction is limited to broadcast channels, leaving digital and streaming platforms unregulated[16].

## Breakthroughs in AI for Scientific Discovery
- NASA is harnessing the power of AI responsibly to drive groundbreaking discoveries in heliophysics, Earth science, and astronomy[11].
- Researchers trained an AI model to accurately predict male fruit fly courtship behavior, offering insights into brain processing and artificial vision[14].
- The costs of building and maintaining large language models have skyrocketed, with OpenAI's GPT-4 and Google's Gemini Ultra requiring $78 million and $191 million respectively for compute[3].
