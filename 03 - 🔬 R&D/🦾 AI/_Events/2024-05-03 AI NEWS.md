## AI Adoption on the Rise
- The U.S. Census Bureau reports that AI adoption by businesses has nearly doubled from 3.7% in fall 2021 to 5.4% in February 2022, with an expected increase to 6.6% by early fall 2022.[2]
- Despite the growth, overall AI adoption remains modest, with variations across industries and firm sizes.[2]
- Colorado and the District of Columbia lead in AI adoption rates, while Mississippi has the lowest.[2]

## New AI Tools and Offerings
- Amazon Web Services (AWS) launched Amazon Q, a generative AI-powered assistant for software development and business operations, promising significant productivity improvements.[1]
- Anthropic announced its first enterprise AI product and a free iPhone app, offering enhanced capabilities for content creation, data analysis, and business needs.[1]

## Legal Challenges and Ethical Concerns
- Several U.S. newspapers are suing OpenAI and Microsoft for copyright infringement, alleging their articles were used without permission to train AI models.[1]
- The lawsuit highlights the importance of ethical content usage and intellectual property rights in AI development and deployment.[1]
- A Maryland high school principal faced controversy due to a deepfake video, emphasizing the dangers of AI technology and the need for countermeasures.[4][8]

## AI in Various Industries
- Top U.S. tech CEOs joined a new AI safety board initiated by Homeland Security to safeguard critical services from AI-related disruptions.[1]
- Law firms face competition from various industries in the battle for AI talent.[5]
- AI is being applied in the food industry, with a radio show featuring an AI recipe help desk.[7]

## Global AI Race
- A report reveals that China tops the U.S. in AI research in over half of the hottest fields, highlighting nuances in the global AI race between the two countries.[6]
