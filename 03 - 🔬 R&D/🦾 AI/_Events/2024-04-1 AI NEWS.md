Recent developments in artificial intelligence (AI) have been highlighted across various sectors, including technology conferences, education, healthcare, and corporate updates. Here's a roundup of AI news as of April 1, 2024:

## Tech Conferences
- **Google Cloud Next 2024**: Scheduled for April 9-11 in Las Vegas, this conference will focus on enterprise and developer-centric advancements in AI, with Google expected to discuss future software development and generative AI[1].
- **ODSC East 2024**: Happening in Boston from April 23-25, this event will feature over 280 speakers and more than 60 workshops on data science and machine learning, with a keynote from MIT professor Alex Pentland[1].
- **Intel Vision 2024**: Intel's conference on April 8-9 in Phoenix will cover AI and its integration into business, with a keynote by CEO Pat Gelsinger[1].
- **Microsoft 365 Community Conference**: This event will discuss the integration of Microsoft's AI products into service offerings, focusing on the AI-powered workplace[1].

## AI in Education
- **Hartford AI Day**: UConn students demonstrated AI skills to business professionals, with a focus on machine learning and chatbots. The event saw record attendance and included speakers from various industries[2].
- **AI-Savvy Students**: College students are entering the workforce with a distinct advantage in AI, with programs like UConn's Innovate Labs introducing them to emerging technologies[2].

## AI in Healthcare
- **AI's Impact on Epidemiology and Infection Control**: AI is being used in healthcare to improve data analysis, research, and education, with potential to simplify tasks for infection preventionists and epidemiologists[3].

## Corporate AI Updates
- **AWS Weekly Roundup**: AWS announced updates on generative AI, community days, and strategic collaborations, including the use of AWS Trainium and Inferentia chips for AI research and development[4].
- **AMD's AI Chip**: Speculation surrounds Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) possibly working on a secret AI chip, with recent updates affecting the company's stock[5].
