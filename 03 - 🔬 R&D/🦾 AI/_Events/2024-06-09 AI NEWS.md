## Apple's AI Focus at WWDC 2024

Apple's Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC) 2024 is rumored to heavily emphasize AI integration within its operating systems, particularly with enhancements to Siri[1]. Key anticipated announcements include:

- Siri's ability to control individual app functions using voice commands
- AI-generated emojis that can be created on the fly based on user text input 
- AI-powered features in built-in iOS apps like Voice Memos, Messages, Photos
- Potential third-party partnership (possibly with OpenAI) for a ChatGPT-like chatbot on Apple devices

Analysts believe compelling new AI features, especially if exclusive to upcoming iPhone 16 models, could spur a much-needed upgrade cycle for Apple after several stagnant years[2]. 

## Comparing AI Stocks: C3.ai vs Super Micro Computer

C3.ai and Super Micro Computer (Supermicro) are two prominent AI stocks with diverging investor sentiment recently[3]. Some key points:

- C3.ai has struggled with decelerating growth, customer concentration risk, lack of profits
- Supermicro has seen rapid growth driven by strong demand for its AI servers 
- Supermicro appears to have a more favorable risk/reward profile currently based on growth potential and valuation

## Perplexica Open-Source AI Search Tool

Perplexica is a new open-source project aiming to provide a transparent, customizable alternative to proprietary AI-powered search engines like Perplexity AI[4]. It leverages open-source large language models to deliver context-aware, insightful search results while allowing searches to be conducted locally for privacy.

## Miss AI: World's First AI Beauty Pageant 

The world's first beauty pageant for AI models, Miss AI, has selected 10 finalists from 1500 submissions[5]. While sparking fascination, it has also drawn criticism for perpetuating stereotypical beauty standards. The AI models are being evaluated on realism, messaging, and social media engagement as potential AI influencers.

## California Proposes AI Kill-Switch Bill

California lawmakers have proposed a new bill to regulate the development of large AI models, requiring safety protocols, oversight, and a "kill switch"[6]. The bill has support from some AI ethicists but is opposed by many tech companies. If passed, it could have significant implications for AI development in Silicon Valley and beyond.
