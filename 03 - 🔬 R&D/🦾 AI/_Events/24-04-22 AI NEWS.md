Here are the top AI news stories from April 22, 2024:

- **AI Armor: Learn How to Harness AI to Invest in Your Company's Future**: A new book by Robert Plotkin provides actionable strategies for AI companies to secure their intellectual property and compete against tech giants using patents and trade secrets.[1]

- **Cognizant and Microsoft announce global partnership to expand adoption of generative AI in the enterprise**: The partnership will leverage Microsoft Copilot and Cognizant's services to help enterprise customers operationalize generative AI for business transformation. Cognizant is deploying Copilot to millions of users and training developers on GitHub Copilot.[2]

- **SAP Cloud Revenue Matches Estimates as AI Adoption Fuels Demand**: SAP SE reported 25% growth in cloud revenue, meeting analyst expectations, driven by strong demand for AI which is fueling adoption of its cloud services.[3]

- **A.I.-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material May Overwhelm Tip Line**: A Stanford report warns that highly realistic AI-generated CSAM could inundate the CyberTipline used by law enforcement, making it even harder to distinguish real cases. Researchers call for more resources and updated legislation to address this threat.[4][5]

- **AI could help determine your salary, and that's a good thing**: Combining AI and predictive analytics could enable more reliable, less biased algorithms for determining compensation over time.[7]

- **AI Boosts Demand for High-Capacity HDDs**: Following a price increase by Western Digital, Seagate is also expected to raise prices on high-capacity hard disk drives as demand surges due to AI applications.[8]

In summary, AI continues to drive innovation, partnerships and demand across cloud services, enterprise transformation, and hardware. However, challenges are also emerging around IP protection for AI startups and the potential for AI-generated content to exacerbate online child exploitation. Businesses and policymakers will need to collaborate to harness the benefits of AI while mitigating the risks.
