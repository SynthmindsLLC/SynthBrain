## Amazon's AI Strategy and Market Movements
- **Amazon CEO Optimistic About Generative AI**: Amazon CEO Andy Jassy remains positive about the company's future in generative AI, despite the recent pullback from the cashierless checkout technology, Just Walk Out. Amazon's strategy focuses on building fundamental AI models and selling them to enterprises, rather than creating consumer-facing applications[1].
- **Investment in Generative AI**: Amazon has invested an additional $2.75 billion in generative AI, totaling $4 billion in Anthropic, which will use Amazon Web Services. This includes access to Anthropic's Claude, a leading generative AI model, for Amazon's enterprise clients[1].
- **Challenges and Cost-Cutting Measures**: Amazon has faced difficulties in captivating customers with consumer-facing products like Rufus and a smarter Alexa assistant. The company has also been cutting costs, particularly in its fulfillment and logistics division, after overexpansion during the pandemic[1].

## Apple's AI-Infused M4 Chips for Macs
- **Apple's Mac Overhaul**: Apple is planning to overhaul its Mac line with AI-focused M4 chips, targeting a release late this year and into the next. The updated lineup is expected to include new iMacs, MacBook Pros, and Mac minis[2].
- **Stock Market Reaction**: Apple's shares jumped following the report of the planned AI-powered Macs, contributing to a 0.7% gain in the S&P 500 and a 1.7% surge in the Nasdaq[3].
