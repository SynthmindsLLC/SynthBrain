## Key AI Trends for 2024

- Next generation generative AI models that go beyond text and images to video, music, and multimodal capabilities[1][7] 
- Increased accessibility of AI, with tools to allow non-experts to customize powerful language models for their needs[1][2]
- Growing focus on responsible AI development, including efforts to mitigate bias, improve transparency, and ensure AI systems remain accountable[1][3]
- AI legislation being developed by governments around the world to regulate the technology while enabling innovation[1][4]
- Quantum computing beginning to have practical applications in accelerating AI workloads[1]

## Google I/O 2024 AI Announcements

- AI Overviews feature rolling out in Google Search to provide AI-generated summaries above traditional search results[10][13][15] 
- Upgrades to Google's Gemini large language model, including multimodal capabilities and a faster "Flash" version[10][11][17]
- LearnLM, a new AI model focused on education use cases[12]
- Imagen 3 for improved text rendering in AI image generation[13]
- Expansions of generative AI across Google Workspace apps[13]

## Other Notable AI News

- Microsoft Build 2024 expected to focus heavily on AI, including a new AI-powered Bing Chat[5][17]
- NVIDIA announces Project GR00T foundation model for humanoid robots and major updates to its Isaac robotics platform[18] 
- New developments help clarify patent law around AI-generated inventions[14]
- USC launches a new $10 million Center for Generative AI and Society[16]
- The global AI market projected to reach $184 billion in 2024[20]
