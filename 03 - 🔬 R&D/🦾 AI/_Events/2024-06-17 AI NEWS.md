## Microsoft Expands Trustworthy and Responsible AI Network in Europe
- Microsoft announced the expansion of its Trustworthy & Responsible AI Network (TRAIN) to Europe to help healthcare organizations operationalize responsible AI through technology-based guardrails.[1]
- European TRAIN members include major hospitals and research institutions across the Netherlands, Finland, Sweden, and Italy.[1]
- The network aims to improve the quality, safety and trustworthiness of AI tools implemented in healthcare.[1]

## U.S. Government Launches AI-Focused Presidential Innovation Fellows Cohort 
- The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) launched its second class of Presidential Innovation Fellows (PIF) in 2024, the first cohort exclusively focused on artificial intelligence.[2]
- The 11 AI experts will work on projects at 8 federal agencies, such as using AI to increase access to justice and enhance electric grid infrastructure.[2]
- This is part of the government's efforts to recruit top AI talent and advance responsible AI development and use.[2]

## NVIDIA Unveils Omniverse Microservices for Physically Accurate Sensor Simulation
- NVIDIA announced Omniverse Cloud Sensor RTX, a set of microservices that enable physically accurate sensor simulation to accelerate the development of autonomous machines.[3]
- Built on the OpenUSD framework and powered by NVIDIA RTX ray-tracing and neural-rendering technologies, it allows developers to test sensor perception and AI software in realistic virtual environments.[3]
- This will help enable the development of large-scale digital twins of factories, cities and even the Earth.[3]

## Other Notable AI News
- Oracle enhanced its low-code app development platform APEX with a generative AI-powered assistant that allows developers to build apps using natural language prompts.[7]
- India is attracting multibillion dollar investments from Amazon and Microsoft for its AI efforts, as the country aims to become a major AI consumer and exporter.[5]
- ChatGPT and other OpenAI services experienced a major outage on June 17.[9]
- You.com, an AI search startup, is raising $50 million in fresh capital as it targets the AI assistant market.[12]
