Here are the top AI-related news stories from April 21, 2024:

- **DC Comics pulls covers over AI content concerns**: DC Comics' decision to pull and replace certain comic book covers due to concerns over AI-generated content topped traffic on Bleeding Cool.[1]

- **Bitcoin miners explore AI to survive halving event**: With the Bitcoin halving cutting miner revenues in half, some mining companies like Core Scientific are diversifying into AI and high-performance computing to generate new revenue streams. However, transitioning Bitcoin mining facilities to support AI workloads presents challenges.[2]

- **Mark Cuban Foundation brings AI bootcamp to Fort Lauderdale**: The Mark Cuban Foundation is hosting an AI bootcamp in Fort Lauderdale, Florida as part of its ongoing initiatives.[7]

- **Salvador Dalí AI installation allows museum visitors to converse with the artist**: The Dalí Museum in St. Petersburg, Florida debuted "Ask Dalí", an AI-powered installation that uses a replica of Dalí's Lobster Telephone sculpture. Visitors can ask questions and hear responses generated by GPT-4 trained on Dalí's interviews, writings, and online information to mimic the artist's voice and speaking style.[8]
