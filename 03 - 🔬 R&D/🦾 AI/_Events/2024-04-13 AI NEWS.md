The past few days have seen significant developments and discussions around the impact and applications of artificial intelligence across various sectors:

## AI and Jobs
- As AI threatens to displace a significant portion of American jobs, the idea of a guaranteed income is regaining attention as a potential safety net. Advocates like Michael Tubbs propose planning for such programs before the widespread job losses occur.[1]
- Tech leaders like Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and Sam Altman have also championed the need for a guaranteed income in the face of AI-driven job displacement.[1]

## AI in Entertainment
- Hollywood performers and media professionals reached a preliminary agreement with major record labels, which includes safeguards against AI encroachment in creative processes. The deal mandates consent and compensation for AI-generated songs mimicking artists' voices.[3]
- AI is being used in film restoration for new home video releases, like James Cameron's "True Lies," sparking debate about the authenticity of the enhanced visuals.[6]
- AI is seen as a key trend driving the future of the U.S. broadcast, media, and entertainment industry.[7]

## AI in Education
- In Mali, start-up RobotsMali is using AI to create children's books in local languages like Bambara, helping to democratize access to education.[4]
- The potential of AI in education, particularly for African languages, is seen as enormous if implemented correctly.[4]

## AI in Healthcare
- Dr. Sanjay Gupta discussed the future of AI in medicine, emphasizing the need for a "trust, but verify" approach as the technology is intended to fill gaps but can sometimes be incorrect.[2]

## AI in Social Media
- Meta is gradually integrating its AI advancements into social media platforms, with a conversational chatbot being tested on Instagram following its introduction on WhatsApp.[5]

These developments highlight the growing influence of AI across various sectors, with both potential benefits and challenges to navigate as the technology continues to advance.
