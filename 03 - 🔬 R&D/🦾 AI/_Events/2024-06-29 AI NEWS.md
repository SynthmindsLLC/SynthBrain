The world of artificial intelligence continues to evolve rapidly in 2024. Here are some of the key developments and trends as of June 29:

## The Rise of Agentic AI 
Early-phase generative AI, or "request/response AI", has not yet lived up to the hype and expectations. However, experts believe that agentic AI represents the next level that will drive tangible business value, especially for enterprises[1]. 

Agentic AI is designed to pursue goals with human supervision. Agents make plans to perform work on a user's behalf given a specific objective. They can also work together, orchestrated by a supervising agent[1].

While consumer applications of agentic AI face challenges navigating the open-ended complexity of the web, enterprise use cases show more near-term potential. With defined destinations and clear routes, enterprise AI agents can accomplish valuable work much sooner[1].

## Generative AI and Copyright Challenges
As generative AI expands, it raises critical copyright questions[3]:

1. Is AI-generated content protected by copyright? 
2. Does training AI on copyrighted material violate the owner's rights?

The U.S. Copyright Office states AI-generated content is not copyrightable because it lacks human authorship. However, works with AI-generated elements combined with "sufficient human authorship" may qualify for protection[3]. 

Ongoing litigation in 2024 will help define the boundaries around using copyrighted material to train AI systems. The evolving legal landscape will shape the future of generative AI and intellectual property[3].

## Allegations Against Perplexity AI
Amazon Web Services is investigating claims that AI startup Perplexity AI scraped online news content, including from sites prohibiting such actions, to train its models[4].

Perplexity, which utilizes AWS servers, denies violating Amazon's terms of service. However, the company has faced recent controversy over alleged content summarization without proper attribution and generating fake quotes[4].

Perplexity's CEO defends that the company aggregates data from other AI systems and is working to improve source attribution. The outcome of Amazon's review remains to be seen[4].

## Other AI News
- Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced the company will begin surfacing AI characters made by creators through its Meta AI studio[2].

- A roundup of the latest AI research papers this week covered topics like a 70B parameter model outperforming humans on the MMLU benchmark, and techniques for controllable story generation[5].

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence continues to present both exciting opportunities and complex challenges across industries and domains. Agentic AI, generative AI, and content ownership issues will be key areas to watch in the coming months and years.
