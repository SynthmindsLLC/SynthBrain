## Technology

- **OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind workers warn of AI's dangers** [13]
	- Current and former employees from top AI companies signed a letter calling for more transparency and oversight around AI development to mitigate potential risks and negative impacts.

- **Apple introduces ReALM, an innovative AI system surpassing GPT-4** [17]  
	- Apple unveiled a new AI model called ReALM that promises to revolutionize how voice assistants like Siri process and respond to commands, potentially outperforming OpenAI's GPT-4.

- **Google rolls back AI search feature after flubs and flaws** [10]
	- Google temporarily disabled its new AI-powered search result overviews as it works to reduce errors and inaccuracies in the AI-generated summaries.

## Business

- **Amazon adds AI heavyweight Andrew Ng to board of directors** [9]
	- Prominent AI expert Andrew Ng, known for his work at Google and Baidu, joined Amazon's board as the company ramps up investments in generative AI, which CEO Andy Jassy believes could be its next major business pillar.

- **Hitachi and Microsoft enter $2B strategic partnership to accelerate business innovation with generative AI** [4]
	- The 3-year deal will embed Microsoft's AI technologies into Hitachi's Lumada solutions to drive transformation in industries like energy and mobility. Hitachi also plans to train 50,000 employees on advanced AI skills.

## Healthcare

- **FDA grants breakthrough designation for at-home cervical cancer screening AI device** [6] 
	- Teal Health's Teal Wand, which uses AI to enable self-collection and analysis of samples for cervical cancer screening, received FDA Breakthrough Device status after promising clinical trial results.

- **AI-powered software offers breakthrough for treating dyslexia** [15]
	- Dysolve, the first AI platform for correcting dyslexia, is undergoing clinical trials in schools after helping hundreds of students improve reading and learning skills through personalized brain training games.

- **Op-comic: AI in healthcare promises to benefit doctors and patients** [18]
	- A doctor's illustrated commentary on how AI scribes and assistants can reduce screen time during exams and allow physicians to focus more on direct patient care.

## Other News

- **Mourners can now speak to an AI version of the dead, raising questions on grief and ethics** [7]
	- Companies are offering services to create AI chatbots that mimic deceased loved ones, sparking a debate on whether it helps or hinders the grieving process.

- **The growing overlap between art and artificial intelligence** [8]
	- An analysis of how artists are embracing AI and robotics in their work, such as Pierre Huyghe's piece with AI-powered robots conducting a ritual over human remains, and the implications for the art world.
