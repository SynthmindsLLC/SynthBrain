## Antitrust Probes and Regulation
- The FTC opened an antitrust probe into Microsoft's AI deal and has sent subpoenas to the tech giant and startup, asking whether their partnership evaded required government review.[5]
- The Justice Department and FTC agreed to divide responsibility for investigating Microsoft, OpenAI and Nvidia for potential antitrust violations in the AI sector.[7] 
- The Treasury Department issued an RFI seeking public feedback on the uses, opportunities and risks of AI in the financial sector as part of its ongoing exploration of the technology's potential.[11]

## Business Applications
- Meta launched new AI tools for businesses on WhatsApp, including AI-powered ad targeting and Meta Verified for enhanced account support.[2][17]
- A new AI copilot plugin was unveiled that can transform existing enterprise systems for financial institutions in seconds, drawing data from various sources.[6]
- Cisco announced Nexus HyperFabric AI Clusters with NVIDIA to enhance AI workloads for data centers.[16]

## Warnings from AI Employees
- A coalition of employees from OpenAI, Google DeepMind and other companies warned that AI systems are powerful enough to pose serious risks like inequality, misinformation and loss of control without proper regulation.[9]

## Space Exploration 
- China's Chang'e-6 mission took a historic selfie from the far side of the moon using an autonomous AI-powered mini-rover, a testament to their software team's AI breakthroughs.[8]

## Other News
- Carnegie Learning's LiveHint AI won the "AI Innovation Award" in the 2024 EdTech Breakthrough Awards.[12][14]
- UT Austin launched a new podcast "AI for the Rest of Us" to explain AI concepts and discuss how to ensure the technology is safe and beneficial.[13]
