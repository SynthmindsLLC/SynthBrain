The world of artificial intelligence saw significant developments on June 11, 2024, with major tech companies making moves, governments weighing regulations, and AI being integrated into various sectors. Here are the top stories:

## Apple Soars with AI Announcements at WWDC 2024
- Apple unveiled new AI features for iOS 18, iPadOS, and macOS at its Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC), including enhancements to Siri and integration with OpenAI's ChatGPT[1][4][5][8][11][17].
- The company's stock hit an all-time high following the announcements, propelling its market cap past Microsoft to become the world's most valuable company once again[6][11][15][17].

## U.S. Government Considers AI Chip Restrictions on China
- The Biden administration is weighing further limits on China's access to advanced AI chips, particularly those with gate-all-around (GAA) architecture[10].
- This move aims to prevent China from using AI technology for military purposes and follows previous restrictions on AI chip sales to Chinese companies[10].

## AI Education and Workforce Development Initiatives
- Miami Dade College received a $2.9 million grant from the National Science Foundation to create the National Applied Artificial Intelligence Consortium, which will support AI education and workforce development at community colleges nationwide[3].
- Microsoft and LinkedIn released data from their Work Trend Index 2024, revealing high AI adoption rates among Indonesian workers and the importance of AI skills for employment[7].

## Protecting Workers in the Age of AI
- The White House released a set of principles to protect workers and ensure their involvement in AI implementation in the workplace[12].
- Colorado passed the Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act, regulating "high-risk" AI systems and requiring transparency in AI use for employment decisions[12].

## G7 Summit to Address AI with Papal Intervention
- World leaders at the upcoming G7 Summit in Italy will discuss AI regulation, with Pope Francis invited to provide guidance on balancing AI's benefits and risks[9][18].
- The summit aims to establish a Western-led framework for AI development and export AI philosophy to the Global South[9].

These developments underscore the rapid advancement of AI and its growing impact on various sectors, from tech and education to government policy and international relations. As AI continues to shape the future, stakeholders must navigate the challenges and opportunities it presents.
