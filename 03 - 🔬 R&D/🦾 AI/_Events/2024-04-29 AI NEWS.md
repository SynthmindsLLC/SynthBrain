- **Second global AI safety summit faces tough questions, lower turnout**: The second AI Safety Summit, co-hosted by Britain and South Korea, is taking place amidst questions about AI's limitations and challenges such as data scarcity and environmental impact. Key attendees from the first summit have turned down invitations.[1]

- **Friends From the Old Neighborhood Turn Rivals in Big Tech's A.I. Race**: Demis Hassabis, CEO of Google DeepMind, and Mustafa Suleyman, CEO of Microsoft AI, who grew up together in London, now find themselves on opposite sides of the intensifying AI competition among tech giants.[2] 

- **Is Microsoft an Excellent AI Stock to Buy?**: Microsoft is capitalizing on the rising effectiveness of AI, prompting investors to consider whether the stock is a good buy at current valuations.[3]

- **San José City Clerk Launches Artificial Intelligence Initiative**: The City Clerk of San José, California has launched an AI initiative to increase language access at city council meetings and public hearings.[4]

- **A better virtual reality experience**: Researchers at The University of Texas at Arlington are working on improving the virtual reality experience using AI and other technologies.[5]

- **Deepfake of principal's voice is the latest case of AI being used for harm**: In Baltimore County, Maryland, a deepfake of a high school principal's voice was used in a criminal case, highlighting the potential for AI to be misused.[7]

- **AI and the future of health care: 'Broader reach, more equity'**: Highmark Health is exploring how AI can be used to improve healthcare outcomes and increase equity in access to care.[8]
