## 2024-03-18 in AI News

On March 18, 2024, significant developments in the AI sector were reported, highlighting collaborations and advancements that could shape the future of technology and AI applications.

- **Apple and Google Collaboration**: Apple Inc. is in discussions to integrate Google's Gemini artificial intelligence engine into the iPhone. This partnership could potentially bring Google's generative AI models to power new features in iPhone software, marking a significant collaboration between two tech giants[1][3]. Alphabet shares saw a more than 6% increase, while Apple's shares went up by 2.5%[3].

- **UC Irvine's AI Tool**: The University of California, Irvine, launched a customized generative artificial intelligence tool, showcasing the growing interest in tailored AI solutions across educational institutions[2].

- **Microsoft and NVIDIA Partnership**: Microsoft and NVIDIA announced major integrations to accelerate generative AI for enterprises. This includes adopting NVIDIA's Grace Blackwell Superchip in Microsoft Azure to enhance AI offerings and the introduction of NVIDIA generative AI Microservices on Microsoft Azure AI[4].

- **NVIDIA's New AI Chips**: NVIDIA announced a new generation of AI chips named Blackwell, expected to ship later this year. These chips are designed to support the running of AI models, indicating NVIDIA's continued dominance in providing essential hardware for AI development[5].

- **Market Response**: The tech sector led stocks higher, with notable increases in shares of Alphabet and NVIDIA, driven by enthusiasm for AI developments. Alphabet's shares jumped amid reports of its talks with Apple, while NVIDIA's shares ticked up in anticipation of its AI developer conference[6][7].

These developments underscore the rapid pace of innovation in the AI sector, with major companies forging partnerships and launching new technologies to harness the power of artificial intelligence.
