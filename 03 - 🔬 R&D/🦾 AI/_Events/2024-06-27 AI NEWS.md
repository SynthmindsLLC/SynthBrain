## New AI Lab Reignites Debate
The departure of Ilya Sutskever from OpenAI to found a new AI lab called Safe Superintelligence (SSI) has sparked debate in the AI community. SSI aims to advance superhuman AI but does not plan to release products or disclose investors, leading to speculation about their motives and potential breakthroughs[1].

## AI Drives Amazon's $2 Trillion Valuation 
Amazon reached a $2 trillion market valuation, largely driven by investor enthusiasm around its AI initiatives. The company's shares surged as it invests in AI startups and custom chips to fuel its AI-driven growth strategies[2].

## AI Developments in Robotics and Conservation
AI is enabling advances in robotics, with companies like Olis Robotics using AI-powered software to automate and operate robots in challenging environments[3]. Machine learning is also being applied to wildlife conservation, with an Oxford University project using AI to individually recognize and track endangered chimpanzees[3].

## Stock Market Gains Fueled by AI 
The Nasdaq Composite surged nearly 19% in the first half of 2024, driven by excitement around artificial intelligence technologies. The S&P 500 also saw significant gains of about 15%. Analysts attribute the market's strong performance to the dominance of AI themes influencing investor sentiment[4].

## Companies to Watch in the AI Boom
Beyond Nvidia, several companies are benefiting from increased AI investment[5]:

- Micron - memory chip maker seeing revenue growth from AI demand 
- Vertiv - cooling systems provider for AI data centers
- Palantir - data company offering practical AI solutions for enterprises
- TSMC - chip manufacturing giant capitalizing on AI hardware needs

Major tech firms like Amazon, Microsoft, Google and Oracle are also expected to benefit as organizations invest more in specialized AI computing services[5].
