## Business and Investment

- India's largest family office, PremjiInvest, plans to increase investments in AI companies and enhance its proprietary AI investment tools, according to CIO TK Kurien. The firm has seen "exponential returns" in the AI space.[2]

- Luxury powerhouse LVMH is partnering with Stanford University's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence to explore AI applications in sales predictions, customer experience, supply chain, marketing, manufacturing, and product design.[1] 

- Despite the AI hype, MongoDB CEO Dev Ittycheria believes it will take time for AI to fundamentally transform lives, with the real value coming from applications built on top of AI technologies.[3]

## Regulation and Policy

- Florida Governor Ron DeSantis signed a bill requiring disclaimers on certain political ads created using artificial intelligence.[5]

- The U.S. federal government passed a bill mandating the sale of TikTok to a U.S.-friendly owner or face a ban, but the company promises a court battle.[1]

## Technology and Applications

- Tesla CEO Elon Musk made a surprise visit to China to discuss the rollout of Tesla's Full Self-Driving system with officials, as the company seeks regulatory approval in new markets.[4]

- The Dallas Stars NHL team is using AI-based tools to brainstorm and create playoff marketing assets.[7]

- Apple's upcoming 2024 OLED iPad Pro is rumored to feature the M4 chip, potentially making it the company's first truly AI-powered device.[8]

- AI-enhanced camera technology led to the arrest of a murder suspect in Blue Springs, Missouri.[6]
