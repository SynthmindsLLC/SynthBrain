## Major AI Outages Hit ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity
- ChatGPT experienced a multi-hour outage in the early morning, followed by a second lengthy outage later in the day.[2][5] 
- Anthropic's Claude and Perplexity AI also faced issues around the same time as ChatGPT's second outage, hinting at potential broader infrastructure problems.[5]
- The concurrent outages among these major AI providers is an uncommon occurrence. OpenAI has not yet provided details on the cause.[2][5]

## Cisco Unveils New AI Infrastructure Solution and $1B AI Investment Fund
- Cisco announced the Nexus HyperFabric AI cluster solution in partnership with NVIDIA, simplifying the deployment of generative AI applications in data centers.[1][6][12]
- Cisco is also launching a $1 billion global AI investment fund to advance industry innovation, support customer AI readiness, and further Cisco's AI strategy.[1][12]
- New Cisco Learning and Certifications programs will help partners and IT pros build AI skills.[6]

## OpenAI Insiders Call for Stronger Whistleblower Protections 
- A group of current and former OpenAI employees published an open letter urging AI companies to establish robust whistleblower protections for workers flagging AI safety risks.[7][8][10]
- The insiders allege OpenAI has neglected safeguards and is recklessly racing to develop artificial general intelligence (AGI), prioritizing growth over safety.[10] 
- They claim OpenAI has used restrictive NDAs to silence employees raising concerns. The letter calls for greater transparency across the AI industry.[7][8][10]

## Other Notable AI Developments
- AI is significantly improving the accuracy of breast cancer screenings, reducing false positives according to a study in Denmark.[11]
- USC researchers developed an AI system that can better detect rare forms of epileptic seizures by analyzing brain interactions.[14]
- A new AI model called Q* is demonstrating near-perfect math problem-solving abilities.[17]
