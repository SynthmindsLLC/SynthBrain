Several notable developments and discussions around artificial intelligence emerged on May 6, 2024:

- **Warren Buffett voices AI fears, likens tech to atom bomb**: At Berkshire Hathaway's annual shareholder meeting, Warren Buffett expressed concerns about the societal implications of AI, comparing its potential impact to the Manhattan Project and nuclear arms race. While acknowledging AI's benefits, Buffett emphasized the importance of ensuring it is used in a "pro-social way".[1]

- **AHA urges need for flexibility in regulation of AI in health care**: The American Hospital Association responded to a request for information from Rep. Ami Bera, stressing the need for flexible regulation of AI in healthcare to keep pace with innovation while addressing risks. The AHA suggested adapting existing technology-focused regulatory frameworks rather than creating entirely new ones.[2]

- **Hollywood faces generative AI labeling dilemma**: As generative AI is increasingly used in film and television, questions arise about disclosure and labeling. With no clear rules in place, some argue for transparency, while others view AI as an extension of long-standing special effects practices. The lack of standards has led to instances of undisclosed AI use in recent productions.[3]

- **Rise of AI agents that take action on users' behalf**: There is a growing trend toward AI "agents" that go beyond simple chatbots to perform tasks like booking flights or handling customer service. While offering productivity benefits, the use of AI agents also raises concerns about accuracy, risks, and the need for safeguards.[4]

- **People using AI to communicate with the deceased**: Some individuals are turning to AI avatar platforms to recreate and interact with digital versions of deceased loved ones. While these tools can provide comfort, they also raise ethical questions about data privacy and the implications of having AI generate content that the deceased never actually said.[5]

- **AI's impact on energy demand and the climate**: The rapid growth of AI is driving increased energy consumption by data centers, benefiting utilities and energy companies. However, the climate implications of this surge in power demand are not receiving as much attention as the financial aspects.[6]
