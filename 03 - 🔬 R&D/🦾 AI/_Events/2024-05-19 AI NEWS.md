## UK Opens AI Safety Office in San Francisco
- The UK's AI Safety Institute has opened a second office in San Francisco to be closer to the epicenter of AI development and work collaboratively with other countries on AI safety research. [5]
- The Institute recently released Inspect, its first set of tools for testing the safety of foundational AI models. The UK aims to present Inspect to global regulators to encourage adoption. [5]

## Concerns Grow Over AI-Generated Election Disinformation 
- Ahead of the 2024 U.S. presidential election, government officials and tech leaders are warning that chatbots and AI could be used to generate disinformation on social media at an unprecedented scale. [8]
- The New York Times created custom chatbots fed with Reddit and Parler data to demonstrate how easily they can generate diverse viewpoints on contentious political topics that could flood social platforms. [8]

## Apple to Introduce AI-Powered Features in iOS 18
- At WWDC in June, Apple is expected to unveil new AI capabilities in iOS 18, including auto-summarizing notifications, improved calendar features, and AI-based photo editing. [12][13]
- However, Apple is seen as playing catch-up in the generative AI race compared to Google and Microsoft. The new features likely won't include a ChatGPT-like chatbot. [12][13]

## Nvidia's AI Chip Dominance Faces Challengers
- Nvidia's GPU chips have become crucial for AI development, driving its market value over $2 trillion. Nvidia is expected to report over 200% year-on-year revenue growth. [16] 
- However, a new generation of AI chip startups are emerging to challenge Nvidia's crown in the lucrative AI hardware market. [16]

## Other AI News
- The White House issued an executive order to promote safe and trustworthy AI development, including prioritizing AI offerings in government authorization processes. [7]
- Experts warn that despite regulation efforts, the rise of AI is unstoppable as the technology rapidly advances. [17]
- Concerns mount that costly new AI-based weight loss drugs like Ozempic could strain government healthcare budgets. [19]
