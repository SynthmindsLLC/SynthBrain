Several notable developments in the field of artificial intelligence made headlines on April 17, 2024:

- **Norway's VG finding success with six AI-related newsroom tools**: Norwegian news outlet Verdens Gang (VG) is leveraging AI to enhance newsroom productivity, including article summaries appealing to young readers, a transcription tool for journalists, a statistics agent to create charts, and a bot to write appeals for Freedom of Information Act denials.[1]

- **Pharma industry pulse**: AI is transforming healthcare by efficiently handling time-consuming tasks. However, measuring the performance of AI systems remains a challenge. In other news, Precision BioSciences announced the return of programs and conclusion of its collaboration with Prevail Therapeutics.[2] 

- **Skilled farmers still needed in the age of AI**: While AI promises to make farming more productive and bring precision agriculture to the next level, the active management, intelligence, and experience of skilled farmers will still be essential.[3]

- **Microsoft's $13 billion OpenAI deal to avoid formal EU probe**: Microsoft's investment in OpenAI is set to avoid a formal investigation by European Union merger watchdogs, as it does not constitute a takeover.[4]

- **Russian US election interference targets support for Ukraine**: Foreign malign influence in the U.S. presidential elections has been slow to start, with AI-enhanced deepfakes and audio fakes not yet widely used.[5]

- **NYC high schooler creates AI algorithm for 911 callers**: A 17-year-old student developed an accurate AI model to predict resources needed for 911 calls with minimal input from the caller.[6]

- **AI eye exams prove their worth**: An AI algorithm can screen for diabetic retinopathy, putting ophthalmology alongside radiology in leveraging AI for quick diagnoses without a doctor.[7]

- **Logitech introduces AI prompt tool and AI-enabled mouse**: Logitech launched a free software tool to help users with ChatGPT prompts and an upcoming mouse with a dedicated AI button.[8]
