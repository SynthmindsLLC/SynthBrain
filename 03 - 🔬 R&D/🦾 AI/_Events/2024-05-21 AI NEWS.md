The world of artificial intelligence continues to evolve at a rapid pace in 2024. Here are some of the top AI news stories and breakthroughs from May:

## Google Unveils AI Overviews Feature at I/O 2024
- At its annual I/O developer conference, Google CEO Sundar Pichai revealed how the company is integrating AI more deeply into its search engine
- A new "AI Overviews" feature will generate concise summaries of information above traditional search results, potentially reducing traffic to web publishers
- Google also showcased a lightweight AI model, new chips, and AI "agents" to help users perform tasks

## Microsoft Set to Unveil Vision for AI PCs at Build 2024
- Following events from OpenAI and Google, Microsoft will showcase its latest AI projects at its Build developer conference 
- Microsoft is expected to reveal how Windows will power new "AI PCs" with dedicated AI chips from Intel, AMD and Qualcomm
- The company aims to maintain its edge in the AI race while also revitalizing stagnant PC sales

## Micron Lifts 2024 Capex Forecast on Rising AI Chip Demand
- Chipmaker Micron slightly raised its 2024 capital expenditure forecast as it ramps up production of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chips for AI
- Micron, one of the top HBM providers, has already sold out of its 2024 supply and allocated much of its 2025 capacity
- The company expects HBM to be a multi-billion dollar business in fiscal 2025 as AI demand soars

## News Publishers Sound Alarm on Google's AI-Infused Search
- News publishers are concerned that Google's new AI Overviews feature in search could have a "catastrophic" impact on their traffic and revenue
- By extracting key information into AI-generated summaries, Google Search may reduce the need for users to click through to news websites
- Publishers are grappling with how to respond to the transformative impacts of generative AI technology

## Other AI News
- The U.S. Patent Office issued guidance that AI cannot be listed as an inventor on patents, but AI-assisted inventions are patentable if humans made significant contributions
- The U.K. Supreme Court ruled that AI cannot be named as an inventor there either
- The Department of Homeland Security warned that AI could be exploited by threat actors to interfere with the 2024 U.S. elections via misinformation and manipulated media
- Experts predict scary AI breakthroughs in 2024 like artificial general intelligence (AGI) and real-time deepfakes realistic enough to swing elections
