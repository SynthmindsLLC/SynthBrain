The past couple days have seen significant AI-related developments on the policy, investment, and application fronts:

## AI Policy and Regulation
- Governments in the US, Canada, and EU are taking a more active role in AI regulation. The US has issued executive orders requiring federal agencies to implement AI safeguards, while individual states are passing laws to combat AI discrimination.[1] 
- Canada is working on an AI safety bill and investing $2.4B CAD in public AI computing resources.[1]
- The EU recently passed comprehensive AI regulations.[1]

## AI Infrastructure Investment 
- Tech giants like Amazon, Meta, Microsoft and Alphabet collectively spent over $32B on AI-related capital expenses like data centers and chips in Q1 2024 alone, with plans to further increase investment.[2]
- The spending is likened to "selling shovels" during a gold rush - the biggest profits will come from providing the underlying AI infrastructure.[2]

## Enterprise AI Applications
- SAP is betting big on AI, investing $1.1B in its Business AI unit to bolster revenues with AI-powered automation and insights for customers. The CFO calls AI the "biggest disruption in the technology industry."[3]
- Apple is in talks with OpenAI to use its technology for new AI features coming to the iPhone later this year in iOS 18.[5] 
- Axon, known for law enforcement technology, recently launched a new AI product that could be a game changer for the company.[6]

## Other AI Developments
- Researchers made a breakthrough in nuclear fusion by overcoming hurdles in plasma density and containment, a key step towards fusion power.[4]
- A cancer vaccine that uses mRNA and AI to create personalized treatments for melanoma is showing promise in preventing recurrence in trials.[4]
- AI is enabling new hyperrealistic deepfake videos that are becoming very difficult to distinguish from real footage.[4]

The rapid pace of AI progress is astounding, but also raises important questions around safety, ethics, and societal impact that will need to be addressed through policy. At the same time, the technology is attracting massive investment and starting to power significant enterprise applications. Expect the transformative impact of AI to only accelerate.
