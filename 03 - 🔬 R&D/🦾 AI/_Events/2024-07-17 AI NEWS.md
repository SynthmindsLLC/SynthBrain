Here's a summary of key AI news from July 17, 2024:

## Major AI Developments

- Google released Genkit for Go, an open-source framework for building AI applications using the Go programming language. It provides tools for integrating large language models, vector databases, and other AI capabilities.

- LexisNexis launched Nexis+ AI, an AI-powered research platform for businesses. It uses generative AI to analyze vast amounts of licensed content and generate insights.

- The USPTO issued new guidance on patent eligibility for AI inventions, including examples of eligible and ineligible claims. The guidance aims to clarify how AI-related innovations can be patented.

## AI in Business and Research

- Accenture research found that companies with advanced digital cores and strategic AI investments achieved 60% higher revenue growth rates and 40% higher profits compared to peers.

- ICL and Lavie Bio announced progress in using AI to develop bio-stimulants that can increase crop yields by 5-10% under extreme weather conditions. Field trials are planned for late 2024.

- Quantum Systems is testing AI upgrades to its reconnaissance drones used in Ukraine, enhancing autonomous navigation and image recognition in GPS-denied environments.

## AI Policy and Ethics

- A study found that common benchmarks used to evaluate AI models have significant limitations and may not accurately reflect real-world performance. Experts called for more robust and standardized evaluations.

- Over 40% of Japanese companies surveyed have no plans to adopt AI, citing concerns about workforce impacts, lack of expertise, and reliability issues.

- The popular TV show Big Brother announced an AI-themed season, highlighting AI's growing presence in popular culture and entertainment.

Overall, the news reflects continued rapid advancement and adoption of AI across industries, alongside growing scrutiny of evaluation methods and societal impacts. The developments in areas like agriculture, legal research, and defense underscore AI's expanding real-world applications.
