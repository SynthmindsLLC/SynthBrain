Here are the top AI news stories from May 5, 2024:

- **These 3 Artificial Intelligence (AI) Stocks Could Crush the Market for the Rest of the Year**: Fool.com contributors identified Amazon, UiPath, and Monday.com as three dynamic AI stocks poised to deliver impressive returns in the second half of 2024.[1]

- **Can AI Help Tablets Grow Like PCs and Smartphones in 2024?**: There is growing speculation that integrating AI into tablets could reignite consumer interest and spur innovation in the sluggish tablet market. Apple has entered the AI arena with its own generative AI models for commercial use.[2] 

- **Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg Missing From AI Safety Board**: Meta's chief AI scientist Yann LeCun disclosed that neither Elon Musk nor Mark Zuckerberg were part of the new AI Safety Institute led by the Biden administration.[3]

- **Warren Buffett Warns of AI Risks**: At Berkshire Hathaway's annual meeting, Warren Buffett likened the rise of AI to the development of nuclear weapons, calling it a "genie that scares the hell out of me."[4]

- **Kaiser Permanente Taps Innovaccer for AI**: Kaiser Permanente is implementing Innovaccer's Healthcare AI platform, with Innovaccer's CTO Mike Sutten, who was previously Kaiser's CTO.[5]

- **AI Could Drive Natural Gas Boom**: Natural gas producers anticipate a substantial increase in demand over the next decade, as AI drives a surge in electricity consumption that renewables might struggle to meet alone.[6]

- **AI Brings Holocaust Survivors' Memories to Life**: An exhibition using AI to depict Holocaust survivors' memories launched at the Knesset in Israel for Holocaust Remembrance Day.[7]

- **Stanford AI Leader Building 'Spatial Intelligence' Startup**: Prominent computer scientist Fei-Fei Li is building a startup that uses human-like processing of visual data to make AI systems smarter.[8]
