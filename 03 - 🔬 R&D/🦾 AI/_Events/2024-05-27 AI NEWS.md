## Research Advancements
- Researchers from Microsoft published a paper on using GPT-4 to generate intuitive cluster descriptions for a new AI-based geodemographic classification system of American neighborhoods. [[Segmentation using large language models: A new typology of American neighborhoods]][2]
- A paper on arXiv.org proposes using knowledge graphs and large language models to generate and evaluate interesting research ideas, representing a step towards an "artificial scientific muse". [[Generation and human-expert evaluation of interesting research ideas using knowledge graphs and large language models]][2]

## Industry Developments  
- **Elon Musk's xAI raises $6 billion:** Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company xAI announced it raised $6 billion in funding from investors including Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia Capital and Saudi Prince Alwaleed Bin Talal. The funds will go towards launching initial products and accelerating research. [[Elon Musk's xAI says it has raised $6 billion to develop artificial intelligence]][18]
- **Microsoft unveils AI-powered Windows:** At its Build developer conference, Microsoft announced a new lineup of Windows PCs called "Copilot+ PCs" with generative AI features. It also previewed an AI model called Phi-3-vision that can analyze images and partnered with Khan Academy on AI-powered math tutoring. [[Microsoft wants to make Windows an AI operating system, launches Copilot+ PCs]][12]
- **Google goes all-in on AI at I/O:** Google mentioned "AI" over 120 times during its 2-hour I/O keynote. It unveiled generative AI features across products like Android, Search, Gmail, and a new video model called Google Veo to rival OpenAI's Sora. [[The top AI announcements from Google I/O]][8]
- **Iyo unveils GenAI earbuds:** Startup Iyo announced the Iyo One, premium Bluetooth earbuds with built-in generative AI capabilities, set to launch this winter at $599-699. It aims to succeed where Humane's Ai Pin and Rabbit's R1 handheld AI devices stumbled. [[Iyo thinks its GenAI earbuds can succeed where Humane and Rabbit stumbled]][20]

## Policy Updates
- The U.S. Office of Management and Budget issued guidance to federal agencies on the responsible use of AI, including designating Chief AI Officers, convening AI governance bodies, and inventorying AI use cases by May 27, 2024. [[OMB Issues Guidance to Federal Agencies on the Use of Artificial Intelligence]][6] 
- The U.S. Senate unveiled a bipartisan blueprint to regulate AI, proposing $32 billion in annual AI research funding by 2026 and measures to mitigate risks around security, bias, job displacement and transparency. [[What to make of the new AI Roadmap from the Senate's Bipartisan Commission]][13]
- The EU's ChatGPT taskforce reported preliminary findings on how GDPR data protection rules apply to the chatbot, but key issues like the lawfulness of OpenAI's data processing remain undecided after a year-long investigation. [[EU's ChatGPT taskforce offers first look at detangling the AI chatbot's privacy compliance]][19]
