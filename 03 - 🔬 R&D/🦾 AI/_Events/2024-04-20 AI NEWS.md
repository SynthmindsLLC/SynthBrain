- **Microsoft teases deepfake AI that's too good to release**: Microsoft demoed an AI framework called VASA-1 that can generate realistic videos of people talking from just a still image, audio sample, and text. However, the company is not releasing it publicly due to concerns about potential misuse and erosion of public trust.[1]

- **Women in AI: Ewa Luger explores how AI affects culture — and vice versa**: Ewa Luger, co-director at the Institute of Design Informatics, studies the social, ethical and interactional issues around AI systems. She highlights the under-researched environmental impacts of large language models and the challenges of regulation keeping up with AI innovation.[2]

- **Meta's AI chatbot is in your Instagram and Facebook. Here's how to use it**: Meta has introduced an AI chatbot integrated into the search and messaging features across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. Users can ask it questions, have open-ended conversations, and even use it to generate ideas and images.[3]

- **OpenAI Slams Musk's 'Revisionist History' in Bid to Toss Lawsuit**: OpenAI refutes Elon Musk's claims in his lawsuit that the company has abandoned its altruistic mission in pursuit of profits. OpenAI maintains Musk's allegations are an attempt to bolster his own competing AI ventures.[4]

- **AI is shaking up how sports like rugby, soccer and cricket are played**: Sports teams are increasingly leveraging AI for player scouting, performance analysis, and fan engagement. This is driving up valuations for sports clubs and allowing smaller teams to be more competitive. However, human expertise and leadership remain crucial.[5]
