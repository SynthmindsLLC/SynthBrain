## Major AI Developments

- **Data Scarcity for AI Training**: A study by the MIT-led Data Provenance Initiative found that many important web sources used for training AI models have restricted access to their data over the past year[2]. This emerging "crisis in consent" could impact the development of future AI models.

- **AI in Healthcare**: Healthcare startups are focusing on differentiating themselves and validating their AI solutions with customers early on. There's increasing emphasis on using AI to streamline administrative processes, promote preventive care, and bring pharmacists into patients' care circles[3].

- **AI in Space Exploration**: Researchers propose using large language models to enable communication with potential extraterrestrial civilizations. The idea is to transmit a well-curated AI model that encapsulates human knowledge, allowing aliens to indirectly converse with us[10].

## AI Regulation and Policy

- **State-Level AI Regulation**: With a lack of federal AI laws in the U.S., state legislators are taking the initiative. In the first half of 2024, over 300 AI-related bills were introduced at the state level, with 11 new states enacting AI laws[13].

- **China's AI Oversight**: The Chinese government is reportedly testing AI systems to ensure alignment with socialist values. Tech giants and AI startups are being required to participate in mandatory government reviews of their AI models[6].

## AI and Society

- **Election Concerns**: There are growing worries about AI's potential to manipulate social media and influence the 2024 U.S. presidential election. A survey found that 73% of Americans believe it's likely AI will be used to manipulate social media during the election[7].

- **AI in Creative Industries**: The emergence of AI continues to reshape various sectors, with a notable impact on creative industries such as audiobooks[4].

## AI Applications

- **AI Bartender**: An AI-powered bartender named Sara debuted at a hotel near Disney World, capable of making hundreds of different drinks and engaging in conversation[11].

- **AI in Exoplanet Research**: AI is being used to analyze data from space telescopes, helping to identify potentially habitable exoplanets[10].
