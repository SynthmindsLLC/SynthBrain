## U.S. Clears Way for Antitrust Inquiries of Nvidia, Microsoft and OpenAI[1]
The Justice Department and FTC have agreed to divide responsibility for investigating Microsoft, OpenAI, and Nvidia for potential antitrust violations in the AI industry. This marks escalating regulatory oversight of the rapidly evolving AI technology domain.

## Computex 2024 Highlights: AI Laptops, Wi-Fi 7, and More[2][3]
Computex 2024 in Taipei showcased the latest innovations in computing, including AI laptops powered by new chips from Intel, AMD and Qualcomm, Wi-Fi 7 routers, AR smart glasses, and unique PC designs. The event highlighted the AI revolution taking place in the tech industry.

## The Future of Generative AI: Trends, Challenges, & Breakthroughs[3] 
Key trends shaping the future of generative AI include growth in multimodality, wider adoption of AI-as-a-service models, movement toward AGI, workforce disruption and reformation, increasing regulatory pressures, bigger emphasis on security and governance, and a focus on quality and hallucination management.

## Apple's Big AI News at WWDC Includes an OpenAI Partnership[8]
Reports suggest Apple will unveil a partnership with OpenAI to integrate ChatGPT into iOS 18 at WWDC next week. Apple is also reportedly in discussions with Google about licensing its Gemini chatbot. This marks a significant shift in Apple's AI strategy.

## AI Could Supercharge Disinformation and Disrupt EU Elections[11]
Experts warn that generative AI threatens to supercharge the spread of election disinformation as voters in the EU elect lawmakers for the bloc's parliament. Malicious actors can use AI to create authentic-looking deepfake content aimed at fooling voters. The EU is using new laws to fight back.

## Skunk Works® Demonstrates AI for Air-to-Air Tactical Intercepts[10]
Lockheed Martin Skunk Works partnered with the University of Iowa to successfully demonstrate the use of AI in simulated air-to-air intercept scenarios. The AI agent directly flew a live aircraft and conducted tactical exercises, showing intentional and decisive actions.
