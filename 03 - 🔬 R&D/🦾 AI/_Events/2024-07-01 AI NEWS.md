Several notable artificial intelligence news stories emerged on July 1, 2024:

## Morgan Freeman Calls Out Unauthorized AI Voice Imitation

Actor Morgan Freeman, known for his distinctive voice, called out the unauthorized use of AI to imitate his voice in a TikTok video[1]. The video, posted by an account called 'Justine's Camera Roll', featured an AI-generated voiceover mimicking Freeman in a humorous scenario. Freeman expressed gratitude to fans for identifying and denouncing this misuse of AI.

## UN Adopts China-Backed Resolution on AI for Developing Nations

The United Nations General Assembly adopted a China-led resolution aimed at helping developing nations benefit from artificial intelligence[2]. While not binding, the symbolic resolution sought to enhance AI capacities in poorer countries through global collaboration on AI skills. It urged international organizations to provide support.

## Robinhood Acquires AI Investment Platform Pluto Capital

Trading app Robinhood announced the acquisition of Pluto Capital, an AI-powered investment platform[5]. This move will provide Robinhood users with advanced data analysis, personalized strategies, and portfolio optimization through AI. It reflects a broader trend of tech firms integrating AI capabilities into their services.

## Other AI News

- A man in Montgomery County, Texas was arrested for using AI to alter a teenage girl's photo[4].
- Meta unveiled five major new AI models and research projects, including multi-modal systems, language models, music generation, speech detection, and efforts to improve AI diversity[3].
