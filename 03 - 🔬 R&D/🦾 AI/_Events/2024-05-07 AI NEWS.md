## Survey: AI's Role in News Media Over Next 3-5 Years
- WAN-IFRA conducting survey of news executives on how AI will impact their organizations in the coming years[1]
- Responses will be presented anonymously in upcoming "AI in Action" report[1]

## Microsoft Develops Secure AI for US Intelligence 
- Microsoft has created a generative AI model that operates independently of the internet[2]
- Allows US intelligence agencies to securely analyze classified data using advanced AI[2]
- First major language model to function autonomously without relying on cloud services or external data[2]

## Apple Developing AI Chips for Data Centers
- Project codenamed "ACDC" aims to leverage Apple's chip design expertise for AI in its server infrastructure[3] 
- Chips will likely focus on running AI inference models rather than training, where Nvidia dominates[3]
- Apple has been working with TSMC on the chip design and production[3]

## Cloud Services and AI Driving Increased Energy Usage
- Data center space expanded 26% last year, largely in areas with ample energy capacity like Dallas[4]
- Training AI models consumes huge amounts of electricity as they ingest massive datasets[4] 
- Demand expected to accelerate for years before potentially leveling off once initial build-out is complete[4]

## Deepfakes and AI-Generated Celebrity Images Gaining Traction
- AI-generated images of Katy Perry at Met Gala fooled many, including her own mother, despite inaccuracies[5]
- Fake AI images of celebrities and public figures like the Pope in designer clothing have gone viral[5]
- Experts warn AI deception will gradually shift people's perception of reality over time[5]

## Other AI News
- ILTA Evolve 2024 panel discusses cybersecurity issues, generative AI, and cyber insurance challenges[6]
- New AI marketing tool Typeface generates entire campaigns from messaging to targeting[7]
- Massachusetts lawmakers look to regulate use of AI deepfakes in political ads to prevent voter confusion[8]
