- **Microsoft releases inaugural Responsible AI Transparency Report**: Microsoft published its first annual report detailing its responsible AI practices, learnings, goals and efforts to earn public trust as it brings generative AI products to market.[1]

- **CIOs and CTOs discuss AI bottlenecks at Fortune dinner**: Corporate tech leaders identified regulatory, organizational, strategic and human bottlenecks as the biggest challenges to AI advancement, more so than technical limitations.[2] 

- **SlashNext launches AI service to combat spam and graymail**: SlashNext unveiled an AI-powered service using large language models to accurately identify and filter out spam and unwanted graymail, aiming to boost productivity for users and security teams.[3]

- **Apple expected to report sales decline as investors await AI in iPhones**: Analysts predict Apple will report its biggest quarterly revenue drop in over a year, with iPhone sales down 10%. Focus is on Apple's plans to integrate generative AI in iPhones to revive sales, especially in China.[4]

- **Tesla's next-gen Dojo AI training tile enters production**: Tesla supplier TSMC confirmed that Tesla's next-generation AI training tile for its Dojo supercomputer is now in production, advancing Tesla's AI capabilities.[8]
