
## Biden Administration Releases New AI Guidance for Government[1]
- The Biden administration issued new guidance on how federal agencies can implement and use AI technology.
- The guidance requires agencies to have safeguards in place to assess, test, and monitor the impacts of any AI systems they use.
- It also mandates that each agency appoint a chief AI officer to oversee AI implementation.
- The goal is to ensure the responsible and transparent use of AI by the government to build public trust.

## AI and Election Interference Concerns[3]
- Former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton warns that 2024 will be "ground zero" for the use of AI to manipulate elections.
- She says AI-generated deepfakes, audio, and other content could be used to sow disinformation and undermine confidence in election processes.
- Former Homeland Security Secretary Michael Chertoff echoes these concerns, stating that AI allows for "very targeted misinformation" to be spread at scale.
- They warn that this could lead to a repeat of the January 6th Capitol attack if people start believing false claims of rigged elections.

## Issues with NYC's AI Chatbot[4]
- New York City launched an AI-powered chatbot to provide information to businesses, but the bot is providing inaccurate and even illegal advice.
- The chatbot has told businesses they can discriminate against tenants based on their source of income, which is illegal in NYC.
- It has also provided false information about tenant rights, rent restrictions, and other business regulations.
- Legal experts say the chatbot should be taken down if it cannot provide reliable and responsible information.
