Several news stories on April 24, 2024 highlight the ongoing developments, debates and applications of artificial intelligence (AI) technology:

## The Practical Uses of Generative AI Remain Unclear
- Despite the hype and investment around generative AI like ChatGPT, its practical utility for most people remains questionable, according to tech observers.[1] Every major tech platform shift goes through a period of figuring out its real-world applications.

## AI Ethics Gaining Attention
- The CEO of Cisco met with Pope Francis at the Vatican and signed an AI ethics pledge.[8]
- The Connecticut state senate is debating a bill to establish a regulatory framework for AI to protect consumers and criminalize deepfake pornography.[7]

## AI Showing Promise in Healthcare 
- A study found that AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Bard show promise as patient information sources for glaucoma, though their accuracy, comprehensiveness and readability varied.[6]

## AI Impacting Businesses and Markets
- Tesla's stock surged 12% as Elon Musk tries to position the company to ride the AI wave.[5] 
- Microsoft is making a push into smaller AI systems, saying giant models aren't necessarily what all customers need.[3]
- Meta's stock plunged 16% on weak revenue guidance, despite beating Q1 estimates, as it continues investing in AI.[2]

The news reflects the ongoing tension between the transformative potential of AI and the challenges in translating it to clear real-world benefits, as well as the increasing focus on developing AI responsibly and ethically as it becomes more influential across domains.
