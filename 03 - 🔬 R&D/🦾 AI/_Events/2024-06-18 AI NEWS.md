## Top AI News Stories

- **VCU launches new minors in artificial intelligence and mixed/immersive reality**: Virginia Commonwealth University is introducing two new interdisciplinary minors in fall 2024 focused on practical AI and mixed/immersive reality studies. The programs aim to equip students from all disciplines with skills to leverage AI and metaverse tools.[1]

- **Telefónica Tech and IBM partner to drive AI and analytics solutions for enterprises**: The two companies are collaborating to deploy an open, hybrid, multi-cloud platform specialized in AI, analytics and data management. They will also create a use case office to develop customer pilots and implement training resources.[3]

- **UNESCO warns AI could spread Holocaust misinformation**: UNESCO has cautioned that AI developments may further propagate false information about the Holocaust, either due to flawed AI or intentional creation of misleading content by hate groups. A key concern is AI being used to generate realistic "deepfakes" suggesting the Holocaust did not occur or was exaggerated.[9]

- **Apple Developer Academy introduces AI training for students and alumni**: Apple is rolling out a new core AI curriculum to thousands of students and alumni across its global network of Developer Academies. Courses will cover fundamentals of AI technologies, tools, and ethical considerations to help learners apply AI to their app development.[8]

- **Nvidia becomes world's most valuable company amid AI boom**: Nvidia's stock surge, driven by strong demand for its AI chips, has propelled the company's market cap past $3.3 trillion, edging out Microsoft and Apple. Nvidia now controls about 70% of the AI chip market, drawing attention from regulators.[13]

- **McDonald's ends test of AI drive-thrus with IBM**: McDonald's is concluding its trial run of IBM's AI-powered voice ordering system at select drive-thrus after inconsistent performance. However, the company still believes AI voice ordering will be part of its future and is evaluating other solutions.[15]

## Implications and Analysis

The [[VCU AI and immersive reality minors]] and [[Apple Developer Academy AI curriculum]] point to the growing recognition that AI literacy will be an essential skill across disciplines. Educational institutions are moving quickly to integrate AI into a wide range of programs.

In the business world, the [[Telefónica Tech-IBM partnership]] exemplifies how AI/analytics vendors are teaming up to provide end-to-end enterprise solutions. Meanwhile, [[Nvidia's ascent to world's most valuable company]] underscores the critical role of semiconductor firms in the AI supply chain.

[[UNESCO's warning about Holocaust misinformation]] highlights the ongoing challenge of combating the malicious use of AI to spread disinformation. This will likely spur further debate around AI regulation and safeguards.

Finally, [[McDonald's conclusion of its IBM AI drive-thru test]] shows that the path to real-world AI deployment is not always smooth, especially in customer-facing applications where accuracy is paramount. Expect further trial-and-error as companies explore optimal use cases.
