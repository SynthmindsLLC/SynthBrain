# Summary

## Purpose

The paper presents a framework for developing automated student feedback systems using generative AI responsibly. It considers the ethical issues around consent, impact on learning, evolution of systems over time. The goal is to provide guidance for implementing abundant, scalable feedback while ensuring it benefits all students.  

## Methods

\- Provides technical background on language models and ChatGPT

\- Outlines refinements needed to current models to provide sufficient and factual feedback

\- Applies the RESPACT framework for responsible AI use in education

## Key Findings

1. Generative AI has the potential to provide unlimited, instant feedback to students at scale
2. Risks include devaluing feedback, failing to support struggling students, lack of transparency
3. Explicit ethical guidelines and oversight critical when developing these systems  
4. Feedback quality depends heavily on training data and continued model updating

## Discussion 

The paper makes a strong case for the need to consider both opportunities and ethical risks as we implement generative AI. It rightly points out that while these tools could greatly increase access to feedback, they may fail to meet the needs of all students or evolve responsibly without deliberate planning and oversight.

## Critiques  

1. More clarity needed on exactly how models can be made transparent and accountable to users
2. Should further emphasize the critical role of human educators in the initial development and continuous updating of AI systems
3. Discussion of consent could be expanded - options for differentiated consent
