Sam Harris is an American philosopher, neuroscientist, author, and podcast host. Some key facts about him:

- One of the "Four Horsemen" of [[New Atheism]], known for his outspoken criticism of religion, especially [[Islam]][1][6] 
- Has written several bestselling books on topics like rationality, ethics, free will, spirituality, and AI, including "[[The End of Faith]]", "[[Waking Up]]", and "[[The Moral Landscape]]"[2][6]
- Hosts the popular "[[Making Sense]]" podcast, which explores important questions related to the human mind, society, and current events[3] 
- Practices and teaches [[meditation]] and is interested in exploring spirituality without religion[4][6]
- Has attracted controversy for some of his views on sensitive topics like race, genetics, and Islam, with critics accusing him of [[Islamophobia]][6][9]
- Identifies as a [[liberal]] but has criticized "woke" politics and what he sees as threats to [[free speech]][6][9]

Overall, Sam Harris is an influential and often polarizing public intellectual who fearlessly explores difficult questions at the intersection of science, philosophy, politics and spirituality. While his provocative takes generate backlash, he aims to have important conversations to help make sense of the human condition.
