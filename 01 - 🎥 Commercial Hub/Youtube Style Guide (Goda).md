# IDEAS
Ideas should follow BENS framework: Big (significant, impactful topics), Easy/Useful
(accessible, practical content), New (latest trends and information), and
Safe/Credibility (establishing authority and trustworthiness). We should aim that our
idea has all 4 elements from the BENS framework and should have no less than 2. 
BENS framework [example]: 
Idea: The GPT store just launched, and 3 million custom GPTs have already been
created. The genius behind the top-ranking GPT on GPT store is giving it away for
free. 
BENS framework: 
Big (&quot;3 million custom GPTs have already been created&quot;
Easy/Useful (&quot;giving it away for free&quot;)
New (&quot;GPT store just launched&quot;)
Safe/Credibility (&quot;The genius behind the top-ranking GPT&quot;)
BENS framework [example]: 
Idea: Prompt Engineering is a new valuable skill when working with AI that already

online and for free

# THUMBNAILS
- Use vibrant colors, bold text, or visually striking elements to stand out.
- Ensure that the thumbnail accurately represents the tone and content of your video.
- Use clear, sharp, and high-resolution images for your thumbnails.
- Play around with different compositions to create visually appealing thumbnails.
- Incorporate concise and compelling text that sparks curiosity and encourages
viewers to click on your video.
- Incorporate contrasting colors, such as red and blue, to make the thumbnail visually
striking and appealing.
- Show the YouTuber or subject in the thumbnail engaged in some kind of action or
activity to pique viewers&#39; curiosity and make them more likely to click on the video.

- Aim for simplicity in your thumbnail design.
- Pay attention to the composition of the thumbnail. Utilize elements like trees or
other objects to provide scale and direct the viewer&#39;s attention towards the main
subject or focal point of the image.
- Create thumbnails that challenge viewers&#39; beliefs or present unexpected
information. This can create a sense of intrigue and the need to click to confirm or
explore further.
- Ensure that your thumbnail prominently showcases the main subject or focus of
your video.
- Use high-quality images or graphics with vibrant colors to grab the viewer&#39;s
attention and make them curious about your video.
- While it&#39;s important to include relevant text, keep it minimal and easy to read.
- Give viewers a reason to click on your video by creating intrigue and curiosity in
your thumbnail.
