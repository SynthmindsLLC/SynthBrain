## Metadata
- Author: [[Gary Marcus]]
- Date: 2024-03-05
- Full Title: Covert Racism in LLMs
- Category: #articles
- Summary: A new paper discusses the shocking findings of covert racism in Large Language Models (LLMs), highlighting the distinction between overt and covert racism in these systems. The study reveals that while overt measures in LLMs seemed fine, covert racism was a significant issue, impacting queries related to consequential matters. The potential implications of this covert racism, especially considering the widespread use of LLMs, are deemed massive and concerning, prompting calls for further investigation and accountability from LLM manufacturers and regulatory bodies. The study suggests that addressing this issue is complex, as even human feedback and guardrails have not proven effective in mitigating covert racism in LLMs.
