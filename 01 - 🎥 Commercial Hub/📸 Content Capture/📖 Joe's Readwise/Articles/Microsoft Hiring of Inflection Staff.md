# Microsoft Hiring of Inflection Staff Came Amid Lack of Consumer AI Vision, and Board Concerns About OpenAI, Insiders Say

- Category: #articles
- Summary: Microsoft's hiring of Inflection staff aimed to boost its consumer AI strategy amid concerns about OpenAI from the company's board. The move was prompted by doubts regarding Microsoft's consumer AI vision and strategy, leading to the creation of a new Microsoft AI organization led by Inflection CEO Mustafa Suleyman. Suleyman's focus on consumer AI products like Copilot AI chatbot and Bing search engine aligns with Microsoft's efforts to challenge Google in online search. While some insiders expressed concerns about the hiring, Microsoft emphasized its continued partnership with OpenAI despite recent board concerns and highlighted a shift towards diversifying AI models and reducing reliance on OpenAI for consumer-focused projects.
