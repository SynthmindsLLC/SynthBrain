# Ridiculed Stable Diffusion 3 release excels at AI-generated body horror

![rw-book-cover](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/sd3_body_horror_1-760x380.jpg)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Benj Edwards]]
- Date: 2024-06-12
- Full Title: Ridiculed Stable Diffusion 3 release excels at AI-generated body horror
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Stability AI's release of Stable Diffusion 3 Medium, an AI image-synthesis model, has faced criticism for generating anatomically incorrect and unsettling images of humans. Users on Reddit have expressed disappointment in the model's depiction of human anatomy, particularly in rendering hands and limbs. The company's decision to filter out adult content from the training data has been blamed for the model's shortcomings in creating realistic human images.
- URL: https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2024/06/ridiculed-stable-diffusion-3-release-excels-at-ai-generated-body-horror/

## Highlights
- On Wednesday, Stability AI released weights for [Stable Diffusion 3 Medium](https://stability.ai/news/stable-diffusion-3-medium), an AI image-synthesis model that turns text prompts into AI-generated images. Its arrival has been ridiculed online, however, because it generate images of humans in a way that seems like a step backward from other state-of-the-art image-synthesis models like [Midjourney](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2024/01/a-crazy-update-midjourney-v6-upgrade-heaps-on-ai-generated-detail/) or [DALL-E 3](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2023/11/from-toy-to-tool-dall-e-3-is-a-wake-up-call-for-visual-artists-and-the-rest-of-us/). As a result, it can churn out wild anatomically incorrect visual abominations with ease. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kjjb3w5pbysahz7qdbcha))
- Hands have traditionally been a challenge for AI image generators due to lack of good examples in early training data sets, but more recently, several image-synthesis models [seemed to have overcome the issue](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2023/03/ai-imager-midjourney-v5-stuns-with-photorealistic-images-and-5-fingered-hands/). In that sense, SD3 appears to be a huge step backward for the image synthesis enthusiasts that gather on Reddit—especially compared to recent Stability releases like [SD XL Turbo](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2023/11/stable-diffusion-turbo-xl-accelerates-image-synthesis-with-one-step-generation/) in November. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kkj4ysz3htjbm5q59tc8p))
- AI image fans are so far blaming the Stable Diffusion 3's anatomy fails on Stability's insistence on filtering out adult content (often called "NSFW" content) from the SD3 training data that teaches the model how to generate images. "Believe it or not, heavily censoring a model also gets rid of human anatomy, so... that's what happened," [wrote](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1de7lbg/comment/l8ae1gp/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button) one Reddit user in the thread. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kmvbfrhvf5cq7ra95wvth))
- The release of [Stable Diffusion 2.0](https://stability.ai/news/stable-diffusion-v2-release) in 2023 suffered from similar problems in depicting humans accurately, and AI researchers soon discovered that censoring adult content that contains nudity also [severely hampers](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/stable-diffusion-1-vs-2-what-you-need-to-know/) an AI model's ability to generate accurate human anatomy. At the time, Stability AI reversed course with SD 2.1 and [SD XL](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2023/07/stable-diffusion-xl-puts-ai-generated-visual-worlds-at-your-gpus-command/), regaining some abilities lost by excluding NSFW content. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kne3zhbyc2gwz1znq3xfy))
- Stability first [announced](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2024/02/stability-announces-stable-diffusion-3-a-next-gen-ai-image-generator/) Stable Diffusion 3 in February, and the company has planned to make it available in a variety of different model sizes. Today's release is for the "Medium" version, which is a 2 billion-parameter model. In addition to the weights being [available on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-3-medium), they are also available for experimentation through the company's [Stability Platform](https://platform.stability.ai/). The weights are available for download and use for free under a [non-commercial license](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-3-medium/blob/main/LICENSE) only. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kq2xv75yd50y0vv3ypa2x))
- Stability AI as a company fell into a tailspin recently with the [resignation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-03-26/stability-ai-ceo-emad-mostaque-resignation-what-happened) of its founder and CEO, Emad Mostaque, in March and then a series of [layoffs](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/04/18/ai-startup-stability-lays-off-10percent-of-employees-after-ceo-exit.html). Just prior to that, three key engineers—Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, and Dominik Lorenz—[left the company](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/03/20/key-stable-diffusion-researchers-leave-stability-ai-as-company-flounders/). And its troubles go back even farther, with news of the company's dire financial position [lingering](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-08-08/stability-ai-s-lead-threatened-by-departures-concerns-over-ceo) since 2023. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j07kqtq78g0g0vcsf3v5qz6q))
