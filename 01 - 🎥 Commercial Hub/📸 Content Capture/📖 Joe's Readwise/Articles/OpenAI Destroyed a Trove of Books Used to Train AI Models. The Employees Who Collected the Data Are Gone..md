## Highlights
- Newly unsealed documents in the class action lawsuit brought by the Authors Guild against [OpenAI](https://www.businessinsider.com/openai-launch-better-gpt-5-chatbot-2024-3) reveal that the startup deleted two huge datasets, named "books1" and "books2," that had been used to train its GPT-3 AI model. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxawgy88hpphwy8z9yrt44bh))
- For months the Guild has been seeking information from OpenAI about the datasets. The company initially resisted, citing confidentiality concerns, before ultimately revealing that it had deleted all copies of the data, according to the legal filings reviewed by Business Insider. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxawhgaex1h0qwqjqsnrpygd))
- The letter goes on to say that none of the other data used to train GPT-3 has been deleted and offered attorneys for the Authors Guild access to those other datasets. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxawm8nyn210dhkgb4zc85za))
