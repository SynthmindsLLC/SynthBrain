## Highlights
- Over the last few days, you probably saw a bunch of enthusiastic news reports and tweets about a new Google Deepmind paper on math, like [this](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2023/dec/14/ai-scientists-make-exciting-discovery-using-chatbots-to-solve-maths-problems) at *The Guardian* ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26v98kw8g5bzygyddg1qts))
- Google DeepMind, along with the mathematician Jordan Ellenberg really did use some AI to help solve a math problem, in [a very clever paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6) that is worth reading, on how to use “program search with large language models” (as the accurate and not at all hypey title explains). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26vs48hq3rqj6jjjwwbqpz))
- But then Google DeepMind’s PR team [oversold the paper](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/funsearch-making-new-discoveries-in-mathematical-sciences-using-large-language-models/), claiming e.g., that they had “solved a notoriously hard challenge in computing” and ending with this exuberant but immensely speculative set of claims: ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26w3ztyjgbhvdtabx7hyb2))
- An LLM didn’t solve the math problem on its own; the LLM was used in a very narrow, prescribed way inside of larger system. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26wk8y5f997evgtbnbqsmm))
- Human mathematicians had to write problem-specific code for each separate mathematical problem. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26wwahsfe9fv2z4qq72988))
- • There is no evidence that the problem was heretofore “unsolvable” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26x2rq3c1d1pczk11n1tch))
- NYU computer scientist Ernest Davis has just written [a terrific new paper about all this](https://cs.nyu.edu/~davise/papers/FunSearch.pdf), going into depth. Highly recommended. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj26xt6vrvt6t7bby013dtm4))
