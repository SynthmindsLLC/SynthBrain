# Stability AI CEO resigns because you’re ‘not going to beat centralized AI with more centralized AI’

- Full Title: Stability AI CEO resigns because you’re ‘not going to beat centralized AI with more centralized AI’
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Stability AI's CEO, Emad Mostaque, has resigned to pursue decentralized AI, stating that centralized AI cannot be defeated by more of the same. Mostaque's departure has led to interim co-CEOs taking over at Stability AI. The company, known for its Stable Diffusion image generation tool, faced financial struggles, failed fundraising attempts, and a shift in focus towards revenue growth. This move comes amidst a broader trend in the AI industry, with other startups like Inflection AI also experiencing significant changes.
