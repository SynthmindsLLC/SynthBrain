- Summary: Gary Marcus highlights how deep learning has faced ridicule and failure in addressing AI's fundamental issues over the past 26 months. Despite significant investment and claims of progress, reliability remains elusive, and the alignment problem persists without a clear solution. Marcus also points out Elon Musk's shift from caution to hype regarding AI technology, emphasizing the ongoing challenges and dangers of unaligned AGI.
