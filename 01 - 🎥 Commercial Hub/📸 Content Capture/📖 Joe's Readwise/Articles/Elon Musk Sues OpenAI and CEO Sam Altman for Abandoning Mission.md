- Summary: Elon Musk has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman, claiming they veered away from the original mission of developing AI for the benefit of humanity instead of profit. Initially envisioned as an open-source, non-profit company, OpenAI's focus on profitability allegedly breached their contract with Musk. The lawsuit also mentions the secrecy surrounding GPT-4, OpenAI's advanced AI model. Despite requests for comment, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Musk have not responded. Musk, who co-founded OpenAI in 2015, stepped down in 2018 and remains involved in various ventures like Tesla, SpaceX, and Twitter.
