## Highlights
- stealing other people’s content and repackaging it without linking to the original source. That’s it. That’s the **only** strategy that can reliably build large audiences from scratch on websites with high content demands. Meaning that, on many websites, especially on social media, there are basically only two types of very large accounts. First, people who have a good reason to already be famous (e.g., pop stars, well-known academics, politicians). Second, those unbothered by what might be called the consistent “minor theft” of ideas, images, or links, usually in a way that starts innocently but grows bolder over time as they build their nest of stolen things.
