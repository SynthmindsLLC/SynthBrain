# Leaked OpenAI Documents Show Sam Altman Was Clearly Aware of Silencing Former Employees

![rw-book-cover](https://wp-assets.futurism.com/2024/05/heknew.jpg)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Maggie Harrison Dupré]]
- Date: 2024-05-23
- Full Title: Leaked OpenAI Documents Show Sam Altman Was Clearly Aware of Silencing Former Employees
- Category: #articles
- Summary: OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, faces criticism for pressuring employees into signing restrictive agreements. Despite claiming ignorance, evidence suggests Altman was aware of clauses allowing the company to claw back equity from former employees. The controversy raises concerns about OpenAI's credibility and treatment of departing staff.
- URL: https://futurism.com/sam-altman-silencing-former-employees

## Highlights
- [according to *Vox's* latest follow-up](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/351132/openai-vested-equity-nda-sam-altman-documents-employees), Altman wasn't in the dark about the equity clauses, as he claimed in his tail-between-legs tweet. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyv7v14bzss9jnv5tz3kkpjg))
- Documentation reviewed by *Vox* reveals that several company leaders — including OpenAI chief strategy officer Jason Kwon, who reportedly told staffers following the initial *Vox* report that OpenAI leadership "caught" the provision "~month ago" — signed documents that plainly outlined the stifling clawback provision. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyv7vehavgeynh4jmerfkrf6))
- unless someone forged the CEO's signature, he gave express permission for these clauses to exist. Outside of forgery, there are only two plausible reasons for Altman's alleged lack of knowledge: either he didn't fully read the employment contracts he was signing or he was lying. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyv7w7tfercs3cdwh15t5n7m))
- According to the *Vox* report, the equity provisions were no secret to OpenAI representatives handling departures, who in some cases gave outgoing employees just seven days to make the incredibly complicated decision about their future — all the while, in instances reviewed by *Vox*, emphasizing possible clawbacks. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyv7wv1jpgzkmazmjdmhr79y))
- Last year, when Altman was briefly forced out of OpenAI in what was pretty much a corporate coup, those who voted to oust the CEO — many of whom departed after losing said coup — claimed that Altman had been "inconsistently candid" in his communications with the board. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyv7yaj2ea5kgc0k10t42j8z))
- Meanwhile, [recent departures](https://futurism.com/openai-safety-worker-quit-confidence-agi) have [ground](https://futurism.com/the-byte/openai-researcher-quits-criticism-superallignment) OpenAI's "Superalignment" safety team — the ones tasked with making sure a killer AI doesn't obliterate humankind — [into dust](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/17/openai-superalignment-sutskever-leike.html).
