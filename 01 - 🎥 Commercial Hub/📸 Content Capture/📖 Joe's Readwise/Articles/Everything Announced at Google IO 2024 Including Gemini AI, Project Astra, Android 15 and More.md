## Highlights
- Google [announced](https://www.engadget.com/googles-new-gemini-15-flash-ai-model-is-lighter-than-gemini-pro-and-more-accessible-172353657.html) a brand new AI model called Gemini 1.5 Flash, which it says is optimised for speed and efficiency. Flash sits between Gemini 1.5 Pro and Gemini 1.5 Nano, which its the company’s smallest model that runs locally on device. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvv92bx0xbvk81fjmnvmn8))
- Google will double Gemini’s context window to two million tokens, which means that it will be able to process two hours of video, 22 hours of audio, more than 60,000 lines of code or more than 1.4 million words at the same time. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvvp3d7w4036vp1318fmga))
- Google [showed off](https://www.engadget.com/googles-project-astra-uses-your-phones-camera-and-ai-to-find-noise-makers-misplaced-items-and-more-172642329.html) Project Astra, an early version of a universal assistant powered by AI that Google’s DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis said was Google’s version of an AI agent “that can be helpful in everyday life.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvw0dmp5e0pbte16765hk7))
- Astra user moves around Google’s London office holding up their phone and pointing the camera at various things — a speaker, some code on a whiteboard, and out a window — and has a natural conversation with the app about what it seems. In one of the video’s most impressive moments, the correctly tells the user where she left her glasses before without the user ever having brought up the glasses. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvwgtnfjxtv3qsebzfyfgp))
- The video ends with a twist — when the user finds and wears the missing glasses, we learn that they have an [onboard camera system](https://www.engadget.com/google-just-snuck-a-pair-of-ar-glasses-into-a-project-astra-demo-at-io-172824539.html) and are capable of using Project Astra to seamlessly carry on a conversation with the user, perhaps indicating that Google might be working on a competitor to Meta’s Ray Ban smart glasses. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvww60ks0h4bbs66tbm17d))
- Google’s new AI-powered media creation engines are called Veo and Imagen 3. Veo is Google’s answer to OpenAI’s Sora. It can produce “high-quality” 1080p videos that can last “beyond a minute”, Google said, and can understand cinematic concepts like a timelapse. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvxpr60sbpfxhrw6b3g135))
- Imagen 3, meanwhile, is a text-to-image generator that Google claims handles text better than its previous version, Imagen 2. The result is the company’s highest quality” text-to-image model with “incredible level of detail” for “photorealistic, lifelike images” and fewer artifacts — essentially pitting it against OpenAI’s DALLE-3. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvy1esj1jex2xshp037yjk))
- AI Overviews and which the company has been testing for a year now, is finally rolling out to millions of people in the US. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvyn8pzf0h9ye0hmwmm72z))
- Google is [integrating Gemini](https://www.engadget.com/google-builds-gemini-right-into-android-adding-contextual-awareness-within-apps-180413356.html) directly into Android. When Android 15 releases later this year, Gemini will be aware of the app, image or video that you’re running, and you’ll be able to pull it up as an overlay and ask it context-specific questions. Where does that leave Google Assistant that already does this? Who knows! Google didn’t bring it up at all during today’s keynote. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvz5m866qwqh6rk1vktq7m))
- Google said it would add [digital watermarks](https://www.engadget.com/google-expands-digital-watermarks-to-ai-made-video-175232320.html) to AI-generated video and text, [make Gemini accessible](https://www.engadget.com/gemini-will-be-accessible-in-the-side-panel-on-google-apps-like-gmail-and-docs-185406695.html) in the side panel in Gmail and Docs, power a [virtual AI teammate](https://www.engadget.com/google-gemini-can-power-a-virtual-ai-teammate-with-its-own-workspace-account-182809274.html) in Workspace, listen in on phone calls and [detect if you’re being scammed](https://www.engadget.com/google-announces-new-scam-detection-tools-that-provide-real-time-alerts-during-phone-calls-181442091.html) in real time, and a lot more. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwvzg9pp67mqdwgxq28z20h))
