# Sam Altman Clearly Freaked Out by Reaction to News of OpenAI Silencing Former Employees

## Metadata
- Author: [[Futurism]]
- Date: 2024-05-19
- Full Title: Sam Altman Clearly Freaked Out by Reaction to News of OpenAI Silencing Former Employees
- Category: #articles
- Summary: OpenAI faced backlash after comparing its new ChatGPT 4o AI to the movie "Her" and dealing with high-profile departures. CEO Sam Altman admitted embarrassment and is working on damage control, focusing on equity issues and addressing concerns about silencing former employees. The company's leadership is under scrutiny for their handling of critical feedback and nondisclosure agreements.
- URL: https://futurism.com/the-byte/sam-altman-nda-superintelligent

## Highlights
- CEO Sam Altman made an unforced error by [comparing the talking AI](https://futurism.com/the-byte/sam-altman-chatgpt-her) to the dark 2013 romance film "Her," kicking off a wave of negative headlines. Then things got even more tumultuous when a [pair](https://futurism.com/the-byte/ilya-sutskever-leaves-openai) of [high-powered researchers](https://futurism.com/the-byte/openai-researcher-quits-criticism-superallignment) left the company in departures that looked like something between "getting pushed out" and "quitting in disgust." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyarzy1yxce9qytbr5e0netp))
- [*Vox* reported](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/2024/5/17/24158478/openai-departures-sam-altman-employees-chatgpt-release) that any workers leaving OpenAI had to sign a draconian nondisclosure agreement to retain their equity stipulating, among other things, that they could never criticize the company in the future. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyas0mpv9m256dzg54ppd09f))
- Altman, as usual, is [taking center stage](https://futurism.com/the-byte/sam-altman-fame) in the company's response — though so far he's seemed to strategically focus on the equity side of the equation rather than the explosive claim that OpenAI is silencing former employees who might have ethical concerns about its work. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyas16xzse78p25nzv050jvs))
- "We have never clawed back anyone's vested equity, nor will we do that if people do not sign a separation agreement (or don't agree to a non-disparagement agreement). vested equity is vested equity, full stop," [he wrote on X-formerly-Twitter](https://x.com/sama/status/1791936857594581428). "There was a provision about potential equity cancellation in our previous exit docs; although we never clawed anything back, it should never have been something we had in any documents or communication." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyas1ve9aq4x9cxx9nf9fjg6))
- "This is on me and one of the few times I've been genuinely embarrassed running OpenAI; I did not know this was happening and I should have," he continued. "The team was already in the process of fixing the standard exit paperwork over the past month or so. if any former employee who signed one of those old agreements is worried about it, they can contact me and we'll fix that too. Very sorry about this." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyas2atapd1c027d2cdx27br))
