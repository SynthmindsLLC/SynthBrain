## Highlights
- Two of OpenAI's founders, CEO Sam Altman and President Greg Brockman, are on the defensive after a shake-up in the company's safety department this week. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81dtarqm905at00w7ah2g7))
- The [company's chief scientist, Ilya Sutskever](https://www.businessinsider.com/ilya-sutskever-leaving-openai-6-months-after-failed-altman-ouster-2024-5), who is also a founder, announced on X that he was leaving on Tuesday. Hours later, his colleague, Jan Leike, followed suit. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81e1styzyfhny81tp6axa2))
- Sutskever and Leike led OpenAI's super alignment team, which was focused on developing AI systems compatible with human interests. That sometimes placed them in opposition with members of the company's leadership who advocated for more aggressive development. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81ebr7pp9s0t0hzeb4xg7t))
- "I have been disagreeing with OpenAI leadership about the company's core priorities for quite some time, until we finally reached a breaking point," Leike [wrote](https://x.com/janleike/status/1791498174659715494) on X on Friday. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81emb0s2d3gbhee8tqkj6w))
- Sutskever was [among the six board members](https://www.businessinsider.com/meet-openai-cofounder-ilya-sutskever-supported-regretted-sam-altman-firing-2023-11) who tried to oust Altman as CEO in November, though he later said he regretted the move. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81ev4yx620vf1ge70za0st))
- as public concern continued to mount, Brockman offered more details on Saturday about how OpenAI will approach safety and risk moving forward — especially as it develops [artificial general intelligence](https://www.businessinsider.com/what-is-agi-artificial-general-intelligence-explained-2023-5) and builds AI systems that are more sophisticated than chatbots. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81fesjmy42fprpnf9jge97))
- "We've repeatedly demonstrated the incredible possibilities from scaling up deep learning and analyzed their implications; called for international governance of AGI before such calls were popular; and helped pioneer the science of assessing AI systems for catastrophic risks," Brockman wrote. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81ghc6sx21ph0v8j1ytpck))
- Altman recently said the best way to regulate AI would be an [international agency](https://www.businessinsider.com/sam-altman-openai-artificial-intelligence-regulation-international-agency-2024-5) that ensures reasonable safety testing but also expressed wariness of regulation by government lawmakers who may not fully understand the technology. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81gtcyr610va13wmgz0jpe))
- Brockman said OpenAI has also established the foundations for safely deploying AI systems more capable than GPT-4. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81h3jnbkamnh0er1j7fam1))
- "These problems are quite hard to get right, and I am concerned we aren't on a trajectory to get there," Leike said. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hy81j3dwttxwg681vf5wde9b))
