- Summary: In an open letter to Sam Altman, the author raises questions about Altman's defense of his $7 trillion fundraising ask. The author questions Altman's belief that LLMs (language models) will secure our collective future and asks if Altman is working on any other technologies. They also express concerns about the potential negative impacts of investing $7 trillion into LLMs and their infrastructure, such as harming content creators, women, the environment, and democracy, as well as destroying jobs. The author urges caution and raises doubts about the certainty of this bet and the rush to make it.
