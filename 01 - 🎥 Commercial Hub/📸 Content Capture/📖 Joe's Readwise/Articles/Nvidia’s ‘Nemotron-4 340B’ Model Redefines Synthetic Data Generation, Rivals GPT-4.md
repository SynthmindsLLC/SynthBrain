## Highlights
- [Nvidia](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/) has once again solidified its position as the undisputed leader in AI innovation with the release of “[Nemotron-4 340B](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-4-synthetic-data-generation-llm-training/),” a groundbreaking family of open models that is set to revolutionize the generation of synthetic data for training large language models (LLMs). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0dxmd35r5f57fmwx89j7ccw))
- The model, which had been operating under the mysterious alias “[june-chatbot](https://twitter.com/lmsysorg/status/1801682893988892716)” on [LMSys.org Chatbot Arena](https://chat.lmsys.org/) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0dxmq4atv5pedaaycd9wfzy))
- The Nemotron-4 340B family, which includes [base](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-4-340B-Base), [instruct](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-4-340B-Instruct), and [reward](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-4-340B-Reward) models, forms a comprehensive pipeline for generating high-quality synthetic data. With an astonishing 9 trillion tokens used in training, a 4,000 context window, and support for over 50 natural languages and 40 programming languages, Nemotron-4 340B outshines its competitors, including Mistral’s [Mixtral-8x22B](https://mistral.ai/news/mixtral-8x22b/), Anthropic’s [Claude-Sonnet](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-family), Meta’s [Llama3-70B](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-70B), [Qwen-2](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2/), and even rivals the performance of GPT-4. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0dxnbawk02sgbaamayw233h))
- One of the most notable aspects of Nemotron-4 340B is its commercially-friendly licensing. [Somshubra Majumdar](https://twitter.com/HaseoX94), a Senior Deep Learning Research Engineer, emphasized this point in a post on X.com, stating, “The license is commercially viable. Yeah, you can use this to generate all the data you want.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0dxnt7da1bb6gwdet10709t))
