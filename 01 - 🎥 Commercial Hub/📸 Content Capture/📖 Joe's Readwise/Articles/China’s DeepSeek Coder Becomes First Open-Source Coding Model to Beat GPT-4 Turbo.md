- Summary: DeepSeek Coder V2, an open-source coding model from Chinese startup DeepSeek, outperforms closed-source models like GPT-4 Turbo in coding and math tasks. It supports over 300 programming languages and excels in general reasoning and language capabilities. The model is available under a MIT license for research and commercial use, offering different parameter options for diverse computing needs.

## Highlights
- Chinese AI startup [DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com/), which previously made headlines with a [ChatGPT competitor](https://venturebeat.com/ai/meet-deepseek-chat-chinas-latest-chatgpt-rival-with-a-67b-model/) trained on 2 trillion English and Chinese tokens, has announced the release of DeepSeek Coder V2, an open-source mixture of experts (MoE) code language model. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0qw5c7z07sqa61m3sjtmhbe))
- outperforms state-of-the-art closed-source models, including GPT-4 Turbo, Claude 3 Opus and Gemini 1.5 Pro. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0qw5xtw976k65wvpgmfrwz4))
- The new V2 offering builds on that work, expanding language support to 338 and context window to 128K – enabling it to handle more complex and extensive coding tasks. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0qw75z21yd1xtqfjkgsnfg1))
- The only model that managed to outperform DeepSeek’s offering across multiple benchmarks was [GPT-4o](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-announces-new-free-model-gpt-4o-and-chatgpt-for-desktop/), which obtained marginally higher scores in HumanEval, LiveCode Bench, MATH and GSM8K. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0qw7wwqs8g34r176s4yhcs6))
- As of now, DeepSeek Coder V2 is being offered under a [MIT license](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-Coder-V2/blob/main/LICENSE-CODE), which allows for both research and unrestricted commercial use. Users can download both 16B and 236B sizes in instruct and base avatars via [Hugging Face](https://t.co/m703gzSMXY). Alternatively, the company is also providing access to the models via API through its [platform](https://platform.deepseek.com/) under a pay-as-you-go model. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j0qw9y6ht71jm6cgs0nsb439))
