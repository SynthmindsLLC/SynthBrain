- If you opened Facebook, Twitter or Instagram about a decade ago, you'd likely see posts from friends and family, in chronological order. Nowadays, users are hit with a barrage of content curated by an algorithm. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmczkeh4g1jfdrhfa4afy5ft))
- In his new book, [*Filterworld*,](https://www.npr.org/2024/01/16/1225002436/book-review-kyle-chayka-filterworld) Chayka examines the algorithmic recommendations that dictate everything from the music, news and movies we consume, to the foods we eat and the places we go. He argues that all of this machine-guided curation has made us docile consumers and flattened our likes and tastes. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmczm7s3ybeks091jv6g7z9j))
