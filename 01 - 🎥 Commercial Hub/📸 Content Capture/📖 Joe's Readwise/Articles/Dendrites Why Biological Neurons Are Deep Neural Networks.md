- Category: #articles
- Summary: Dendrites are key components of biological neurons and play a crucial role in neural computations. While early neural networks were inspired by the description of biological neurons, it is now clear that dendrites are much more powerful and complex than previously thought. Dendrites possess voltage-gated ion channels, including sodium channels and NMDA receptors, which enable them to generate action potentials and perform non-linear integration of incoming information. This computational complexity allows dendrites to discriminate the order and velocity of incoming action potentials, process temporal patterns, and generate sequence-selective outputs. Recent research has even discovered a new type of electrical response called dendritic calcium action potentials, which are highly selective to specific input strengths. Understanding the computational capabilities of dendrites is essential for better understanding the power and sophistication of biological neurons.
