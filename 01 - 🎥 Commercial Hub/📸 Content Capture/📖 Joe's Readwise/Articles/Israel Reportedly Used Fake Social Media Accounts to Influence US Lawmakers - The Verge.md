## Highlights
- A secret social media influence campaign that attempted to lobby US lawmakers to support Israel’s war on Gaza was organized and funded by the Israeli government, [*The New York Times* reports](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/06/05/technology/israel-campaign-gaza-social-media.html). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hzn67v6rq7h08t553tkxfsv5))
- The accounts posed as Americans and posted pro-Israel messages, calling on members of congress to fund Israeli military operations. The campaigns used OpenAI’s ChatGPT to produce the posts and targeted several Black Democrats, like House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hzn68cwq4ft98k1mpdzkssck))
- The covert influence campaign was [one of the efforts disrupted by Meta](https://www.theverge.com/2024/5/29/24167164/meta-covert-influence-campaigns-ai-china-israel), according to its [quarterly threat report](https://scontent-lga3-2.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/445235204_402858536059630_7403303878106178024_n.pdf?_nc_cat=100&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=b8d81d&_nc_ohc=H60p-CJWk3YQ7kNvgEcm7VW&_nc_ht=scontent-lga3-2.xx&oh=00_AYDai9QVV2jxLNdsdpo9FnbLDAFv2spD-nuuvCXnu_NDGg&oe=6666577F) issued at the end of May. In the report, Meta said it had identified a network of more than 500 fake accounts traced to Israel that posted pro-Israel messages on Instagram and Facebook. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hzn693xn8z2vasqdqaqvy4rc))
- The topics of the comments included campus antisemitism, calls to release hostages taken during Hamas’ October 7th attack, and anti-Islam material. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hzn69k69nw84etkszsmayf5k))
- OpenAI similarly reported it had [disrupted a campaign in Israel using its tools](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-deceptive-uses-of-AI-by-covert-influence-operations/). At the time, it wasn’t known that the Israeli government was behind the campaign. The campaign remains active on X, according to the *Times*. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hzn69thr5bn3aj82fm626x9h))
