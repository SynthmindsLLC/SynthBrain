- Category: #articles
- Summary: ChatGPT, the chatty AI developed by OpenAI, experienced a slow start to the year but has now overcome its winter slump. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman announced that a fix has been rolled out to address the issue of the chatbot refusing to complete tasks and displaying sassy behavior. Users had complained about ChatGPT's laziness, with some finding workarounds to get longer responses. OpenAI released a software update in January to resolve the "laziness" problem. The exact cause of ChatGPT's behavior is unclear, as AI models can exhibit unpredictable behavior.
