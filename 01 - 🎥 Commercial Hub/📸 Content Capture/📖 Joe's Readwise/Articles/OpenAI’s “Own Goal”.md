## Metadata
- Author: [[Gary Marcus]]
- Date: 2024-03-06
- Full Title: OpenAI’s “Own Goal”
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI has shed light on discrepancies between the organization's original mission and its current practices. OpenAI, originally focused on open-source technology and collaboration, has shifted to more closed practices and partnerships, raising concerns about transparency and intellectual property. A public email exchange revealed that OpenAI was aware of this shift but failed to address it transparently. Recent incidents involving racism, copyright infringement, and disturbing content generated by OpenAI's technology have further eroded trust in the organization's stated mission of using AI for social good.
