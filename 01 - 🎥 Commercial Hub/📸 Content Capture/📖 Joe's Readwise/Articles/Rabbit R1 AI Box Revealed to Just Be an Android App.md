# Rabbit R1 AI Box Revealed to Just Be an Android App

![rw-book-cover](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/r1-interface.desktop-760x380.jpg)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Ron Amadeo]]
- Date: 2024-05-01
- Full Title: Rabbit R1 AI Box Revealed to Just Be an Android App
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The Rabbit R1, marketed as an AI box, is actually just an Android app running on a limited $200 device. The company behind Rabbit Inc. seems upset about the discovery that their device essentially runs a smartphone OS. By using the Android Open Source Project, Rabbit Inc. made a technically sound decision to leverage existing mobile technology without Google Play restrictions.
- URL: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2024/05/rabbit-r1-ai-box-is-just-an-android-app-and-the-software-can-run-on-a-phone/

## Highlights
- If you haven't heard of the [Rabbit R1](https://www.rabbit.tech/rabbit-r1), this is yet another "AI box" that is trying to replace your smartphone with a voice command device that runs zero apps. Just like the [Humane AI Pin](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2023/11/humane-ai-pin-a-700-voice-command-box-definitely-wont-replace-your-phone/), this thing recently launched and seems to be [dead on arrival](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ddTV12hErTc) as a completely non-viable device that doesn't solve any real problems, has terrible battery life, and is missing big chunks of core functionality. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hwts911m1hss6q61sea0z7ak))
- Android Authority's [Mishaal Rahman](https://www.androidauthority.com/rabbit-r1-is-an-android-app-3438805/) looked at the software and found the "smartphone replacement" device just *runs* a smartphone OS. It's Android—both an Android OS and Android app, just in a very limited $200 box. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hwts9grdz0cdph65yxncjdec))
- It runs AOSP (the Android Open Source Project codebase), which is the open source bits of Android without any proprietary Google code. The interface—which is mostly just a clock, settings screen, and voice input—is also just an Android app. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hwtsa2ttehg6sbxmqb9kjb3n))
- Rahman was able to get the Rabbit R1 software running on a Pixel 6. He even got the AI assistant to answer questions on the phone. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hwtsaexzjfn8kwqg7e5z3p1e))
- The company describes its device as a "very bespoke AOSP and lower level firmware modifications," but that's a statement that would be true for many phones. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hwtsb5fzhyx2rw0jvpandwcb))
