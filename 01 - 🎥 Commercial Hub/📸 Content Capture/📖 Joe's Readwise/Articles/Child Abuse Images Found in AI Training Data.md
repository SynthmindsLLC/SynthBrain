## Highlights
- Stanford researchers have discovered over 1,000 child sexual abuse images in an AI dataset used to train [popular image generation tools](https://www.axios.com/2022/09/12/ai-images-ethics-dall-e-2-stable-diffusion) such as Stable Diffusion. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj4twccax78z98ay18g8sns4))
- The [Stanford Internet Observatory report](https://cyber.fsi.stanford.edu/io/news/investigation-finds-ai-image-generation-models-trained-child-abuse) pinpoints the LAION-5B model, a popular open source dataset maintained by a Germany-based nonprofit, as the source of the images. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj4tx3r8kh607vbq4ck3vqrz))
- A LAION spokesperson [told Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-12-20/large-ai-dataset-has-over-1-000-child-abuse-images-researchers-find) it pursues a "zero tolerance policy" for illegal content and has removed LAION datasets from the internet while it investigates. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj4txjrr2kfbd2j3r5qe0n7p))
- A Stability AI spokesperson told Bloomberg the company had filtered the LAION data before using it for training, and said the firm has "implemented filters to intercept unsafe prompts or unsafe outputs when users interact with models on our platform." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hj4tyzp51xrmmses8gbp8hqx))
