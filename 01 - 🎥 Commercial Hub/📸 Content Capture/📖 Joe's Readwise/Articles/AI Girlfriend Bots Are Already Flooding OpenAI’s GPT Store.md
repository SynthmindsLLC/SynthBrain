## Highlights
- It’s day two of the opening of OpenAI’s [buzzy GPT store](https://qz.com/gpts-generative-ai-digital-brains-tech-1851103960), which offers customized [versions of ChatGPT](https://qz.com/openai-will-now-let-you-create-your-own-personal-chatbo-1850998451), and users are already breaking the rules. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkxjdd0j032994exr6gm6qx8))
- A search for “girlfriend” on the new GPT store will populate the site’s results bar with at least eight “girlfriend” AI chatbots including “Korean Girlfriend”, “Virtual Sweetheart”, “Your girlfriend Scarlett”, “Your AI girlfriend, Tsu✨”. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkxjdt3dekt3aty2dw4ma590))
- The AI girlfriend bots go against OpenAI’s usage policy, which was updated when the GPT store launched yesterday (Jan. 10). For the GPT store, the [company bans GPTs](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies) “dedicated to fostering romantic companionship or performing regulated activities.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkxjecne2jc3ty0f5q13dss2))
- The proliferation of these apps may stem from the [epidemic of loneliness and isolation](https://www.hhs.gov/about/news/2023/05/03/new-surgeon-general-advisory-raises-alarm-about-devastating-impact-epidemic-loneliness-isolation-united-states.html) Americans are facing. Alarming studies show that [one-in-two American adults](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) have reported experiencing loneliness, with the US Surgeon General calling for the need to strengthen social connections. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkxjf64xeya0wa0et380gw3g))
- OpenAI’s store rules being broken on the second day of operation illustrates how hard it could be to regulate GPTs. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkxjfwb0k1rvvdndc6prff35))
