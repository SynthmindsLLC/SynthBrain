- Summary: An ex-OpenAI board member revealed that CEO Sam Altman was briefly ousted for lying to the board and withholding information. The board lost trust in Altman due to his deceit and lack of transparency, leading to his removal. Despite his brief ousting, Altman was reinstated as CEO shortly after due to staff threats and speculation about Microsoft's interest in his team.
