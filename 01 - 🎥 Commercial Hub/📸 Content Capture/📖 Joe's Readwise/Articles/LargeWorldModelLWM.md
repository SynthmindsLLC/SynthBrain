- Summary: Large World Model (LWM) is a versatile autoregressive model trained on extensive video and book data, utilizing RingAttention to enhance language, image, and video comprehension and generation. By combining video sequences with language, LWM aims to bridge the gap in understanding complex, non-verbal aspects of the world, providing valuable insights for AI applications. The model sets new standards in processing long video sequences and offers solutions for vision-language training challenges, with various context sizes and capabilities available for language-only and vision-language models. LWM's codebase is optimized for training on TPUs and GPUs, paving the way for enhanced understanding of multimodal data and broader AI capabilities.
