## Highlights
- The Alignment Problem, by Brian Christian (2020) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqaza1n1f7qakstkwwrzbb))
- The problem with A.I. isn’t that it’s going to end the world, Christian says. The problem is determining how to “align” machine behavior with human values, a conundrum we have been trying and mostly failing to solve since the invention of the cotton gin. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqca6qhw7m81vz2bc9w37j))
- Artificial Intelligence, by Melanie Mitchell (2019) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqer85cc2z362kh6ac74c0))
- “Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans” provides the necessary history of the development of the technology, from the psychologist [Frank Rosenblatt and his perceptron](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2019/09/professors-perceptron-paved-way-ai-60-years-too-soon) (a simple neural network) in the 1950s to the [Stanford computer scientist Fei-Fei Li](https://profiles.stanford.edu/fei-fei-li) and her [ImageNet](https://www.image-net.org/) (a landmark visual database used for training intelligent machines). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqggg7h7ekved4xqpj5z5g))
- The Algorithm, by Hilke Schellmann (2024) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqja1qt7pw7je4btx1mkem))
- Human beings aren’t very good at making decisions in the first place, but A.I. does not liberate us from our human limitations; it chains us to them. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqp0f66d5bm873f8rbwa35))
- The nightmares Schellmann describes — the use of facial recognition software in hiring decisions and the drive toward total employee surveillance — are more chilling than any apocalyptic scenario because they are actually happening. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqpjed19paj4s7kmhv3hvk))
- Progressive Capitalism, by Ro Khanna (2022) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqq2gs5fgvfbbdztjsz671))
- “While we allowed the internet to emerge without a strong ethical or legal framework in place, we should not make the same mistake when it comes to the emergence of artificial intelligence,” he writes. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqsaw27sswfpdqhb4yz00h))
- He cites a PricewaterhouseCoopers report that concludes that by 2030, A.I. will add over $15 trillion to the global G.D.P., but notes that almost all that value will go to a sliver of the population. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqssfm51ybhanr5jpfgg9j))
- (At the moment, he adds, only 0.0006 percent of venture capital in the United States goes to Black women.) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqt5338qcar5mhm2r5ppdv))
- Khanna proposes “technology hubs” in the Midwest and South to spread the benefits — by supporting research and career training — while remaining pro-growth. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqtr19wtar1y2bdngedmeb))
- AI 2041, by Kai-Fu Lee and Chen Qiufan (2021) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxqwny5ef5emy8gm16nzr2w))
- “We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the effect in the long run.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnxr0jkz5cnfhjw95h386fkb))
