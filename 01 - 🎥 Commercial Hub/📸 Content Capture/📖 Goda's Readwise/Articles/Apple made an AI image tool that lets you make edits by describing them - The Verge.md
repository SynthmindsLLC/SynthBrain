## Highlights
- MGIE, or MLLM-Guided Image Editing, will crop photos and brighten specific areas of a photo. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6dbsysx005ynj0brc3r5b))
- Apple researchers [released a new model](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.17102.pdf) that lets users describe in plain language what they want to change in a photo without ever touching photo editing software. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6dk63em2tbb035r5xgmak))
- can crop, resize, flip, and add filters to images all through text prompts. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6e19h3zrtkc6bwt835f5s))
- modifying specific objects in a photo to make them a different shape or come off brighter. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6ed7k98ezkqp3t2pma3nb))
- blends two different uses of multimodal language models. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6ejj9dpwcvw2g8etbgaan))
- First, it learns how to interpret user prompts. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6eqqwb95npxbdnrkb088c))
- Then it “imagines” what the edit would look like (asking for a bluer sky in a photo becomes bumping up the brightness on the sky portion of an image, for example). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6f2fr3bjkwpc3a6kq71wr))
- Typing the prompt “make it more healthy” adds vegetable toppings. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6fj06m1548vsd3rdwy8de))
- “add more contrast to simulate more light,” the picture appears brighter. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6fzt4b0zmj1pd93ak4t5j))
- Apple made MGIE available through GitHub for download, but it also released a web demo on Hugging Face Spaces, [reports *VentureBeat*](https://venturebeat.com/ai/apple-releases-mgie-a-revolutionary-ai-model-for-instruction-based-image-editing/)*.* ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqn6h3cdz2jt3jgmjhxk28q4))
