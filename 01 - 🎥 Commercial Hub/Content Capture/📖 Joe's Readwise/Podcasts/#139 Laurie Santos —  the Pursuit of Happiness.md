## Highlights
- Misconceptions About Happiness and the Arrival Fallacy
  Key takeaways:
  - Effort does not always lead to happiness and success
  - Our minds can deceive us about what makes us happy
  - Material achievements do not result in lasting happiness
  - We need to work on identifying what truly brings us joy
  - The arrival fallacy leads us to believe that happiness will come when certain conditions are met
