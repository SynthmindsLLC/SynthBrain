- The Minimum Building Blocks for a Technological Civilization: 20 Parts
  Key takeaways:
  - There is a series of books by Gingory that outlines steps to create a civilization through in-situ resource utilization (ISRU).
  - The minimum building blocks for a civilization are roughly 20 parts.
  - Amino acids are important because they can be combined to create different properties.
  - Technology only needs about 20 properties to create all technology for a civilization.
  - Self-replicating assemblers are a key part of bootstrapping a civilization on Mars.
  - The use of ISRU and 20 basic building blocks has profound implications for sustainability on Earth.
  - The 20 building blocks can be used to create logic, actuation, micro robots, and larger robots.
  - The larger robots can then construct structural elements to further advance the civilization.
