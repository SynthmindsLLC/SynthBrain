## Highlights
- The Usability of Chat GPT: The Magic Ingredient of RLHF
  Key takeaways:
  - Chat GPT was an important moment in what has been seen so far.
  - The usability of chat GPT was more significant than the underlying model or its components.
  - RLHF stands for Reinforcement learning with human feedback.
  - RLHF is the magic ingredient that enhances the effectiveness of the model.
  - Training the models on text data helps them learn about the underlying representations of the content.
  - Although the base model performs well on evaluations and tests, it is not very useful or easy to use.
  - RLHF incorporates human feedback to make the model more useful and aligned with human preferences.
  - RLHF process works remarkably well with minimal data.

- Alignment, Capability, and the Fuzzy Division: Exploring the Connection
  Key takeaways:
  - Alignment and capability are closely related. Better alignment techniques lead to better capabilities and vice versa.
  - The division between alignment and capability is not clear-cut and can vary in different cases.
  - RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) is a process that involves human input to determine the best way to phrase something.
  - Agreement on broad bounds of what AI systems can do will be necessary for society.
  - Different countries and individual users may have different preferences in RLHF tuning.
  - Steerability features, like the system message in GPT, can be important for user customization.
