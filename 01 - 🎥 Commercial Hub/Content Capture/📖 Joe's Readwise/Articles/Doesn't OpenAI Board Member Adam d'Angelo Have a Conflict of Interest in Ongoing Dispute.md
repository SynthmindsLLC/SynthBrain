## Highlights
- • **April 2018**: Adam D’Angelo [joined](https://twitter.com/adamdangelo/status/988859015315701760?lang=en) OpenAI’s board.
  • **December 2022**: Quora [launched Poe](https://voicebot.ai/2022/12/29/quora-debuts-generative-ai-chatbot-platform-poe/), the Platform for Open Exploration, as a ChatGPT-like general knowledge chatbot.
  • **March 2023**: Poe added [a subscription tier](https://voicebot.ai/2023/03/15/quora-unveils-subscriptions-for-generative-ai-chatbot-hub-poe/) that matched the $20 per month price of ChatGPT Plus.
  • **April 2023**: Poe added the ability to [create your own bots](https://venturebeat.com/ai/poe-introduces-chatbot-creation-feature-with-simple-text-prompts/) using either ChatGPT or Anthropic’s Claude large language models (LLM).
  • **July 2023**: Poe [adds support](https://www.neowin.net/news/poe-chatbot-adds-support-for-several-meta-llama-2-models/) for Meta’s Llama 2 LLM.
  • **August 2023**: Poe adds support for Google’s PaLM 2 LLM.
  • **October 2023**: Poe [announces a new marketplace](https://voicebot.ai/2023/10/31/poe-starts-sharing-revenue-with-generative-ai-chatbot-developers-in-new-creator-economy/) where custom bot creators can earn revenue from users. Bot creators can also add a custom knowledge base to ground the answers, customize responses, and share with others.
  • **November 2023**: OpenAI announces [GPTs](https://synthedia.substack.com/p/openai-has-halted-chatgpt-plus-subscriptions), a new feature that enables the creation of customized chatbots based on ChatGPT. Sam Altman also indicated there would be a [marketplace launched later in the month](https://synthedia.substack.com/p/12-things-that-matter-from-openai) that would allow bot developers to share and sell access to their creations.
