## Metadata
- Author: [[Gary Marcus]]
- Date: 2023-12-07
- Full Title: The Truth About Google’s Amazing New Demo
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google released a demo video showcasing their new project called Gemini, which featured impressive real-time interactions. However, the author, a tech industry veteran, didn't watch the entire video because they believe that demos often do not reflect the true capabilities of a product. They reference a previous example with OpenAI's robot Rubik's cube video, which turned out to be less applicable in the real world than initially portrayed. The author questions the authenticity of Google's demo and suggests that Gemini may have limited understanding and unpredictable reasoning.
