- Summary: Alibaba researchers are working diligently to develop large language models (LLMs) to compete with OpenAI, mirroring the intense schedules and dedication seen in Silicon Valley. The demanding work ethic in China's LLM space involves top talent dedicating long hours to coding, model training, and research. Alibaba's Qwen team has produced LLMs with billions of parameters, leading to applications in enterprise communication and online retail. As China's tech firms race to advance in the AI space, Alibaba is investing in startups and expanding into multilingual models to carve out a niche in the market.
