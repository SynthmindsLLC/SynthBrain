- Summary: Google explains the issues with its Gemini AI tool, which generated "embarrassing and wrong" images due to tuning problems, showing racially diverse Nazis and US Founding Fathers. The model's tuning to display a range of people led to overcompensation and being overly conservative, causing it to refuse specific prompts incorrectly. Google apologized for the errors and stopped user image generation with Gemini AI temporarily while they work on improving its abilities to provide accurate depictions requested by users. The company aims to address these challenges and ensure accuracy before reenabling the feature.

## Highlights
- Google has issued an explanation for the “embarrassing and wrong” images generated by its Gemini AI tool. In [a blog post on Friday](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-image-generation-issue/), Google says its model produced “inaccurate historical” images due to tuning issues. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqf1h1rwhdw1s4nt1x7n4kjp))
- Gemini generating images of [racially diverse Nazis](https://www.theverge.com/2024/2/21/24079371/google-ai-gemini-generative-inaccurate-historical) and US Founding Fathers earlier this week. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqf1h7g9wrcara3fq5n5zhax))
- Our tuning to ensure that Gemini showed a range of people failed to account for cases that should clearly *not* show a range,” Prabhakar Raghavan, Google’s senior vice president, writes in the post. “And second, over time, the model became way more cautious than we intended and refused to answer certain prompts entirely — wrongly interpreting some very anodyne prompts as sensitive.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqf1hqha0mtx0q8yqpzkmcc4))
- This led Gemini AI to “overcompensate in some cases,” like what we saw with the images of the racially diverse Nazis. It also caused Gemini to become “over-conservative.” This resulted in it refusing to generate specific images of “a Black person” or a “white person” when prompted. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqf1jc328a8bf0wrztyp5faw))
- if you prompt Gemini for images of a specific type of person — such as “a Black teacher in a classroom,” or “a white veterinarian with a dog” — or people in particular cultural or historical contexts, you should absolutely get a response that accurately reflects what you ask for. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqf1kvwq2e9tmce6v289x7jg))
- Google [stopped letting users create images of people](https://www.theverge.com/2024/2/22/24079876/google-gemini-ai-photos-people-pause) with its Gemini AI tool on February 22nd — just weeks after it [launched image generation](https://www.theverge.com/2024/2/1/24057438/bard-gemini-imagen-google-ai-image-generation) in Gemini (formerly known as Bard).
