## Metadata
- Author: [[Nadeem Sarwar]]
- Date: 2024-02-14
- Full Title: ChatGPT Creator OpenAI May Take on Google With Its Own Internet Search Tool
- Category: #articles
- Summary: OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, is reportedly developing its own internet search tool to compete with Google. It is unclear whether the search tool will be integrated into ChatGPT or launched as a standalone product. OpenAI has been working towards this goal for over a year, with ChatGPT now capable of browsing the web using Microsoft's search engine, Bing. Microsoft, which has heavily invested in OpenAI, may support the search service with its own search engine, Bing, in an effort to compete with Google. The details of OpenAI's web search product are still unknown, but there are similar examples like Arc Search that scrape the web and present the results in a custom web page.
