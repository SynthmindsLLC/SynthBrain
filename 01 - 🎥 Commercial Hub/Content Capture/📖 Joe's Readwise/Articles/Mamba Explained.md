- Summary: Mamba is a new AI model based on State Space Models (SSMs) that competes with Transformer models, offering similar performance but faster processing speeds. ## Highlights
- **Mamba**, however, is one of an alternative class of models called **State Space Models** (**SSMs**). Importantly, for the first time, Mamba promises similar performance (and crucially similar *[scaling laws](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.15556.pdf)*) as the Transformer whilst being feasible at long sequence lengths (say 1 million tokens). To achieve this long context, the Mamba authors remove the “quadratic bottleneck” in the Attention Mechanism. Mamba also runs *fast* - like “up to 5x faster than Transformer fast”.
