This model outperforms other advanced open-source models including GPT-3.5 and Mistral Medium in several benchmarks. While Smaug-72B does not yet reach human-level performance, its release marks a significant advancement in open-source AI and challenges the dominance of Big Tech companies. Abacus AI's future plans include publishing research papers and applying their techniques to other models. The emergence of Smaug-72B and other open-source models is seen as a positive development for AI innovation and democratization.

## Highlights
- A new open-source language model has claimed the throne of the best in the world, according to the [latest rankings from Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/spaces/HuggingFaceH4/open_llm_leaderboard) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hp350n78p7e7q88tq3nmy9ar))
- The model, called “[Smaug-72B](https://huggingface.co/abacusai/Smaug-72B-v0.1),” was released publicly today by the startup [Abacus AI](https://abacus.ai/), which helps enterprises solve difficult problems in the artificial intelligence and machine learning space. Smaug-72B is technically a fine-tuned version of “[Qwen-72B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-72B),” another powerful language model that was released just a few months ago by Qwen, a team of researchers at Alibaba Group. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hp3512c7f3zrdttzcq6mpxpn))
- Smaug-72B outperforms GPT-3.5 and Mistral Medium, two of the most advanced open source large language models developed by OpenAI and Mistral, respectively, in several of the most popular benchmarks. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hp351fjc8s9hb1dtr6pd5ct5))
