# Google Splits Up Its Responsible AI Team

![rw-book-cover](https://media.wired.com/photos/65b9a3a6190e126aeac48076/master/pass/Google-Breaking-Up-Responsible-Innovation-Team-Business-1360360657.jpg)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Paresh Dave]]
- Full Title: Google Splits Up Its Responsible AI Team
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google's Responsible Innovation team, known as RESIN, which reviewed internal projects for compatibility with Google's AI principles, has been restructured and its leader, Jen Gennai, has left the role. The team of about 30 people has been split into two, with some members remaining in place and others transferred to the trust and safety team. Google has not provided details about how AI principles reviews will be handled going forward, but a spokesperson says the changes signal the company's commitment to responsible AI development. RESIN's departure raises concerns among employees about the potential hasty development of AI products.
- URL: https://www.wired.com/story/google-splits-up-responsible-innovation-ai-team/

- Google’s Responsible Innovation team, known as RESIN, was located inside the office of compliance and integrity, in the company’s global affairs division. It reviewed internal projects for compatibility with [Google’s AI principles](https://www.wired.com/story/google-sets-limits-on-its-use-of-ai-but-allows-defense-work/) that define rules for development and use of the technology, a crucial role as the company [races to compete in generative AI](https://www.wired.com/story/gadget-lab-podcast-597/). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6hb5tw2pzzdr457pt10ca))
- RESIN’s role has looked uncertain since its leader and founder Jen Gennai, director of responsible innovation, suddenly left that role this month, say the sources, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss personnel changes. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6hwzrv6bpf3fjw0gwb50b))
- Google split Gennai’s team of about 30 people into two. Some will remain in place while others were transferred to trust and safety, which fights abuse of Google services, and also resides in the global affairs division. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6jq2d0x377ayyvzph0kkt))
- The rationale for the changes and how responsibilities will be broken up couldn’t be learned. Some of the sources say they have not been told how AI principles reviews will be handled going forward. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6jz5qmb0cen1kh23t8rfn))
- The move “brought this particular Responsible AI team to the center of our well-established trust and safety efforts, which are baked into our product reviews and plans,” he says. “It will help us strengthen and scale our responsible innovation work across the company.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6mne9gt09qm6v0y2ct2xe))
- Google is known for frequently reshuffling its ranks but RESIN had largely been untouched since the group’s founding. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6n47qrmjf26fk447fzecz))

She is now responsible AI product director at Salesforce, according to her LinkedIn profile. Tangdall declined to comment and Gennai didn’t respond to calls for comment. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6nkhk4c3qcea9ehw97cjh))
- Google created its Responsible Innovation team in 2018 not long after AI experts and others at the company publicly [rose up in protest](https://www.wired.com/story/googles-contentious-pentagon-project-is-likely-to-expand/) against a Pentagon contract called Project Maven that used Google algorithms to analyze drone surveillance imagery. RESIN became the core steward of a set of [AI principles introduced after the protests](https://www.wired.com/story/google-sets-limits-on-its-use-of-ai-but-allows-defense-work/), which say Google will use AI to benefit people, and never for weapons or undermining human rights. Gennai helped author the principles. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6p9yahbde6gtqnwjf1kdn))
- The group stopped the release of [AI image generators](https://blog.google/technology/ai/update-our-progress-responsible-ai-innovation/) and [voice synthesis algorithms](https://blog.google/technology/ai/update-work-ai-responsible-innovation/) that could be used to create deepfakes. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6pvnyyvz6vgbtc5wzjf7m))
- Gennai has said early reviews of AI systems pay off by preventing costly ethical breaches. “If implemented properly, Responsible AI makes products better by uncovering and working to reduce the harm that unfair bias can cause, improving transparency and increasing security,” [she said during a Google conference in 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvytEFQ7mIo). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hnp6qgpx574e7hjh3z6rempa))
