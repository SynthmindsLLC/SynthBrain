- Category: #articles
- Summary: The Air Canada chatbot incident, where a customer was misinformed about a refund for bereavement trips, raises questions about accountability when chatbots provide incorrect information. Air Canada attempted to shift blame onto the chatbot as a separate legal entity, but the tribunal judge disagreed. This situation highlights the potential implications for companies if they are held responsible for the inaccuracies of their chatbots, which could impact the use of large language models in this technology sector. Gary Marcus has long cautioned about the unreliability of LLM-powered chatbots, and this case underscores the challenges faced by the industry in ensuring accurate and trustworthy interactions with customers.
