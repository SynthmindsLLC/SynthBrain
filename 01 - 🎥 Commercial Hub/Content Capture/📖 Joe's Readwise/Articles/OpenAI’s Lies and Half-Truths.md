- Summary: OpenAI has been criticized for not being fully transparent in its communication, with instances like CTO Mira Murati evading questions during interviews. The company's history reveals a pattern of misleading statements, such as overemphasizing openness while not following through, and making claims that don't hold up under scrutiny. Concerns have been raised about the company's practices, including the handling of data, financial transparency, and their stance on regulation, indicating a lack of full disclosure in various important aspects. Despite ongoing scrutiny, OpenAI continues to operate without a complete commitment to transparency and candor.
