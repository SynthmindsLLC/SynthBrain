## Metadata
- Author: [[Morgan Meaker]]
- Date: 2024-02-20
- Full Title: AI Is Coming for the Experts. First, It Needs Their Help
- Category: #articles
- Summary: A new workforce of language experts, writers, and physicists are turning to data labor, training AI systems to improve their skills. They are employed by data labor platforms, such as Remotasks, which works closely with clients like OpenAI and Microsoft. Their role is ultimately to produce data that moves the envelope in terms of what AI systems are capable of, and to provide expert fact-checking. While wages can be high, there is also concern that these workers are training themselves out of a job.
