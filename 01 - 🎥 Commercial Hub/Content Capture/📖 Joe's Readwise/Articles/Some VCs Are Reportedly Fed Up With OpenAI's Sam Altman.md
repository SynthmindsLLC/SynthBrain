Anonymous sources quoted in the report criticize Altman for his lofty rhetoric, cult-of-personality, and alleged intellectual dishonesty. They express concerns about his true intentions and focus on building his own reputation rather than benefiting humanity. Despite the negative feedback, many sources chose to remain anonymous, indicating a fear of Altman's influence. Overall, there are ongoing criticisms and allegations surrounding Altman's leadership and behavior within the industry.
