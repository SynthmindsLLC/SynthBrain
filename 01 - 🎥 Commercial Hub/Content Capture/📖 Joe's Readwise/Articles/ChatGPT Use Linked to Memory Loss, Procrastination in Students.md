- Category: #articles
- Summary: A new study has found a concerning link between memory loss, procrastination, and decreased grades in students who heavily rely on the AI chatbot ChatGPT for academic tasks. The research, conducted with university students, revealed that those under heavy academic workload and time pressure were more likely to use ChatGPT, leading to negative consequences on their academic performance. The ease of obtaining quick responses from the chatbot may reduce students' cognitive efforts, resulting in memory issues and lower GPAs. While some students avoided using ChatGPT due to fear of getting caught, the study suggests a bidirectional relationship between chatbot usage and negative effects. The findings highlight the potential downsides of excessive reliance on generative AI tools in education.
