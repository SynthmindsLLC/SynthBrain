# What Happened to Artifact?

## Metadata
- Author: [[Sarah Perez]]
- Date: 2024-01-18
- Full Title: What Happened to Artifact?
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Artifact, a news app founded by Instagram's co-founders, has announced that it will shut down due to a lack of growth and dwindling downloads. The app struggled to catch on with a mainstream audience and failed to gain an international following. Furthermore, Artifact faced stiff competition from other news aggregators and struggled to maintain a sharp focus on delivering a better AI-powered news reading experience. Despite its closure, Kevin Systrom, co-founder of Artifact and Instagram, hinted that he is excited to continue building new things.
- URL: https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/18/why-artifact-from-instagrams-founders-failed-shut-down/

## Highlights
- New data indicates that Artifact couldn’t catch up to the competition and struggled to grow its user base outside the U.S. It also saw downloads of the app steeply dropping off after launch, indicating a failure to catch on with a more mainstream audience. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmfgm5c4z2r9jkqcqwhyvnha))
- Artifact saw an estimated 444,000 downloads since its February 2023 launch. However, a large part of those downloads, just under 100,000, arrived around its launch. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmfgp4tyw4ap8g5fhcywptn5))
- Though rival news aggregator SmartNews [was also losing downloads and active users at the time](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/08/as-twitter-x-rivals-explode-news-aggregator-smartnews-struggles-to-retain-users/), in addition to its CEO shake-up, it still managed to pull in far more users than Artifact. During the time that Artifact was on the market, SmartNews saw 2 million downloads. Artifact had less than a quarter of that figure. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmfgpx5356j4j23d729drmce))
- All of a sudden, the app was allowing users to [post their links](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/13/personalized-news-ap-artifact-becomes-a-discovery-engine-for-the-web-with-new-links-feature/), similar to Pinterest, followed by an ability to [post text content, like on Twitter/X](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/28/artifact-co-founder-mike-krieger-says-theres-a-flavor-of-twitter-in-apps-latest-release/). Soon after, it added [an option for sharing places](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/instagram-co-founders-app-artifact-now-let-you-discover-recommended-places-too/), turning Artifact into a recommendations engine for the real world. That then begged the question as to what Artifact wanted to be — was it a social news reading or just a social network? By adding more functionality, Artifact may have potentially confused users as to when or why they should use it. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hmfgrde499bzf9gqc1346xd3))
