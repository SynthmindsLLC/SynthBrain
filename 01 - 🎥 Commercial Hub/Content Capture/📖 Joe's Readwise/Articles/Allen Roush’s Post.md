## Highlights
- The top 7b parameter model (MistralTrix-v1) on the huggingface Open LLM Leaderboard was trained by an amateur following a medium tutorial on using DPO on a SFT of mistral. They used an already available DPO dataset contributed by Intel.
  Let all of that sink in.
  The *best* model available in Jan 2024 to easily run on regular sized GPUs in Open Source LLM land was made by someone who describes themselves like this "[I] am in no way a professional when it comes to LLM's or computer science in general, just a guy that likes to nerd about and tinker around." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hke3jkxgdme0hgbre8bfdprz))
- Both of these models are basically equivalent to GPT-3.5 (the stats claim they're significantly superior) in output quality, yet they run blazingly fast on a local GPU, they support the whole gamut of constrained/controlled generation techniques (while GPT-3.5/4 doesn't!) , and they are effectively "free" in that it's trivial to get "spicy" output out of them.
