- Summary: Mistral AI, a French challenger to OpenAI, has announced a new AI language model called Mixtral 8x7B that reportedly matches the performance of OpenAI's GPT-3.5. Mistral's models run locally with open weights, which can be downloaded and used with fewer restrictions than closed AI models. Mixtral 8x7B can process a 32K token context window and works in multiple languages. It outperforms Meta's larger LLaMA 2 70B model and matches or exceeds GPT-3.5 on certain benchmarks. The rise of open-weights AI models like Mixtral has surprised many in the industry.
