- Summary: Lawmakers are pushing for stronger laws against nonconsensual AI-generated pornography, particularly after Taylor Swift became a victim of this disturbing practice. Representative Joe Morelle introduced a bill to outlaw nonconsensual AI-generated porn, stating that the sexual exploitation involved in these deepfake images is appalling and should be a federal crime. The bill, titled "Preventing Deepfakes of Intimate Images Act," aims to make this dangerous practice illegal and hold perpetrators accountable. It has gained support from both Democrats and Republicans, with 20 co-sponsors and counting. This legislation is an important step in regulating AI and addressing the harmful impacts of deepfakes on women.
