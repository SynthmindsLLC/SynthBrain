- Category: #articles
- Summary: Effective Altruism (EA) has funded the "AI Existential Risk" ecosystem with over half a billion dollars. The movement focuses on preventing an apocalypse caused by advanced artificial intelligence (AI) and has gained significant influence. Major funders include Open Philanthropy, SFF, and LTFF, who have contributed millions of dollars to AI safety projects and research. The movement has also created an influential subculture that has moved the concept of human extinction from AI from science fiction to the mainstream. However, there has been backlash against EA, with concerns about its association with fraud and the events surrounding OpenAI. The article raises questions about how the funding could have been used for other causes and whether there will be more reflection and changes within the EA community.
