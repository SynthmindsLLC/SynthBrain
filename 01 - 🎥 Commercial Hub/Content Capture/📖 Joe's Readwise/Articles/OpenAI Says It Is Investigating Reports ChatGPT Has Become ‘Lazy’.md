## Metadata
- Author: [[Andrew Griffin]]
- Date: 2023-12-08
- Full Title: OpenAI Says It Is Investigating Reports ChatGPT Has Become ‘Lazy’
- Category: #articles
- Summary: OpenAI is investigating complaints about its ChatGPT application, built on the GPT-4 model, being perceived as "lazy." Users have reported that the chatbot refuses to follow instructions or seems uninterested in answering queries. Some users have even claimed that ChatGPT responds in a sassy manner, telling them to do the work themselves. OpenAI has acknowledged the feedback and stated that no changes have been made to the model to explain its behavior. The company is looking into the issue and working on addressing it.
