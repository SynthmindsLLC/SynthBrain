- Full Title: AI Is Saving Sales Professionals More Than Two Hours of Work Each Day
- Category: #articles
- Summary: According to a survey conducted by HubSpot, sales professionals in the US are saving over two hours of work each day thanks to artificial intelligence (AI). The survey found that sales professionals are using AI to automate tasks such as scheduling meetings and note-taking, resulting in significant time savings. Additionally, AI tools like ChatGPT are being used by nearly a third of sales workers to help with tasks like writing sales content or outreach messages. The study emphasizes that AI is not replacing salespeople but rather taking care of repetitive aspects of their work.
