- Summary: Gemini is a family of highly capable multimodal models developed by Google. It consists of three models: Ultra, Pro, and Nano. While Gemini Ultra is not yet available, Pro is comparable to GPT 3.5 and Nano is designed for mobile phones. Gemini has shown impressive performance in various modalities, such as image understanding, video understanding, and speech recognition. However, there are some limitations in the evaluation methods used, and the comparison with GPT 4 is not entirely fair. Overall, Gemini shows promise in its ability to understand complex topics and provide customized explanations, but further details about the dataset and other key aspects are yet to be provided.
