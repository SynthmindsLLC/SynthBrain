- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google's AI strategy at Google has left users bewildered due to a chaotic approach that introduced a rapid succession of new AI products with confusing names like Bard, Gemini, and Gemini Advanced. This strategy has led to internal jokes among employees and confusion among users about the different versions and capabilities of the AI offerings. The inconsistent and overlapping naming schemes, along with the lack of clear explanations from Google, have contributed to the confusion and public apprehension about the company's AI products. Google's hasty and muddled rollout has created a crisis of understanding and trust, highlighting the need for a more transparent and coherent AI product roadmap to rebuild consumer confidence.
