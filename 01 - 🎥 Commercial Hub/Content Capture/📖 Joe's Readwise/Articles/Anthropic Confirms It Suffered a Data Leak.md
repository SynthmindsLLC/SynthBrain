# Anthropic Confirms It Suffered a Data Leak

## Metadata
- Author: [[Taryn Plumb]]
- Full Title: Anthropic Confirms It Suffered a Data Leak
- Category: #articles
- Summary: AI startup Anthropic has confirmed that it suffered a data leak when a contractor inadvertently sent a file containing non-sensitive customer information to a third party. The leaked information included a "subset" of customer names and open credit balances as of the end of 2023. Anthropic emphasized that this was an isolated incident caused by human error and not a breach of their systems. The company has notified affected customers and advised them to be alert to any suspicious communications. The data leak comes at a time when the Federal Trade Commission is investigating Anthropic's strategic partnerships with Amazon and Google.
