## Metadata
- Author: [[Stephen Fry]]
- Date: 2024-02-02
- Full Title: The One and the Many
- Category: #articles
- Summary: "The One and the Many" explores the concept of group behavior and individual autonomy. The author reflects on the predictability of groups, using examples such as audience attendance at a play and the emergence of personality types within groups. They also discuss the influence of statistics and mathematics in understanding human behavior and the potential for artificial intelligence to predict individual behavior. The author concludes by highlighting the disconnect between the negative perception of humanity as a mass and the positive experiences individuals have with others on a personal level.
