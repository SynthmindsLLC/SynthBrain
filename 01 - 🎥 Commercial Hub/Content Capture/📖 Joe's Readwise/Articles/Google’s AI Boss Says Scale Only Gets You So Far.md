# Google’s AI Boss Says Scale Only Gets You So Far

## Metadata
- Author: [[Will Knight]]
- Date: 2024-02-19
- Full Title: Google’s AI Boss Says Scale Only Gets You So Far
- Category: #articles
- Summary: DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis believes that AI's biggest breakthroughs are yet to come and will require more than just increased computing power. He has led the development of Gemini, an AI model powering Google products, to rival OpenAI. Hassabis emphasizes the need for innovation beyond sheer scale to achieve artificial general intelligence, focusing on advancements like agents and tool use. While the competition in AI may shift towards developing more capable agents, concerns about safety and the need for rigorous testing remain at the forefront of discussions with government organizations.
