- Summary: People are starting to return their Vision Pro headsets for reasons such as discomfort, headaches, and eye strain. Some buyers have expressed dissatisfaction with the device, noting that it is too uncomfortable to wear even for short periods of time. Others have complained about the lack of a killer app and the headset's limited productivity relative to its price. The hardware design and the device's inability to cater to individual needs and preferences have also been cited as issues. It remains to be seen how this vocal subset of early adopters will impact the future of the Vision Pro.
