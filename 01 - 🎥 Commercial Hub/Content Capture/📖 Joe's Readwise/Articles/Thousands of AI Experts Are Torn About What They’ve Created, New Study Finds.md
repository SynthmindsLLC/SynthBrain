- Summary: A new study has found that thousands of AI experts are divided on the potential outcomes of advanced AI development. The study, conducted by AI Impacts, surveyed machine learning researchers and found that a significant portion of respondents believed there was at least a 10% chance of AI leading to outcomes as bad as human extinction. This challenges the prevailing narratives about AI extinction risk, as researchers did not neatly align into pessimists and optimists. The study also revealed that experts disagreed on what actions should be taken in response to these risks, highlighting the uncertainty surrounding the development of powerful AI systems.
