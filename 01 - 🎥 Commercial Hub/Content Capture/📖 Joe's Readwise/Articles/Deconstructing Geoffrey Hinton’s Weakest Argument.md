## Metadata
- Author: [[Gary Marcus]]
- Date: 2024-02-05
- Full Title: Deconstructing Geoffrey Hinton’s Weakest Argument
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Gary Marcus deconstructs Geoffrey Hinton's arguments against his belief that large language models (LLMs) have limitations in understanding language. Hinton's arguments include the claim that humans also make errors and hallucinations, but Marcus argues that the errors made by LLMs are different and more significant. Marcus also clarifies that he never claimed that LLMs store text, but rather they reconstruct it in a way that can sometimes lead to close memorization. Additionally, Marcus challenges Hinton's argument about understanding, stating that current neural networks lack deep understanding and common sense. Marcus concludes by highlighting the support he has received from others in the field who share his views.
