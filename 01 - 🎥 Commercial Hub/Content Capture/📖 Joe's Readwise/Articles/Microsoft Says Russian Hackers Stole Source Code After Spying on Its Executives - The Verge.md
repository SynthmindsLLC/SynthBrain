- Summary: Microsoft disclosed that Russian state-sponsored hackers accessed some source code after spying on its senior leadership team. The attack, linked to the SolarWinds incident, was described as ongoing. The hackers, known as Nobelium or Midnight Blizzard, are attempting to breach Microsoft and its customers using stolen information. Microsoft is enhancing security measures to defend against this advanced persistent threat and is actively investigating the situation.
