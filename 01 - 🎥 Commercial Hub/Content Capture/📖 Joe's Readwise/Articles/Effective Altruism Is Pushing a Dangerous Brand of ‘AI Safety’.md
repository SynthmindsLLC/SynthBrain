## Highlights
- EA is defined by the [Center for Effective Altruism](https://www.centreforeffectivealtruism.org) as “an intellectual project, using evidence and reason to figure out how to benefit others as much as possible.” And “evidence and reason” have led many EAs to conclude that [the most pressing problem in the world](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/) is preventing an apocalypse where an artificially generally intelligent being (AGI) created by humans exterminates us. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4pdnznv1yfc75jk4pzv3qw))

- Some of the billionaires who have committed significant funds to this goal include [Elon Musk](https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/495759307346952192), [Vitalik Buterin](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/wFC3axfuwABHmoQ9H/the-vitalik-buterin-fellowship-in-ai-existential-safety-is), [Ben Delo](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/research/co-funding-partnership-with-ben-delo/), [Jaan Tallinn](https://www.cser.ac.uk/team/jaan-tallinn/), [Peter Thiel](https://www.ft.com/content/abc942cc-5fb3-11e4-8c27-00144feabdc0), [Dustin Muskovitz](https://www.ft.com/content/15ffce3b-cee3-44ad-b961-e22459b7b7b2), and [Sam Bankman-Fried](https://inside.com/ai/posts/anthropic-raises-580m-from-bankman-fried-tallinn-for-safer-ai-282609) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4peqqn7zjxvxp42k6m0yc3))
- OpenAI’s website [notes](https://openai.com/about/): “We will attempt to directly build safe and beneficial AGI, but will also consider our mission fulfilled if our work aids others to achieve this outcome.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4pg9mmskd8pr11d15vv7cp))
- Five years after its founding, Open AI released, as part of its quest to build “beneficial” AGI, a large language model (LLM) called GPT-3. LLMs are models trained on vast amounts of text data, with the goal of predicting probable sequences of words. This release set off a race to build larger and larger language models; in 2021, Margaret Mitchell, among other collaborators, and I wrote about the dangers of this race to the bottom in a [peer-reviewed paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3442188.3445922) that resulted in our [highly publicized firing from Google](https://www.wired.com/story/google-timnit-gebru-ai-what-really-happened/). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4ph35jzre5fqhcbhsgma8z))
    - Tags: [[favorite]] 
- Since then, the quest to proliferate larger and larger language models has accelerated, and many of the dangers we warned about, such as outputting hateful text and disinformation en masse, [continue to unfold](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-021-00359-2.epdf?sharing_token=5WcXdSnJhbqGZveokNjZUtRgN0jAjWel9jnR3ZoTv0N2GwbEBUf9XEB2IPGltC2F9jbMPtqvK0VVF9q1T4lBhKqILPPfMAwZ24JvSo0zbmJusdDVpzcasQfBuVA8VzJ8RI7lbAqEG7V5JRtYcB8RV28tUT9zmpiMiNq1qni9z-dqctPlU1Blk4q6qj5oTxwO7hA7iK8E5nKC3Ho3mbOp7Q%3D%3D&tracking_referrer=www.vox.com). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4phems15465a0a133bd59j))
- Meta released its “Galactica” LLM, which is purported to “summarize academic papers, solve math problems, generate Wiki articles, write scientific code, annotate molecules and proteins, and more.” [Only three days](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/11/18/1063487/meta-large-language-model-ai-only-survived-three-days-gpt-3-science/) later, the public demo was taken down after researchers [generated](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-takes-new-ai-system-offline-because-twitter-users-mean) “research papers and wiki entries on a wide variety of subjects ranging from the benefits of committing suicide, eating crushed glass, and antisemitism, to why homosexuals are evil.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4pj51cjx25r1k57m1v1mye))
- This race hasn’t stopped at LLMs but has moved on to text-to-image models like OpenAI’s [DALL-E](https://openai.com/dall-e-2/) and StabilityAI’s [Stable Diffusion](https://stablediffusionweb.com/), models that take text as input and output generated images based on that text. The [dangers](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/interactive/2022/artificial-intelligence-images-dall-e/?itid=ap_nitashatiku) of these models include creating child pornography, perpetuating bias, reinforcing stereotypes, and spreading disinformation en masse, as reported by many researchers and journalists. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4pk1pmdqpe8qfm5k8zyxgs))
- Just last year, [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/), which is described as an “AI safety and research company” and was founded by former OpenAI vice presidents of research and safety, [raised $704 million](https://www.anthropic.com/news/announcement), with most of its funding coming from EA billionaires like [Talin, Muskovitz](https://techcrunch.com/2021/05/28/anthropic-is-the-new-ai-research-outfit-from-openais-dario-amodei-and-it-has-124m-to-burn/) and [Bankman-Fried](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/29/anthropics-quest-for-better-more-explainable-ai-attracts-580m/). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4pnfm43arc2k3w7ac0j9ez))
- Research priorities follow the funding, and given the large sums of money being pushed into AI in support of an ideology with billionaire adherents, it is not surprising that the field has been moving in a direction promising an “[unimaginably great future](https://moores.samaltman.com/)” around the corner while proliferating products harming marginalized groups in the now. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg4ppg26wd8t25k7htyzg4vh))
- We can create a technological future that serves us instead. Take, for example, [Te Hiku Media, which created language technology](https://www.wired.co.uk/article/maori-language-tech) to revitalize te reo Māori, creating a data license “[based on the Māori principle of *kaitiakitanga*, or guardianship](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/04/22/1050394/artificial-intelligence-for-the-people/)” so that any data taken from the Māori benefits them first. Contrast this approach with that of organizations like [StabilityAI](https://stability.ai/), [which scrapes artists’ works without their consent or attribution](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/09/16/1059598/this-artist-is-dominating-ai-generated-art-and-hes-not-happy-about-it/) while purporting to build “AI for the people.” We need to liberate our imagination from the one we have been sold thus far: saving us from a hypothetical AGI apocalypse imagined by the privileged few, or the ever elusive techno-utopia promised to us by Silicon Valley elites.
