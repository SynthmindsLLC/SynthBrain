## Highlights
- On Thursday, the makers of [ChatGPT](https://mashable.com/category/chatgpt) and DALL-E announced [Sora](https://openai.com/sora), a text-to-video diffusion model. As of today, Sora is available to red teamers, or experts who adversarially test the model for harms and risks. Per the announcement, it is also available to a select group or visual artists, designers, and filmmakers "to gain feedback on how to advance the model to be most helpful for creative professionals."
