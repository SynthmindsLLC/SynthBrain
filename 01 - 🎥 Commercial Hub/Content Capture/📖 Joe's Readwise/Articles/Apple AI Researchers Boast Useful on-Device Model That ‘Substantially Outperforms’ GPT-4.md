- Summary: Apple's AI researchers have developed a system called ReALM that surpasses the performance of ChatGPT 4.0 in reference resolution tasks. This system could make Siri smarter and more useful by considering on-screen, conversational, and background entities. Apple's model achieves significant improvements over existing systems and competes well with state-of-the-art language models like GPT-4.

## Highlights
- In a newly published research paper, Apple’s AI gurus describe a system in which Siri can do much more than try to recognize what’s in an image. The best part? It thinks one of its models for doing this benchmarks better than ChatGPT 4.0. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy3mjt79q5d2cjbe8pgsr2))
- In the [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.20329.pdf) (ReALM: Reference Resolution As Language Modeling), Apple describes something that could give a large language model-enhanced voice assistant a usefulness boost. ReALM takes into account both what’s on your screen and what tasks are active. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy432ywc1dk1696334zm4c))
- 1. On-screen Entities: These are entities that are currently displayed on a user’s screen ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy482n31st9td31djttc18))
- 2. Conversational Entities: These are entities relevant to the conversation. These entities might come from a previous turn for the user (for example, when the user says “Call Mom”, the contact for Mom would be the relevant entity in question), or from the virtual assistant (for example, when the agent provides a user a list of places or alarms to choose from). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy4j316c4pwggfda76vtwa))
- 3. Background Entities: These are relevant entities that come from background processes that might not necessarily be a direct part of what the user sees on their screen or their interaction with the virtual agent; for example, an alarm that starts ringing or music that is playing in the background. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy4qezjbh63rnhdt6ae12s))
- We also benchmark against GPT-3.5 and GPT-4, with our smallest model achieving performance comparable to that of GPT-4, and our larger models substantially outperforming it. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htdy65gpjkxepvdjf69skf6a))
