- Category: #articles
- Summary: Computer vision expert Andrej Karpathy has left OpenAI for the second time, marking the first major staff shakeup since the firing and re-hiring of CEO Sam Altman. Karpathy clarified that his departure was not the result of any particular event or issue, and he mentioned "conspiracy theories" about his exit. Despite the controversies at OpenAI, Karpathy stated that his last year at the company had been "really great" and that he has exciting personal projects ahead. This departure comes as a surprise, as co-founder Ilya Sutskever's position has been in jeopardy since the CEO fiasco.
