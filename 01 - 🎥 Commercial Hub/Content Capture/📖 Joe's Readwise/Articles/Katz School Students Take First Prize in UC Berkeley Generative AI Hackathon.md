- Summary: Two Katz School AI students, Niranjan Kumar Kishore and Tharun Prabhakar, won first prize at the 2023 UC Berkeley AI Summit Generative AI Hackathon by solving a complex business problem using the latest generative AI in under two hours. Their end-to-end automated generative AI application answered questions on generative AI's impact, opportunities, and challenges, as well as its role in healthcare. The competition featured over 100 students and professionals from various companies and universities, showcasing the growing importance of generative AI in problem-solving and creativity. Kishore and Prabhakar's success highlights the potential of advanced AI techniques in creating tailored and relevant outputs through prompt augmentation and few-shot learning.
