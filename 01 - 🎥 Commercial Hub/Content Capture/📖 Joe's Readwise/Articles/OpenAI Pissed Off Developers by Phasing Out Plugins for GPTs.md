## Highlights
- Just before Christmas weekend, OpenAI emailed plugin developers, people building practical tools with ChatGPT, asking them to turn their work into GPTs. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hjmazn53asah6s8vj1v5ga89))
- FYI, plugins aren’t going away yet,” said Logan Kilpatrick, OpenAI’s Head of Developer Relations, in a [tweet](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/1737997158970970122?s=20) responding to a disgruntled developer who got the email - “yet” being the keyword here. “Once the GPT store goes live I imagine most people will move over to GPTs,” said Kilpatrick ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hjmb14m2k78s43bv1w0sqy71))
- Sam Altman previously stated that “ChatGPT plugins don’t have [product market fit](https://web.archive.org/web/20230531203946/https://humanloop.com/blog/openai-plans)” in an interview with Human Loop, though OpenAI subsequently requested the article [be taken down](https://humanloop.com/blog/openai-plans).
