- Summary: Adobe has introduced a new AI-powered ad creation platform called GenStudio at its Summit event. This platform aims to simplify the use of Adobe's generative AI tools for building marketing campaigns. GenStudio serves as a centralized hub for promotional campaigns, offering brand kits, copy guidance, preapproved assets, and generative AI-powered tools to create ads for various platforms. It also provides analytics to optimize campaign performance. GenStudio is currently in alpha, with pricing varying by company. Adobe is also adding an AI-powered chatbot to its customer experience management platform and introducing updates to its Firefly AI model. Additionally, Adobe is partnering with Microsoft to integrate workflows and insights. Adobe emphasizes the commercial viability and safety of its AI products, aiming to stay ahead of competitors in the market.
