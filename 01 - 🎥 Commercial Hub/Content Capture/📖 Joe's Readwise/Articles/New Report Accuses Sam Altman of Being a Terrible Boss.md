- Summary: Sam Altman, the CEO of OpenAI, has been accused of manipulative and toxic behavior in recent reports. The Washington Post claims that Altman's firing was prompted by complaints of psychologically abusive and chaotic behavior, including pitting employees against each other. Altman was also accused of lying to the board in an attempt to get another member fired. While Altman enjoys employee loyalty, the allegations against him may have permanently damaged his reputation. OpenAI has reinstated Altman as CEO, but the dust has yet to settle on the situation.
