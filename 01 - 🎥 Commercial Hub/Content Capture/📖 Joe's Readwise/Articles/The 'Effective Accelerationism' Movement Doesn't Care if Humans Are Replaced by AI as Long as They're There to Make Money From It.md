# The 'Effective Accelerationism' Movement Doesn't Care if Humans Are Replaced by AI as Long as They're There to Make Money From It

- Full Title: The 'Effective Accelerationism' Movement Doesn't Care if Humans Are Replaced by AI as Long as They're There to Make Money From It
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The Effective Accelerationism movement, also known as e/acc, is a pro-AI ideology that supports the growth and development of artificial intelligence without regulation. While proponents claim it is not about replacing humans with robots, the movement has been criticized for its reckless approach to AI advancement. E/acc adherents believe in the creation of an AI singularity and see it as a necessary part of evolution beyond humanity. The movement has attracted attention from tech influencers and investors, but it has also raised concerns about the lack of safety and ethical considerations. Critics argue that the potential dangers of uncontrolled AI development outweigh its benefits.
