## Metadata
- Author: [[Scott Alexander]]
- Date: 2024-01-16
- Full Title: Against Learning From Dramatic Events
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The article argues against the idea of learning from dramatic events, using examples such as the COVID lab leak hypothesis, the government's response to 9/11, and mass shootings. The author suggests that updating beliefs based on single events is not productive and can lead to overreactions. They emphasize the importance of having prior models and predictions for various events, and caution against making significant updates based on isolated incidents. The article also highlights the role of coordination and common knowledge in response to dramatic events.
