## Highlights
- In recent days, users of GPT-4, first released in March 2023, have been taking to [OpenAI's developer forum](https://community.openai.com/t/how-to-deal-with-lazy-gpt-4/689286/19) and social media to vent about the model seemingly being far less capable than it once was. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3mcw3cmggwrdcqz1ece84m))
- The reality is that it has become unusable," one user wrote on OpenAI's online forum last week. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3md8z1hnb81eer0mnjxgv6))
- signs that [GPT-4 was getting lazier and dumber](https://www.businessinsider.com/openai-gpt4-ai-model-got-lazier-dumber-chatgpt-2023-7) emerged in summer last year. The model seemed to be having trouble as it exhibited "weakened logic," while returning wrong responses to users. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3mdrre8pr0rt1scre10pf0))
- More evidence of laziness emerged again earlier this year, with [OpenAI CEO Sam Altman](https://www.businessinsider.com/sam-altman-chatgpt-openai-ceo-career-net-worth-ycombinator-prepper-2023-1) even acknowledging that GPT-4 had been lazy. [He posted on X in February](https://www.businessinsider.com/chatgpt-openai-sam-altman-chatbot-less-lazy-after-users-complain-2024-2) that a fix had been issued to address complaints.
  > gpt-4 had a slow start on its new year's resolutions but should now be much less lazy now! ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3me039bcakcfde28a1yyf4))
- Take Anthropic's Claude. The [OpenAI rival](https://www.businessinsider.com/ai-models-can-learn-deceptive-behaviors-anthropic-researchers-say-2024-1), backed by the likes of Google and Amazon, released a premium version of its Claude model earlier this month called Claude 3 Opus. Think of it as an equivalent to GPT-4. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3mesqgtc2d2e7x35dm53mk))
- This week, Claude 3 Opus overtook GPT-4 on [LMSYS Chatbot Arena](https://huggingface.co/spaces/lmsys/chatbot-arena-leaderboard), an open platform for evaluating AI models. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3mf67ws7hsmqhrw9jjp6v9))
- On OpenAI's forum, meanwhile, users have been describing Claude as far more reliable for tasks like coding, and have described Claude 3 Opus as akin to the sharper performance GPT-4 had upon its release. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht3mgh9e3jf0gshjtwh4fvzf))
