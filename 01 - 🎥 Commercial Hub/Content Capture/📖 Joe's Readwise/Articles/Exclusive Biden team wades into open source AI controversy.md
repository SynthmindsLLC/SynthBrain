- Summary: The Biden Administration is addressing the controversy surrounding open source AI models and their impact on society. The National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) has been tasked with studying the open source question and making recommendations. The NTIA administrator, Alan Davidson, wants to gather a wide range of viewpoints before submitting a report to the White House. There are differing opinions on whether open source AI models should be protected or publicly released for testing and broader access. Davidson believes there are middle-ground options that can promote safety and access to AI tools. The NTIA is holding an event to kick off its work and will have a public comment period before drafting its report.
