- Summary: House Republicans are set to vote on a resolution to formalize an impeachment inquiry into President Joe Biden. The vote comes as Hunter Biden, the president's son, has been subpoenaed to appear for a closed-door deposition. House Republicans have previously struggled to gather enough votes to legitimize their ongoing inquiry, but now feel confident they have enough support. The argument from Republican proponents of the inquiry is that a floor vote will strengthen their legal standing and fortify their subpoenas for key witness testimony.
