- Category: #articles
- Summary: Elon Musk has pledged to make his AI chatbot, Grok, more politically neutral after it apparently expressed similar left-wing political views as ChatGPT, another chatbot. Musk responded to a post by a research scientist who compared the political beliefs of the two chatbots using the Political Compass Test. Musk criticized the test but stated that his team would take immediate action to shift Grok closer to political neutrality. He also expressed his opinion that the questions in the test lacked nuance and accuracy. Musk's company, xAI, launched Grok to premium X users and is working to improve the chatbot, which is still in beta.
