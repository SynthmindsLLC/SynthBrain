- Summary: TechCrunch tested Anthropic's new chatbot, Claude 3 Opus, which is a multimodal model that claims to best OpenAI's GPT-4 on several benchmarks. TechCrunch designed its test to list questions on subjects that the average person might ask about. The test covered trivia, medical and therapeutic advice, generating and summarizing content, and geopolitical questions. While Opus was able to answer some questions well, it struggled with current events questions and had limits to its training knowledge.
