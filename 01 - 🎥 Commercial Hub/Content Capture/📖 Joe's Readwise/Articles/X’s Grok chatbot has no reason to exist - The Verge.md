- Summary: Grok, the AI bot built into the platform formerly known as Twitter, is facing questions about its purpose and usefulness. While it was pitched as a fun and funny chatbot that could tap into the wealth of information on Twitter, it seems to lack a clear reason for existing. Grok is a paid add-on to the platform, designed to provide up-to-the-minute information and ease the transition away from advertising. However, it falls short in terms of interface integration, humor, and its ability to compete with free chatbot options like Bing, Bard, and ChatGPT. Additionally, Grok's reliance on the X ecosystem, which has faced backlash and a shift in user preferences, raises concerns about its long-term viability.
