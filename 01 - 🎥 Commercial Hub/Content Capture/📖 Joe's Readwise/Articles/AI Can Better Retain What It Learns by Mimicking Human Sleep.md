# AI Can Better Retain What It Learns by Mimicking Human Sleep

## Metadata
- Author: [[Matthew Sparkes]]
- Full Title: AI Can Better Retain What It Learns by Mimicking Human Sleep
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Researchers at the University of Catania in Italy have developed a new method of training AI called wake-sleep consolidated learning (WSCL), which mimics the way human brains reinforce new information during sleep. WSCL involves training models on a set of data during the "awake" phase, and then having periods of "sleep" and "dreaming" where the models parse through previous lessons and consume entirely novel data. The researchers found that models trained with WSCL showed a significant accuracy boost and better retention of old tasks compared to traditionally trained systems. However, some experts caution against rigidly mimicking the human brain and suggest exploring alternative approaches inspired by other biological systems, such as dolphins.
