- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google's new generative AI model, Gemini, has been unveiled, but it is not the groundbreaking model that was initially anticipated. Gemini comes in three versions: Gemini Ultra, Gemini Pro, and Gemini Nano, each with different capabilities and target devices. Gemini Pro, which powers Google's ChatGPT competitor Bard, offers improved reasoning and writing capabilities but does not surpass OpenAI's GPT-3.5. Gemini Ultra, on the other hand, is natively multimodal and can comprehend text, images, audio, and code, but its benchmark scores are only marginally better than GPT-4. Google has faced criticism for its rushed launch and concerns about the training data used for Gemini.
