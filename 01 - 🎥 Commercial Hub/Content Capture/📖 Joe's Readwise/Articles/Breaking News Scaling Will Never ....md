- Category: #articles
- Summary: Scaling neural networks for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is not a viable path due to limitations in generalization beyond training data. The need for alternative approaches that can generalize better beyond training data is crucial for achieving AGI. Gary Marcus emphasizes the importance of considering these implications for the future of AI development.

- Neural networks (at least in the configurations that been dominant over the last three decades) have trouble generalizing beyond the multidimensional space that surrounds their training examples.
