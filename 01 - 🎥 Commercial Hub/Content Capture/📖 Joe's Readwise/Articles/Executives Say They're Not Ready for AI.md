## Highlights
- Top executives are admitting they're far from ready to deal with changes wrought by generative [AI](https://www.axios.com/technology/automation-and-ai), more than a year after ChatGPT exploded on the business scene, according to a [new global survey](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/24361346-del-6723_stateofgenai_report_v4-1_compressed) by Deloitte's AI institute. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7jqkzj0tn8cf0fzavwezb1))
- Executives who reported the most investment and knowledge in generative AI capabilities are the ones most worried about the technology's impact on their business. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7jqx5r2k2vfgcvhvy5p6ph))
- [The survey](https://deloitte.com/us/state-of-generative-ai) of 2,800 director to C-suite level executives— released as the World Economic Forum in Davos kicks off — found that only 1 in 5 executives believes their organization is "highly" or "very highly" prepared to address AI skills needs in their company. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7jr4hejaa3mxxwb92gww5h))
- • Only 47% say they are sufficiently educating employees about AI. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7jrc6z1x9zz8nj2cfkmrg0))
- If you're going to look at this as some side initiative — a department of generative AI that's going to come up with all the use cases — it's going to be a massive failure," Joe Ucuzoglu, Deloitte Global CEO, told Axios in an exclusive interview. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7js2z5nx6fznnsfmc06jbj))
- You need an ecosystem mindset," he said. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hm7js6ckfx8m8npwcejn584w))
