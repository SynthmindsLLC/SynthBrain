# Executives Say They're Not Ready for AI

![rw-book-cover](https://images.axios.com/I8hE8PlDiDP_z5o0uDsoMq4WR8Y=/0x0:1607x904/1366x768/2024/01/15/1705331084774.png)

## Metadata

- Full Title: Executives Say They're Not Ready for AI
- Category: #articles
- Summary: According to a global survey by Deloitte's AI institute, top executives are admitting that they are not ready to deal with the changes brought about by generative AI, even after more than a year since ChatGPT gained popularity. Executives who reported the most investment and knowledge in generative AI capabilities are the ones most worried about the technology's impact on their business. The survey found that only 1 in 5 executives believe their organization is highly prepared to address AI skills needs, and just 1 in 4 believe their organizations are well-prepared to address AI governance and risks. The majority of executives are focused on the tactical benefits of AI rather than using it for new types of growth.
