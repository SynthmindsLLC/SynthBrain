## Highlights
- **Adam D’Angelo:** The current CEO of Quora, a social platform for questions and answers, D’Angelo spent nearly four years at [Facebook](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/META/) and was CTO of the tech giant from 2006 to 2008. He is not an employee at OpenAI.
  **Tasha McCauley:** McCauley, who is not an OpenAI employee, is on the board of directors of both OpenAI and GeoSim Systems, a geospatial tech company. She is an adjunct senior management scientist at Rand Corporation and has been on the OpenAI board since 2018.
  **Helen Toner:** Toner is a board member and non-OpenAI employee who spent time at the University of Oxford’s Center for the Governance of AI, and has been a director of strategy for Georgetown’s Center for Security and Emerging Technology for nearly five years. Last year, Toner [told](https://www.jpolrisk.com/the-risks-of-ai-an-interview-with-georgetowns-helen-toner/) the Journal of Political Risk that, “Building AI systems that are safe, reliable, fair, and interpretable is an enormous open problem... Organizations building and deploying AI will also have to recognize that beating their competitors to market— or to the battlefield — is to no avail if the systems they’re fielding are buggy, hackable, or unpredictable.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hfq63gds9y1ndevsbba4gr5m))
