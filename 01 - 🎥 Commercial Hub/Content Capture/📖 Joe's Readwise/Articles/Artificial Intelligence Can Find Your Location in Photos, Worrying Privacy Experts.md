- Category: #articles
- Summary: Artificial intelligence (AI) has the ability to accurately geolocate where photos are taken, according to a student project called Predicting Image Geolocations (PIGEON). The project, developed by Stanford graduate students, used a neural network program called CLIP to analyze images from Google Street View and classify them by their position on the globe. PIGEON was able to identify the location of a Google Street view image with a 95% accuracy rate, often within 25 miles of the actual site. While this technology has beneficial applications, such as identifying locations in old snapshots or conducting surveys, privacy experts are concerned about its potential for abuse, including government surveillance and stalking.
