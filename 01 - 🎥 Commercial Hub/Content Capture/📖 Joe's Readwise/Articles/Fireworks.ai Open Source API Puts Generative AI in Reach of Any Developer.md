- Summary: Fireworks.ai is a startup that offers an open source API for generative AI models. The company focuses on fine-tuning existing models to suit the specific needs of businesses, allowing developers to quickly implement generative AI capabilities in their applications. By limiting model size to between 7 billion and 13 billion tokens, Fireworks.ai enables users to work with smaller, more focused data sets for more practical business applications. Co-founder and CEO Lin Qiao, who previously worked at Meta, leads the company in providing accessible AI tools without the need for extensive engineering resources. The company has raised $25 million in funding from investors like Benchmark and Sequoia Capital, and aims to empower users to experiment with multiple models and effectively utilize their data.

- [Fireworks.ai](http://Fireworks.ai) is one such startup.
