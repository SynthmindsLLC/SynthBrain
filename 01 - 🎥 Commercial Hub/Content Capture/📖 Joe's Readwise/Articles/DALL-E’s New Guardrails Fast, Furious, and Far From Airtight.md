- Summary: DALL-E, the image generation system developed by OpenAI, is facing challenges in implementing effective guardrails to prevent inadvertent plagiarism. Guardrails are necessary to reject certain requests that may violate copyright or generate harmful content. However, previous attempts at implementing guardrails in language generation have been unsuccessful in finding the right balance between being too restrictive or too permissive. OpenAI is now trying to establish guardrails for plagiarism in the image domain, but it remains to be seen how effective they will be. Additionally, the inconsistency and ambiguity in how search engines like Bing handle copyright and originality further complicate the issue.
