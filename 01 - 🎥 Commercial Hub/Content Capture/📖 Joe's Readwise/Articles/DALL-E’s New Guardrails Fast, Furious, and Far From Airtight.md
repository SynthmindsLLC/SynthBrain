- One way of course to knock down the problem decisively would be to train only on public domain training data. Another approach would be to *license* any other data. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkscsa78f4we9cpafxq4n7b4))
- Guardrails are where the system rejects certain requests (“teach me how to build a nuclear bomb” or “write a racist screed insulting so and so”). Such guardrails have previously been installed for moral / ethical reasons. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hksctekycs2965f9nkmppxp4))
- virtually any guardrail has to thread a needle between the Scylla of being too restrictive and Charybdis of being too permissive. None thus far have done this effectively. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hkscv4az40dskecbyekr8pmp))
