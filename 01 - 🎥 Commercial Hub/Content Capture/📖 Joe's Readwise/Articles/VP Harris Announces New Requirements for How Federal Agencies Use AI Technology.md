- Category: #articles
- Summary: Vice President Kamala Harris announced new rules for how US government agencies can use AI technology to prevent discrimination and protect people's rights. Agencies must now disclose the AI systems they use, conduct risk assessments, and designate a chief AI officer for oversight. These measures aim to ensure transparency, accountability, and ethical AI practices across federal agencies.
