# Mistral AI Releases New Model to Rival GPT-4 and Its Own Chat Assistant

![rw-book-cover](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/GettyImages-1630826367.jpg?resize=1200,800)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Romain Dillet]]
- Date: 2024-02-26
- Full Title: Mistral AI Releases New Model to Rival GPT-4 and Its Own Chat Assistant
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Mistral AI has introduced Mistral Large, a large language model to compete with top-tier models like GPT-4 and Claude 2. The company has also launched Le Chat, its own chat assistant, available in beta. Mistral AI, known for raising significant funds, offers Mistral Large through a paid API and usage-based pricing, positioning itself similarly to OpenAI. Additionally, Mistral AI has partnered with Microsoft to provide Mistral models to Azure customers, potentially expanding its customer base.
- URL: https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/26/mistral-ai-releases-new-model-to-rival-gpt-4-and-its-own-chat-assistant/

## Highlights
- The company is launching a new flagship large language model called Mistral Large. It is designed to rival other top-tier models when it comes to reasoning capabilities, such as GPT-4 and Claude 2. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk4yhd1cqcx502w72prycjt))
- Mistral Large, the startup is also launching its own alternative to ChatGPT with a new service called [Le Chat](https://chat.mistral.ai/). This chat assistant is currently available in beta. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk4ysb7m3fv8nbt1gqq379c))
- The company was officially incorporated in May 2023. Just a few weeks after that, Mistral AI raised a [$112 million seed round](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/13/frances-mistral-ai-blows-in-with-a-113m-seed-round-at-a-260m-valuation-to-take-on-openai/). In December, the company [closed a $415 million funding round](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/11/mistral-ai-a-paris-based-openai-rival-closed-its-415-million-funding-round/) with Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) leading the round. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk4zag5202t0kkfmc8ydpqs))
- Mistral AI’s business model looks more and more like OpenAI’s business model as the company offers Mistral Large through a paid API and usage-based pricing. It currently costs $8 per million of input tokens and $24 per million of output tokens to query Mistral Large. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk4zxes85z8vr43c6trcaxv))
- Mistral AI is also launching a chat assistant today called Le Chat. Anyone can sign up and try it out on [chat.mistral.ai](https://chat.mistral.ai/). The company says that it is a beta release for now and that there could be “quirks”. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk50s7xxn3ejacewv9znbhs))
- Mistral AI is also using today’s news drop to announce a partnership with Microsoft. In addition to Mistral’s own API platform, Microsoft is going to [provide Mistral models](https://aka.ms/aistudio/landing/mistral-large) to its Azure customers. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqk51eskq2f479jz9jc90fqp))
