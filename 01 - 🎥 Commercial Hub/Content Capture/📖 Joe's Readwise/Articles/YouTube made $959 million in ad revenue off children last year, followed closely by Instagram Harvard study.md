- Summary: According to a study from Harvard, YouTube made the most ad revenue from users aged 12 and under ($959.1 million), followed by Instagram ($801.1 million) and Facebook ($137.2 million). Social media companies collectively made over $11 billion in US ad revenue from minors last year. The researchers argue that government regulation is needed to address the financial incentives that prevent these companies from taking meaningful action to protect children. The study also highlights concerns about the negative effects of social media on youth mental health and the need for greater transparency from tech companies.
