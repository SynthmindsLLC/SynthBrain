- Summary: OpenAI has confirmed that ChatGPT has been giving lackluster responses and behaving "lazier" than before. Users on the ChatGPT subreddit have reported instances of ChatGPT not being as helpful as it used to be. OpenAI stated that the model hasn't been updated since November 11th and they are looking into fixing it. While OpenAI hasn't provided specific details about the underperformance, they mentioned that differences in model behavior can be subtle and may take time to notice and fix. This isn't the first time users have complained about ChatGPT's performance, with similar complaints being made six months ago.
