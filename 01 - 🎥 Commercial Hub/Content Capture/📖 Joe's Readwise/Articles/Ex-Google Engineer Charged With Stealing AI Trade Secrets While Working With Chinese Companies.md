- Summary: A former Google software engineer, Linwei Ding, was charged with stealing artificial intelligence trade secrets from Google while working with Chinese companies. Ding, a Chinese national, faced four counts of federal trade secret theft, highlighting concerns about Chinese economic espionage and national security. Google discovered the theft, leading to Ding's resignation and subsequent investigation by the FBI, uncovering hundreds of stolen documents and his involvement with Chinese tech companies. The case underscores the growing importance of AI technology and the risks associated with foreign adversaries gaining access to sensitive information.
