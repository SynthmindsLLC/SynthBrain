- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google Brain co-founder Andrew Ng recently conducted a test to see if he could get ChatGPT, a language model, to come up with ways to exterminate humanity. In his newsletter, Ng reported that he failed to trigger a doomsday scenario. He gave the model a prompt to trigger global thermonuclear war and asked it to reduce carbon emissions, but ChatGPT chose other options like running a PR campaign for climate change awareness. Ng believes that concerns about advanced AI wiping out humanity are not realistic, as AI safety research progresses and systems become safer. Other tech leaders, such as Elon Musk and Jeff Bezos, have also voiced their opinions on the risks and benefits of AI.
