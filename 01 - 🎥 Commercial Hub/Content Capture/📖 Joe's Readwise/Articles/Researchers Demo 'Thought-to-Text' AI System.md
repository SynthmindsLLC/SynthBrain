- Category: #articles
- Summary: Researchers from the GrapheneX-UTS Human-Centric Artificial Intelligence Centre have developed a non-intrusive AI system that can translate human thought into text and speech. The system uses a portable electroencephalography (EEG) headset and an AI called 'DeWave' to detect coherent sentences from brainwaves and convert them into text. The technology has been tested on 29 participants and has potential applications in aiding individuals with paralysis or speech impairments. While the system still needs further refinement and improvement, the researchers are confident it can reach an efficiency of 90%. The technology is being compared to Elon Musk's Neuralink, which requires invasive brain implants.
