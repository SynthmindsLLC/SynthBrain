## Metadata
- Author: [[Lucas Ropek]]
- Date: 2023-12-08
- Full Title: Should Section 230 Protect AI Companies From Being Sued Out of Existence?
- Category: #articles
- Summary: A bipartisan bill is being fast-tracked in the Senate that would remove legal immunity under Section 230 for AI platforms. The bill, authored by Sens. Josh Hawley and Richard Blumenthal, aims to strip "immunity from AI companies" in civil claims and criminal prosecutions. The implications for AI companies like OpenAI are uncertain, as the debate revolves around whether they should be considered content creators or platforms. The issue highlights the complex legal and regulatory challenges posed by AI, and the need for new precedents and legislation in this area.
