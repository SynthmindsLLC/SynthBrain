- Summary: Glean, founded by Arvind Jain, aims to revolutionize enterprise search by connecting to company databases and using AI to provide employees with relevant answers. By addressing the common struggle of workers to find necessary data, Glean offers a secure and private solution that learns from customer data to personalize results. Despite competition from other AI providers, Glean's success is evident through its growing customer base, recent funding round, and plans to expand its team and product offerings to meet the rising demand for GenAI solutions in the enterprise sector.

## Highlights
- Enter [Glean](https://www.glean.com/), whose software connects to enterprise first- and third-party databases to field plain-English requests (e.g. “How do I invest in our company’s 401k?”) from employees, sort of like a custom [ChatGPT](https://techcrunch.com/tag/chatgpt/). Launched by Arvind Jain, the co-founder of cloud data management company Rubrik, Glean was inspired by Jain’s observations that Rubrik employees often struggled to find the information they needed to do their jobs — and that staffers at other companies were struggling with the same. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnsa88n47ced76bntnhtb8g))
- A recent Gartner [survey](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2023-05-10-gartner-survey-reveals-47-percent-of-digital-workers-struggle-to-find-the-information-needed-to-effectively-perform-their-jobs) found that 47% of desk workers have trouble finding the data they need to perform their jobs. In the same survey, workers reported that the growing number of apps they have to manage at work — 11 on average now versus six five years ago — is exacerbating the challenge. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnsatt8kpt0h4m8g8662xn8))
- A recent Cisco [poll](https://www.datanami.com/2024/01/26/new-cisco-study-highlights-the-impact-of-data-security-and-privacy-concerns-on-genai-adoption/#:~:text=The%20Cisco%202024%20Data%20Privacy,have%20trust%20issues%20with%20GenAI.) found that more than one in four organizations have banned the use of GenAI over privacy and data security risks. In the poll, companies said they feared GenAI tools would compromise their IP or potentially disclose other sensitive information to the public — or their rivals. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnsceg6cvvxh5ab0xxvhs50))
- Glean respects the same permissions set in a company’s data sources (Slack, Teams, Jira, ServiceNow, etc.), so employees only receive answers based on the data they’re allowed to access,” Jain said. “When a user deletes a document in the underlying application, the document gets deleted from the Glean system.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnscwyz0jdw9mw5ett3627n))
- Jain, while neither confirming nor denying Glean hallucinates, highlighted the mitigations in place to make the platform’s GenAI more reliable. including a model trained on customer data to learn industry and firm-specific jargon and letting customers switch among several open source GenAI models to drive Glean’s core experience. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnsdj3x1y0zzvzxasnjseje))
- Glean also employs RAG (short for Retrieval-Augmented Generation), an increasingly common technique used to “ground” GenAI by retrieving data from outside sources of knowledge, to boost performance. Jain says that every answer Glean gives is “fully referenceable” back to the original source. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnse3d35265vk68jjnkzngb))
- Despite competition from vendors like Microsoft (specifically Copilot) and OpenAI (ChatGPT) as well as enterprise search providers such as Coveo, Sinequa and Lucidworks, Jain says that business has been quite strong as of late, with annual recurring revenue close to quadrupling in the last year. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnseqn8tddydb3dxcm6skdx))
- Responding to a December 2023 [survey](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231205317078/en/10-of-Organizations-Surveyed-Launched-GenAI-Solutions-to-Production-in-2023) by Convrg.io, the Intel subsidiary, only 10% of organizations said that they’d launched GenAI solutions all the way to production in 2023. The vast majority of solutions remained in the research and testing phases, the organizations said — implying companies haven’t been successful in finding money-making GenAI use cases. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hqnsf4np225chaybhyx82z8h))
