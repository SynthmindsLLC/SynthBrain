- Summary: Eran Adi Cioban, a director at The Mofet Institute, shares insights on learning from system prompts and extracting instructions for OpenAI's GPT-4's search capability. The post highlights the use of the 'browser' tool for searching and identifies three conditions where it is appropriate to use: current events or real-time information, unknown terms, and explicit search requests. The post also mentions a function called 'mclick' that processes sources and selects at least three diverse and high-quality sources. The directive to retrieve in parallel is discussed, as well as the limitation of the 'open_url' function to only opening user-provided links.
