- Summary: IT professionals are concerned about the security of Large Language Models (LLMs) due to numerous jailbreaking attacks. The variety of ways to attack LLMs poses a significant threat to their security and reliability. Maintaining a small attack surface is crucial in cybersecurity, but LLMs seem to have an infinite vulnerability to attacks.
