- Summary: Google researchers have developed a new system called ASPIRE that teaches AI the value of saying "I don't know." This approach encourages AI to express doubt when it's unsure of an answer, revolutionizing how we interact with digital assistants. ASPIRE acts as a confidence meter for AI, helping it assess its own answers and assign a confidence score. The goal is to promote more reliable digital decision-making by acknowledging the limits of AI and communicating them clearly. This system creates a more cautious and trustworthy AI that can acknowledge when a human might be better suited to answer.
