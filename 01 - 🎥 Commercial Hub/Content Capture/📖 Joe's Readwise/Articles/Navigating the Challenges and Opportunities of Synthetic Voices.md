## Highlights
- Today we are sharing preliminary insights and results from a small-scale preview of a model called Voice Engine, which uses text input and a single 15-second audio sample to generate natural-sounding speech that closely resembles the original speaker. It is notable that a small model with a single 15-second sample can create emotive and realistic voices. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5mp8z2t2mdgtbwm4a455gm))
- developed Voice Engine in late 2022, and have used it to power the preset voices available in the [text-to-speech API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/text-to-speech) as well as [ChatGPT Voice and Read Aloud](https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt-can-now-see-hear-and-speak). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5mphzby4hbbdn0hgxpemqe))
- late last year we started privately testing it with a small group of trusted partners. We've been impressed by the applications this group has developed. These small scale deployments are helping to inform our approach, safeguards, and thinking about how Voice Engine could be used for good across various industries ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5mq4kjsbzjmzpvs51300a0))
- We recognize that generating speech that resembles people's voices has serious risks, which are especially top of mind in an election year. We are engaging with U.S. and international partners from across government, media, entertainment, education, civil society and beyond to ensure we are incorporating their feedback as we build. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5mr7gkfmvxybkq4ab1680r))
- Partners must also clearly disclose to their audience that the voices they're hearing are AI-generated. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5mry29g2887tj4ejh29d3y))
- watermarking to trace the origin of any audio generated by Voice Engine, as well as proactive monitoring of how it's being used. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01ht5ms5p4djfrdt1krtt3j5vx))
- In line with our [approach to AI safety](https://openai.com/blog/our-approach-to-ai-safety) and our [voluntary commitments](https://openai.com/blog/moving-ai-governance-forward), we are choosing to preview but not widely release this technology at this time.
