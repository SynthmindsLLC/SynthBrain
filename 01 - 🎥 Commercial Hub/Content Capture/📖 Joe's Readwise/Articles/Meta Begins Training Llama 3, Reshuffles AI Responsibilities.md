- Category: #articles
- Summary: Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced that the company has begun training Llama 3, the next generation of its primary generative AI model. He also stated that Meta is committed to releasing its AI models via open source, when possible, and that the company is reshuffling its AI org chart. These changes are aimed at helping Meta build artificial general intelligence systems to give its products more human-like capabilities in the future. The company is moving its Fundamental AI Research unit to the Family of Apps unity, which will reunite AI research and product work. Additionally, Meta plans to end the year with over 340,000 Nvidia H100 GPUs and nearly 600,000 H100 equivalents.
