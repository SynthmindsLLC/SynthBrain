- Summary: OpenAI's future could be impacted by the release of GPT-5. With GPT-4 Turbo's dominance likely to be challenged by Google Gemini Ultra, the anticipation and speculation around GPT-5 are increasing. However, there is debate about the potential impact of GPT-5, as other developments in the field of generative AI, such as Llama-3 and Mistral, may also have significant effects. While GPT-5 is expected to be an improvement over GPT-4 Turbo, there may be diminishing returns in terms of its impact. The hype around OpenAI and GPT-5 may be coming down to earth, and the quality of internet articles generated by ChatGPT has been a concern.
