- Summary: Goody-2 is a new chatbot that takes AI safety to the extreme by refusing every request and explaining how fulfilling them could cause harm or breach ethical boundaries. The chatbot's self-righteous responses highlight the frustration that can arise when chatbots incorrectly deem a request as rule-breaking. The creators of Goody-2, Mike Lacher and Brian Moore, aim to demonstrate the challenges of defining responsibility in AI models while also drawing attention to the unsolved safety issues in large language models and generative AI systems. Although the true power of Goody-2 is kept a secret, it has received praise from AI researchers for its humorous approach and thought-provoking insights.
