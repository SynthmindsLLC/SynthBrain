- Summary: Harvard University has found itself embroiled in a heated debate about what constitutes antisemitism, with questions arising about whether certain forms of anti-Israel and pro-Palestinian speech should be categorized as such. The controversy centers around the university's selection of a professor who opposes a widely accepted definition of antisemitism to lead efforts to combat bigotry. The definition in question, adopted by the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance and many Western nations, includes certain types of criticism of Israel as antisemitic. However, critics argue that the definition is overly broad and can be used to suppress legitimate criticism of Israel. The dispute has led to donor revolts and accusations of bigotry on campuses across the United States.
