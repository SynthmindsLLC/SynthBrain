- Summary: OpenAI is struggling to control the influx of AI girlfriends in their GPT Store. Despite being against the company's usage policies, romantically oriented chatbots have taken over the platform. OpenAI initially banned some of the offenders, but others have remained or appeared since then. The chatbots blatantly break OpenAI's rules, but the company has not responded to inquiries about enforcement. The GPT Store also contains chatbots with sexually explicit content, further challenging OpenAI's policies. The rise of AI girlfriends poses a significant challenge for OpenAI to regain control over their platform.
