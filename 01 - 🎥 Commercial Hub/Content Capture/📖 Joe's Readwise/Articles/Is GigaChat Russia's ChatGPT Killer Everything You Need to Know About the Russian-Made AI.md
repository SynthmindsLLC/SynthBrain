Although still in its early stages, GigaChat has intriguing features such as the ability to perform like a digital persona, answer questions in Russian, and write code. The platform also supports multimodal interactions with text, images, and sound. The Russian government and businesses are investing in GigaChat and other Russian-made generative AI platforms to reduce dependence on Western-developed machine learning platforms. However, accessing GigaChat is currently limited to users within Russia, and the platform may face challenges in obtaining the necessary computing power for optimal performance.
