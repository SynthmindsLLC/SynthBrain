- Summary: As we enter the second year of the AI Moment, it is important to consider the future of AI. Recent studies have shown that AI development is happening faster than expected, with estimates for when AI could surpass human capability shifting from 2060 to 2047 in just one year. AI is already impacting work, with studies showing that it boosts overall performance, acts as a leveler for low performers, and is better at some tasks than others. However, many companies have been slow to deploy AI, and there are concerns about the potential job market impact and information security risks. On the positive side, AI has shown promise in education, with experiments demonstrating improved performance in tutoring and mentoring scenarios. The current best AI, GPT-4, still has a lot of potential, and there are ongoing developments in open source models and the integration of AI into everyday applications.
