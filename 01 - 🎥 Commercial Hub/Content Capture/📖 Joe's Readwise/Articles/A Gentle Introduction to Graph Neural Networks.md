- Category: #articles
- Summary: This article is a beginner's guide to graph neural networks (GNNs), which are neural networks that operate on graph data. The article explains what kind of data is best represented as a graph, such as social networks, images, and text. It also discusses the three types of prediction tasks on graphs: graph-level, node-level, and edge-level. The article explains how GNNs work and how to represent graphs to be compatible with neural networks. It also explores more advanced concepts such as learning edge representations, using global representations, and designing new mechanisms for graph information to be executed and propagated in a GNN.
