- Summary: Students and alumni at Harvard University are seeking new ways to address various issues at the institution. Pro-Palestinian and Muslim students have accused the school of failing to protect them from harassment and racism, while alumni are pressuring for a focus on free speech and diversity. A legal group representing 14 students has asked the Department of Education to investigate the school for discrimination. Meanwhile, alumni are racing to collect signatures to join Harvard's Board of Overseers and steer the school in a different direction. The fallout from the conflict in Gaza has divided the campus, with protests, charges of antisemitism, and criticism of the university's response.
