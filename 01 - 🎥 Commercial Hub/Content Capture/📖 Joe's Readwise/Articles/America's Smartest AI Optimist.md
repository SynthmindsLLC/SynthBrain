- Category: #articles
- Summary: Ted Underwood, an English professor at the University of Illinois, is hailed as America's smartest AI optimist. Underwood believes that artificial intelligence, particularly large language models (LLMs), can help uncover new insights into literature and human culture. By using LLMs to analyze vast amounts of text, Underwood aims to understand plot, character motivation, and even quantify elements like suspense in literature. However, he acknowledges the need to address biases and ensure that LLMs are trained on diverse and curated datasets. Underwood emphasizes the importance of the humanities being involved in shaping the development and use of AI technology to prevent it from being solely determined by Silicon Valley and corporate interests.
