# Child Abuse Images Found in AI Training Data

## Metadata
- Author: [[Ryan Heath]]
- Date: 2023-12-20
- Full Title: Child Abuse Images Found in AI Training Data
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Stanford researchers have found over 1,000 child sexual abuse images in an AI dataset used to train image generation tools. This discovery highlights the issue of AI developers not having clear records of the material used to train their models. The presence of even a small selection of these illegal images can lead to the creation of numerous new synthetic images of child abuse. The report identifies a popular open-source dataset called LAION-5B as the source of these images. While some developers have flagged the existence of these images since April, there is likely more child sexual abuse material to be discovered in AI training data.

## Highlights
