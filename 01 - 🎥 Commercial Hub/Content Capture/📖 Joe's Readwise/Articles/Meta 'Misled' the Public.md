- But the complaint said an internal user survey report from Meta known as the Tracking Reach of Integrity Problems Survey (TRIPS) — which an internal memo at [Instagram](https://www.businessinsider.com/how-instagram-used-to-look-2011-2016-2016-6) once called "our north star, ground-truth measurement" — reported significantly higher levels of hate speech just months earlier.
