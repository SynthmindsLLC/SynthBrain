- Summary: Inflection, after raising $1.3 billion for personalized AI, is now being absorbed by its major investor, Microsoft. Co-founders are moving to Microsoft, leaving behind a diminished company that failed to keep pace with AI advancements from competitors like OpenAI and Google's Gemini. The shift to an AI studio business model may be challenging, and Microsoft's involvement raises questions about their intentions in the tech industry. The situation highlights the power dynamics and strategies of legacy tech companies in the AI field, showing the harsh realities of competition and investment in the tech world.

## Highlights
- In June 2023, Inflection announced it had [raised $1.3 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/29/inflection-ai-lands-1-3b-investment-to-build-more-personal-ai/) to build what it called “more personal AI.” The lead investor was Microsoft. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hscnws2fym8xw2d6zwnbh1ny))
- Co-founders Mustafa Suleyman and Karén Simonyan [will go to Microsoft,](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2024/03/19/mustafa-suleyman-deepmind-and-inflection-co-founder-joins-microsoft-to-lead-copilot/) where the former will head up the newly formed Microsoft AI division, along with “several members” of their team as Microsoft put it — or “most of the staff,” as [Bloomberg reports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-03-19/microsoft-hires-deepmind-co-founder-suleyman-to-run-consumer-ai) it. Reid Hoffman will stay behind with new CEO Sean White to try to salvage what’s left of the company, which, I feel I have to repeat, raised $1.3 billion dollars 9 months ago and [$225 million in mid-2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/13/inflection-ai-led-by-linkedin-and-deepmind-co-founders-raises-225m-to-transform-computer-human-interactions/). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hscnxj204d0hh11d5a0716n6))
- The new focus, on an “AI studio business, where custom generative AI models are crafted, tested and fine tuned for commercial customers,” probably would have been fine to focus on a year or two ago, but I suspect that at this point they will be fighting over crumbs.
