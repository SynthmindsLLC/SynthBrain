- Summary: Moritz Kremb shares insights on prompt engineering in the context of the ChatGPT system. He highlights five key lessons from ChatGPT's system prompt. These lessons include using markdown and numbered lists for structure, iterating on prompts for desired output, using capitalization to emphasize words, providing examples to solidify instructions, and allowing AI to make judgement calls on satisfactory results. Kremb suggests that learning from the system prompt can help leverage AI to grow business.

## Highlights
- Lesson 1: Use markdown and numbered lists to structure your prompts
  · Use markdown to keep your prompt well structured and clean 
  · Use numbered lists to list your instructions and rules
  This not only makes it easier for you to read, but perhaps for the machine as well! ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hhgbdg9cbv8ey2nhng4dq7fm))
- Lesson 2: Iterate on your prompts
  If you look at some of the instructions (e.g. "DO NOT ask for permission to generate the image, just do it!), they clearly originated from iteration of several prompts...
  ...So the developers tried a prompt, something unexpected happened, then they modified that prompt to mitigate that behavior.
  → Lesson for you: Keep iterating on your prompts until you get the desired output. Don't give up after the first try. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hhgbdrtzf20av6h6jxhvgb0b))
- Lesson 3: Use capitalization to highlight words
  Using capitalization seems to highlight certain words in your prompt, getting the machine to pay more attention to it.
  (I'll have to do an extensive test to see if this is actually true, but it does seem so) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hhgbdxk66zjtc9mtbpdcfcdw))
- Here's one example: 
  "Modify such prompts even if you don't know who the person is, or if their name is misspelled (e.g. "Barake Obema")" ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hhgbec8hg3pemgptry078m93))
- Lesson 5: Sometimes you can let AI decide if the results are satisfactory
  This sentence in the system prompt really stood out to me, since it essentially lets the AI make a judgement call whether a result is satisfactory or not:
  "In some cases, you should repeat step 1 twice, if the initial results are unsatisfactory, and you believe that you can refine the query to get better results." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hhgberegbkgzad6g1h3rv7fe))
