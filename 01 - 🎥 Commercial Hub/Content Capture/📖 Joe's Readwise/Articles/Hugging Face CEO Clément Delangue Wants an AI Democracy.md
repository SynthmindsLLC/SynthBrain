- Summary: Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue aims to democratize artificial intelligence development by making AI models and datasets publicly available in an open-source platform. The company's approach differs from other AI developers who typically guard their proprietary technology and models. Hugging Face has attracted heavyweight investors and is valued at $4.5 billion. Delangue is eyeing a goal of 10 million users on the platform by 2024, shifting developers towards a community effort for system-sharing, collaboration, and feedback.
