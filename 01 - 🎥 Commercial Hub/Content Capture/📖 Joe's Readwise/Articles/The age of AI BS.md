## Metadata
- Author: [[Emily Stewart]]
- Date: 2024-03-27
- Full Title: The age of AI BS
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The text discusses how companies are exaggerating their use of artificial intelligence (AI) to attract investors, a phenomenon referred to as "AI washing." Some companies are making false claims about their AI capabilities, while others are hyping up the potential of AI without concrete evidence. Even tech giants like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft are cautioned about overselling their AI capabilities to clients. The text highlights the uncertainty and exaggeration surrounding AI technology and its potential impact on society. Overall, there is a belief that there is a lot of money to be made in AI, leading to overselling and exaggeration in the industry.
