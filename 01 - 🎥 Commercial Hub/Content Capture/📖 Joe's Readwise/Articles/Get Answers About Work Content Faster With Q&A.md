- Summary: Q&A is a feature in Notion that allows users to quickly find answers to work-related questions within their workspace. It can surface relevant content, summarize information, and provide answers to complex queries. Q&A is designed to save time and improve productivity by reducing the need to search for information or rely on colleagues for answers. It can be used by individuals in various roles, such as engineers, product teams, designers, and marketing teams, to unblock themselves and access the information they need. To maximize the value of Q&A, it is recommended to have a comprehensive and connected workspace that includes key information and resources.
