- Summary: In the last two weeks at OpenAI, there have been significant developments, including a Sora demo that impressed but failed basic science checks, the departure of respected researcher Andrej Karpathy, and a mysterious ChatGPT meltdown. Additionally, competitors like Google and Mistral are catching up with GPT-4, casting doubts on the robustness of certain AI processes. OpenAI is facing legal challenges with new lawsuits filed against them, raising concerns about copyright issues and a broader class action. Amidst these events, the organization is still awaiting new board members, with much to contemplate for those who will join in the future.
