## Highlights
- [SydeLabs](https://www.sydelabs.ai/), a California-based startup, is solving this challenge with a real-time, intent-based firewall. The startup today raised $2.5 million in seed funding from RTP Global, Picus Capital and other angels. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckmqzfxp0645dzg2shwxfg))
- SydeLabs has developed three key AI-focused products: SydeBox, SydeGuard and SydeComply. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckndsk228cxk5j4fh2jmrq))
- The first, currently available in beta, is a self-service red-teaming solution that allows teams to stress-test their AI apps and models to find if they are susceptible to any vulnerabilities. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htcknvkxzs51qw1rd3ggqrrn))
- other two, slated to go live soon, are aimed at providing intent-based protection in real-time and highlighting/fixing the gaps in AI systems that could lead to compliance issues in different parts of the world. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckp1znmszw8x4k9w0ws1n5))
- During the beta of SydeBox, we have been able to uncover various vulnerabilities like training data leaks, system prompt leaks, prompt injections, safety alignment bypasses, etc. On the other hand, our real-time intent-based protection system SydeGuard can detect and prevent prompt injections, denial of wallet attacks, data leak attempts, system prompt leaking attempts, misuse of AI systems, etc,” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckprcf4f56g9awr3q33beg))
- The red teaming solution uses an AI agent that crafts test attacks (based on in-house research and public data) and an LLM that detects the success of the attacks based on the response of the target system. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckq1tt90755jk8fp8qwj7v))
- SydeGuard uses a combination of proprietary models that identify the intent of the end user across various Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckqc0qgrewtj8d1pc2gx63))
- models look at every single prompt for potential threats and give a risk score to the prompt, the user’s session and the user itself. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckqk9ztgxx4cx4ccg2qe8h))
- it shares the data with enterprise security teams while giving them options to either block the prompt/user, monitor and give a standard response or send it to a honeypot to deceive the attacker with dummy data. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckr07fp7ybdt92crwe25v5))
- In the long run, it intends to offer the red teaming solution free of cost to enterprises looking to detect vulnerabilities in their application and monetize SydeGuard using a consumption-based model. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htcks1k8vt1knxqv9hecbnvq))

[Skyflow](https://venturebeat.com/ai/skyflow-launches-privacy-vault-for-building-llms/), a startup providing enterprises with a privacy vault for secure AI development, has also raised [$30 million](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240328765469/en/Skyflow-Raises-30-Million-led-by-Khosla-Ventures-for-Data-Privacy-in-AI-and-Global-Data-Residency) in a fresh round. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01htckspfv1jb636atzrsb0v1q))
