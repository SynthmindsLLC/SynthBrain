- Full Title: The Next Phase of AI Is Here — And It's Not Looking Pretty
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The text discusses the challenges faced by AI startups in a new phase where the initial hype and optimism are giving way to uncertainties and struggles. It highlights the struggles of companies like Inflection AI, Stability AI, and Cohere, which have faced setbacks such as key personnel leaving, difficulties in generating revenue, and challenges in attracting consumer interest. The text also points out that the dominance of Big Tech companies and the need for decentralized AI are complicating factors for these startups. Overall, it suggests that the landscape for AI startups is becoming more challenging and less certain than previously anticipated.
