- Full Title: Sorry, but FunSearch Probably Isn’t a Milestone in Scientific Discovery
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Google DeepMind's recent paper on math, titled "FunSearch," has garnered enthusiastic attention in the media. However, the paper's claims have been oversold by DeepMind's PR team. While the paper does present a clever use of AI in solving a math problem, it is unlikely to have significant implications in fields such as cancer research or battery development. The solution to the problem involved the use of a large language model (LLM) in a narrow and prescribed manner, requiring human mathematicians to write problem-specific code. Additionally, this is not the first time AI has been used in mathematics.
