We know only that in the earliest Greek poets a new point of view dawned, never dreamed of in the world before them, but never to leave the world after them. ([Location 168](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B071LDBDTM&location=168))
- That is the miracle of Greek mythology—a humanized world, men freed from the paralyzing fear of an omnipotent Unknown.
