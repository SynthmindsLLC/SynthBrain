- Memo Reading Meeting Structure at Amazon
  Summary:
  Meetings at Amazon and Blue Origin start with a silent study hall where participants read a six-page memo.
  This ensures that everyone is on the same page and allows for a more elevated and effective discussion. The purpose of this approach is to prevent people from skipping or bluffing about reading the memo, and instead, to carve out time for them to go through the document before the meeting.
