- Challenges of AI Latency and Cost in Cloud Services
  Summary:
  The AI industry faces significant challenges with latency and cost in cloud services, making it difficult to build production quality code due to the excessive time taken for requests and the economically untenable pricing for a million tokens on platforms like Amazon, Google, and OpenAI.
  This situation creates an opportunity for new cloud services to emerge with millisecond latency and more affordable pricing, potentially leading to a thousandfold increase in market capability. Such advancements could result in the proliferation of open source models, increased pressure on proprietary models, and a reallocation of revenue in the industry, potentially affecting the market cap of companies like Nvidia and OpenAI.
  The existing revenue composition of enterprises in the industry, heavily reliant on subscriptions and AWS-like services, is deemed unsustainable due to high costs and slow performance.
  Therefore, for the industry to thrive, cloud services need to become extremely cost-effective.
  Moreover, the capability to enable a thousandfold decrease in cost will be crucial, potentially disrupting major players like Azure and AWS, as startups are working on proprietary hardware solutions to address these challenges.
