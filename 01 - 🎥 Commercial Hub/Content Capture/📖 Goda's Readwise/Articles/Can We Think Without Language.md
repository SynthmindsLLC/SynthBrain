- This language system seems to be distinct from regions that are linked to our ability to plan, remember, reminisce on past and future, reason in social situations, experience empathy, make moral decisions, and construct one’s self-image. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hsgjh6388c9xa5nwx21sbqsc))

- We have shown that damage to the language system within an adult human brain leaves most other cognitive functions intact. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hsgjmdr1rqp4kkd0e17hqbyx))
