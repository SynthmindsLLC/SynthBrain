## Highlights
- Arthur C. Clarke, a renowned British writer and inventor, is credited with inventing the concept of geosynchronous satellites. His 1945 paper, "Extra-terrestrial Relays," outlined the principles of global broadcasting via communication satellites, which later became a reality in the 1960s. Clarke's idea of communication satellites in geostationary orbit, now known as the 'Clarke Orbit' in his honor, laid the foundation for modern satellite communication systems ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hrf1g73wytwdwd3gpw0dqcfz))
