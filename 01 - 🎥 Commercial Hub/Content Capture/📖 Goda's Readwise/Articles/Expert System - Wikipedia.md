## Highlights
- In [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) (AI), an **expert system** is a computer system emulating the decision-making ability of a human [expert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expert).[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expert_system#cite_note-Jackson1998-1) Expert systems are designed to solve complex problems by [reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_reasoning_system) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j2kqsb37f7m22shfe4bavcgk))
- An expert system is divided into two subsystems: 1) a *[knowledge base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_base)*, which represents facts and rules; and 2) an *[inference engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inference_engine)*, which applies the rules to the known facts to deduce new facts, and can include explaining and debugging abilities.
