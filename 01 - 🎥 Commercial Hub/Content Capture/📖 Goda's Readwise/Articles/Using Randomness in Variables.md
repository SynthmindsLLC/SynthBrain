## Highlights
- Programmers often bound the randomness of variable values to analyze and predict real-world processes, such as stock prices, the spread of infectious disease, or trends in a social network. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j8f7x3h62d818bj9jk7fesc2))
- The outcomes of complex processes are difficult to predict mathematically.
  Sometimes, these processes may seem completely random. However, there are patterns and bounds to their randomness.
  So, by bounding the randomness of key variable values, programmers can help predict which outcomes are more likely than others. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01j8f7xvq3yageak5kcejm0na1))
