## Highlights
- 82% of parents said they wanted help in evaluating whether or not new AI products, like ChatGPT, were safe for their kids to use. Only 40% said they knew of any reliable resources that would help them to make those determinations. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hh88n8rcdwfcja6mpwb3623v))
- Common Sense warned that the false information AI produces could shape users’ worldviews and make it even more difficult to separate fact from fiction. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hh88sq9f7cdhmccpfaejs24h))
- The only AI products to receive good reviews were [Ello’s](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/ai-ratings/ello) AI reading tutor and book delivery service, [Khanmingo](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/ai-ratings/khanmigo) (from Khan Academy), and [Kyron Learning’s](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/ai-ratings/kyron-learning) AI tutor  — all three being AI products designed for educational purposes.
