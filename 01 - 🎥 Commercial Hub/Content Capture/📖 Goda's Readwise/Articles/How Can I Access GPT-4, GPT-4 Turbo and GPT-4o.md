## Highlights
- GPT-4o is OpenAI's new flagship model that can reason across audio, vision, and text in real time. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwbynzfr2ckface720amn9w))
- Specifically, GPT-4o will be available in ChatGPT Free, Plus, and Team (with Enterprise coming soon) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwbz8t05qy9fsx7zj7r7gbf))
- GPT-4o has the same high intelligence but is faster, cheaper, and has higher rate limits than GPT-4 Turbo. Specifically: ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwbzr3eg9qy013tt60ewsje))
- Pricing: GPT-4o is 50% cheaper than GPT-4 Turbo, coming in at $5/M input and $15/M output tokens). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwbzxvzf3prfhkqsm52av59))
- Speed: GPT-4o is 2x as fast as GPT-4 Turbo. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwc07w8d655wqcfxa4vhzcr))
- GPT-4o currently has a context window of 128k and has a knowledge cut-off date of October 2023. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwc0jft3hd7xma5nw6fcw2d))
- GPT-4o is available to anyone with an OpenAI API account, and you can use this model in the [Chat Completions API,](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/text-generation/chat-completions-api) [Assistants API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/assistants), and [Batch API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/batch). Function calling and JSON mode are also supported by this model. You can also get started via the [Playground](https://platform.openai.com/playground). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwc15f8pfw0gn18j22820yy))
- As of May 13th 2024, Plus users will be able to send up to 80 messages every 3 hours on GPT-4o and up to 40 messages every 3 hours on GPT-4. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwc9xebnzgkady4nxr2y8gm))
- We may reduce the limit during peak hours to keep GPT-4 and GPT-4o accessible to the widest number of people. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwca6nzdjxszkvkqfc92q94))
- Please note that unused messages do not accumulate (i.e. if you wait 6 hours, you will not have 80 messages available to use for the next 3 hours on GPT-4). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwcakqk4k3kwyb14zy7d7ns))
