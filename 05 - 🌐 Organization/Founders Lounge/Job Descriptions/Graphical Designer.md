**Job Title:** Graphic Designer

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a forward-thinking AI company that has recently transitioned from an LLC to a C Corporation, with a strategic focus on AI education and responsible integration. We are dedicated to demystifying AI and making it accessible to a diverse audience, ensuring ethical use and transformative growth. Our core values include collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit. We are expanding our team and looking for a creative and innovative Graphic Designer to join us in shaping the future of AI.

**Role Summary:**
As a Graphic Designer at Synthminds, you will be responsible for creating compelling visual content that aligns with our brand identity and communicates our vision to a global audience. You will work closely with our marketing and content teams to produce graphics for digital channels, educational materials, and promotional campaigns.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design and produce graphic content for our websites (Synthminds.ai), social media platforms, and digital marketing campaigns.
- Collaborate with our Chief Commercial Officer and marketing team to enhance brand visibility and engagement through visually appealing designs.
- Create educational and promotional materials, including infographics, course catalogs, white papers, and presentations, to support our AI educational programs and services.
- Work with our content team to design visuals for our YouTube channels, blogs, and podcasts, ensuring consistency and quality across all media.
- Assist in the development of branding materials for events and workshops, including webinars, conferences, and bootcamps.
- Contribute to the visual storytelling of our AI insights, training, and solutions, including photorealistic AI marketing images.
- Provide design support for direct sales and email marketing efforts, creating templates and graphics that resonate with our target audience.

**Qualifications:**
- 1 year experience in Graphic Design, Visual Arts, or a related field.
- Proven graphic design experience with a strong portfolio showcasing a range of projects.
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and other graphic design software, as well as use of AI tools (such as Midjourney, Leonardo AI, etc.).
- Experience with video editing and motion graphics is a plus.
- Understanding of branding, layout, color theory, and typography in both digital and print media.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote environment.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to articulate design concepts and rationale.
- A passion for AI technology and a commitment to our core values.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- The opportunity to be part of a dynamic team that is at the forefront of AI innovation and education.
- A culture that values continuous learning, adaptation, and the exploration of new ideas.
- The chance to create impactful designs that will be seen by a global audience and contribute to the responsible integration of AI.

**How to Apply:**
Please submit your resume, cover letter, and a link to your portfolio to [careers@synthminds.ai](mailto:careers@synthminds.ai). In your cover letter, explain why you are passionate about AI and how your design skills can contribute to the mission of Synthminds.

Synthminds is an equal opportunity employer and strongly encourages applications from people of color, persons with disabilities, women, and LGBTQ+ applicants.
