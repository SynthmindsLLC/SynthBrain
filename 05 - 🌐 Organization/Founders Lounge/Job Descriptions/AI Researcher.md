**Job Title:** AI Research Analyst - Commercial Hub Team

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**  
Synthminds is a pioneering AI company that has established itself as a leader in AI education, integration, and responsible AI practices. With a history of innovation and a commitment to demystifying AI, we are dedicated to empowering individuals and organizations to harness the transformative power of AI technologies. Our core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit guide our mission to educate a diverse audience and shape the future of AI.

**Position Overview:**  
We are seeking a highly motivated and skilled AI Research Analyst to join our dynamic Commercial Hub Team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for conducting in-depth research on various AI topics to support the development of engaging and informative content across our digital channels. This role is critical in ensuring that Synthminds remains at the forefront of AI advancements and continues to provide cutting-edge educational resources to our community.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Conduct comprehensive research on the latest AI trends, tools, applications, and ethical considerations.
- Collaborate with content creators, including our renowned YouTube influencer and AI educators, to develop white papers, course materials, and other educational content.
- Analyze and synthesize complex AI concepts into accessible and actionable insights for a diverse audience.
- Support the Commercial Hub Team in identifying new AI topics for webinars, podcasts, and speaking engagements.
- Maintain a deep understanding of AI advancements to inform strategic decisions and content development.
- Engage with AI communities and forums to gather information and stay updated on industry developments.
- Assist in the creation of AI-related proposals and presentations for conferences and client engagements.

**Qualifications:**

- 1 year of experinece in Computer Science, AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, or a related field.
- Proven experience in AI research or a similar role, with a strong portfolio of research projects or published papers.
- Excellent analytical and critical thinking skills, with the ability to distill complex information into clear, concise content.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills, comfortable working in a remote team environment.
- Familiarity with AI educational platforms and a passion for continuous learning and teaching in the AI space.
- Experience with AI tools and platforms, such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and large language models, is highly desirable.
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects simultaneously with a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail.

**What Synthminds Offers:**

- A remote and flexible work environment.
- An opportunity to work with a team of leading strategists, speakers, instructors, creators, and developers within the AI space.
- Access to a broad network of AI professionals and educational partners.

- Professional development opportunities in a company committed to continuous learning and adaptation.
