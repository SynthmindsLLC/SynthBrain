**Job Title: Director of Business Development at Synthminds**

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI firm that specializes in AI education and integration. We are a team of strategists, speakers, instructors, creators, and developers dedicated to demystifying AI and empowering clients with cutting-edge AI tools and business applications. Our suite of No-code AI courses on the Uplimit platform has received the highest ratings, and we are recognized as top AI experts on Upwork. As we transition from an LLC to a C Corp, we are realigning our focus towards AI education and responsible integration, guided by our core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit.

**Position Overview:**
The Director of Business Development will play a critical role in driving Synthminds' growth and expansion. This individual will be responsible for identifying new business opportunities, building and maintaining client relationships, and developing strategies to enter new markets. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in AI, a passion for education, and a proven track record in strategic business development.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and execute a comprehensive business development strategy to expand Synthminds' presence in targeted customer segments and channels.
- Cultivate relationships with mid-sized businesses, adult learners, AI enthusiasts, entrepreneurs, and other key customer segments to drive revenue growth.
- Partner with educational institutions and other organizations to enhance our AI educational offerings and integration services.
- Identify and secure sponsorship and partnership opportunities that align with our core values and strategic goals.
- Work closely with the marketing team to leverage digital channels, content marketing, and direct sales efforts to generate leads and increase brand visibility.
- Represent Synthminds at industry events, conferences, and workshops to network and promote our services.
- Collaborate with the leadership team to refine the organizational structure and operational processes to support business development activities.
- Manage a team of business development professionals, setting goals, and ensuring alignment with the company's strategic direction.

**Qualifications:**
- An advanced degree (MBA or equivalent) is preferred.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in business development, preferably in the AI or tech industry.
- Demonstrated ability to develop and implement successful business development strategies.
- Strong understanding of AI technologies and their applications in business.
- Excellent communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.
- Proven leadership abilities and experience managing a team.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and adapt to changing market conditions.
- Commitment to Synthminds' core values and a passion for AI education and responsible integration.

**What We Offer:**
- A dynamic and supportive work environment with a team of dedicated professionals.
- The opportunity to be at the forefront of AI innovation and education.
- Opportunities for professional growth and development.
- A culture that values collaboration, creativity, and an explorative spirit.
