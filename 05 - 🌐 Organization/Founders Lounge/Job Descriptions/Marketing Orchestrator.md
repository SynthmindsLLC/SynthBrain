**Job Title:** Marketing Orchestrator

**Location:** Flexible/Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a forward-thinking AI company that specializes in AI insights, training, and solutions. With a history of innovation and a pivot towards AI education and responsible integration, we are on a mission to demystify AI and make it accessible to a diverse audience. Our core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit guide us as we empower individuals and organizations to ethically integrate AI into their workflows and daily lives.

**Role Overview:**
We are seeking a dynamic Marketing Orchestrator to join our Commercial Hub and play a pivotal role in amplifying our brand and educational message across multiple channels. The ideal candidate will be a strategic thinker with a passion for AI and a talent for creating synergies across various marketing platforms. You will work closely with our Chief Commercial Officer, Goda, and leverage her influence as a YouTube personality to broaden Synthminds' reach.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and execute a cohesive marketing strategy that aligns with Synthminds' mission and strategic goals.
- Orchestrate marketing campaigns across digital channels, including our website, social media (LinkedIn, Instagram, potentially Facebook), and online forums and communities.
- Collaborate with educational and technology partners to co-create content and leverage sponsorship opportunities.
- Manage content marketing efforts, including YouTube channels, blogs, podcasts, webinars, and workshops.
- Drive direct sales through inbound marketing with HubSpot, engagement on Upwork, and networking at AI conferences.
- Organize events and workshops, such as free intro to AI webinars, prompt engineering conferences, and AI bootcamps.
- Oversee email marketing strategies for Synthminds, aiming to create an "insiders" community.
- Analyze market trends and adjust marketing strategies accordingly.
- Measure and report on the performance of marketing campaigns, gaining insights and assessing against goals.

**Qualifications:**
- At least 1 year of experience in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field.
- Proven experience in marketing, with a strong understanding of digital marketing channels and strategies.
- Experience with AI, technology, or education sectors is highly preferred.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, evolving environment and adapt strategies quickly.
- Strong analytical skills and data-driven thinking.
- Familiarity with marketing and project management tools such as HubSpot, Kajabi, Gumroad, and Notion.
- Creative mindset with an explorative spirit, aligned with Synthminds' core values.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- An opportunity to be at the forefront of AI advancements and education.
- A collaborative and innovative team culture.
- Professional development and continuous learning opportunities.
- A chance to contribute to a company with a strong commitment to diversity, inclusion, and ethical AI practices.
