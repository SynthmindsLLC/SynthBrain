**Job Title:** Community Manager at Synthminds

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a forward-thinking AI company that has transitioned from providing bespoke AI solutions to focusing on AI education and responsible integration. With a commitment to our core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit, we aim to demystify AI and make it accessible to a diverse audience. Our team is led by Wes Shields, a recognized leader in the AI space, and we are proud to offer a suite of services including AI insights, training, and custom solutions.

**Job Description:**
We are seeking a dynamic and engaging Community Manager to join our team at Synthminds. The ideal candidate will be passionate about AI and have a knack for building and nurturing online communities. You will be the voice of Synthminds, connecting with AI enthusiasts, learners, and professionals across various platforms, fostering a collaborative environment, and driving meaningful conversations around AI education and integration.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and implement community engagement strategies that align with Synthminds' mission and core values.
- Manage and grow our online presence on social media platforms, including LinkedIn, Instagram, and potentially Facebook, as well as our Discord community.
- Facilitate and moderate discussions, ensuring a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members.
- Organize and host events such as webinars, workshops, and office hours to engage the community and provide value.
- Collaborate with the marketing team to create and distribute engaging content across our channels, including YouTube, blogs, and podcasts.
- Serve as a liaison between the community and Synthminds, gathering feedback, and providing insights to the team to help shape future offerings.
- Support the launch and promotion of new courses, products, and partnerships.
- Work closely with our educational partners, including Uplimit and Yeshiva University Global, to co-create content and programs that benefit our community.
- Advocate for diversity and inclusion within the community, actively engaging in initiatives to involve more women and people of color in the AI field.

**Qualifications:**
- Proven experience in community management, social media, or a related field.
- Strong understanding of AI concepts and enthusiasm for the industry.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage and inspire a diverse audience.
- Experience with content creation and digital marketing tools.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a remote, cross-functional team.
- Familiarity with online community platforms, content management systems, and analytics tools.
- Commitment to continuous learning and staying updated on the latest AI trends and developments.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- An opportunity to be at the forefront of AI education and responsible integration.
- A collaborative and supportive team culture.
- Professional development opportunities in a rapidly growing company.
- A chance to make a significant impact in shaping the future of AI and its community.

**How to Apply:**
If you are passionate about AI and community building, we would love to hear from you. Please send your resume, a cover letter explaining why you are the perfect fit for this role, and any relevant work samples or case studies to careers@synthminds.ai.

Synthminds is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
