**Job Title:** Social Media Manager

**Location:** Remote

**Department:** Commercial Hub

**Reports To:** Marketing Orchestrator

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI education and integration company, dedicated to demystifying AI and making it accessible to a diverse audience. With a strategic focus on AI education, responsible integration, and community building, we are committed to empowering individuals and organizations to ethically harness the transformative power of AI. Our team is led by industry experts and thought leaders who are passionate about innovation, excellence, and continuous learning.

**Job Summary:**
We are seeking a creative and strategic Social Media Manager to join our Commercial Hub, reporting to the Marketing Orchestrator. The ideal candidate will be responsible for amplifying Synthminds' presence across various social media platforms, engaging with our community, and driving our mission to educate and integrate AI into everyday life. You will play a crucial role in shaping our digital narrative, fostering relationships, and building a community around our core values and services.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Implement a cohesive social media strategy that aligns with Synthminds' business goals and brand voice.
- Manage and grow our social media profiles on platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, and potentially Facebook, ensuring consistent and engaging content.
- Collaborate with our Chief Commercial Officer, Goda, to leverage her YouTube influence and enhance brand visibility.
- Coordinate with the Content Team to produce and share high-quality, informative, and engaging multimedia content, including videos, podcasts, and blog posts.
- Monitor social media trends and insights to optimize content and campaigns for maximum engagement and conversion.
- Engage with our community by responding to comments, messages, and fostering discussions that align with our commitment to AI education and responsible integration.
- Work closely with the Marketing Team to coordinate marketing efforts across all channels and ensure a unified brand message.
- Participate in AI conferences and events, promoting Synthminds' participation and sharing key takeaways with our online community.
- Support the launch and promotion of new educational offerings, partnerships, and initiatives through compelling social media campaigns.

**Qualifications:**
- At least 1 year of experience in Marketing, Communications, or a related field.
- Proven experience as a Social Media Manager or similar role, with a strong portfolio showcasing successful campaigns and community engagement.
- Excellent understanding of social media platforms, analytics tools, and content management systems.
- Strong copywriting, design and communication skills, with the ability to craft messages that resonate with diverse audiences (AI use preferred).
- Creative mindset with a passion for AI and technology.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote, fast-paced environment.
- Familiarity with the AI industry and educational platforms is a plus.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- Opportunity to work with a dynamic team of AI experts and thought leaders.
- Access to cutting-edge AI tools and educational resources.
- A culture that values collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit.
- A chance to be part of a company that is shaping the future of AI education and integration.
