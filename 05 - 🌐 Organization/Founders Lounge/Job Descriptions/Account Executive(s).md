**Job Title:** Account Executive at Synthminds

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI company that has established itself as a leader in the integration and education of artificial intelligence technologies. With a history of bespoke AI solution development and a strategic pivot towards AI education and responsible integration, we are on a mission to empower individuals and organizations to ethically harness the power of AI. Our core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit guide our operations and relationships. As we transition to a C Corporation, we are refining our organizational structure and enhancing our operational efficiency to support our future growth and expansion plans.

**Job Description:**
We are seeking a dynamic and results-driven Account Executive to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing client relationships, identifying new business opportunities, and driving revenue growth. You will play a pivotal role in expanding our footprint in the AI space, contributing positively to the broader societal impacts of AI.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and maintain strong relationships with mid-sized businesses, adult learners, AI enthusiasts, entrepreneurs, and key partners.
- Identify and pursue new business opportunities, with an eye towards future global expansion.
- Present and sell Synthminds' AI integration services, educational programs, and custom solutions to potential clients.
- Work closely with our marketing team to coordinate marketing efforts across various channels, including digital, content marketing, events, and direct sales.
- Collaborate with our educational and technology partners to enhance service offerings and client satisfaction.
- Provide packages of tailored AI insights, training, and solutions based on client needs, ensuring alignment with Synthminds' value propositions.
- Manage the entire sales cycle from prospecting to closing deals, including follow-up and support.
- Assist clients in applying for and securing funding for AI integration when necessary.
- Contribute to the company's revenue streams by meeting or exceeding sales targets.
- Provide feedback from clients to our product and service development teams to ensure continuous improvement.

**Qualifications:**
- Proven track record of success in sales, account management, or business development, preferably in the AI, tech, or SaaS sectors.
- Strong understanding of AI technologies and their business applications.
- Excellent communication, negotiation, and presentation skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative team.
- Adaptability and willingness to learn in a rapidly evolving industry.
- Commitment to Synthminds' core values and ethical AI practices.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A chance to be part of a company at the forefront of AI advancements.
- Opportunities for professional growth and development in the AI field.
- A collaborative and innovative work environment.
- Competitive salary with performance-based incentives.
- Access to cutting-edge AI tools and platforms for professional use.
