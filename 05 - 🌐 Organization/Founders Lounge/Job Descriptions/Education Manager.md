**Job Title:** Education Manager

**Location:** Flexible/Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI company that has established itself as a leader in the field of artificial intelligence education and integration. With a focus on demystifying AI and making it accessible to a diverse audience, Synthminds offers a suite of services that empower individuals and organizations to harness the transformative power of AI technologies. As we continue to grow and evolve, we are seeking a passionate and experienced Education Manager to join our dynamic team.

**Job Description:**
The Education Manager at Synthminds will play a critical role in shaping the future of AI education. You will be responsible for overseeing the development and delivery of our comprehensive AI educational programs, ensuring they meet the highest standards of quality and relevance. Your work will directly contribute to our mission of empowering adult learners, upskilling professionals, and fostering a community of AI enthusiasts and early adopters.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and manage AI educational content and curriculum for various learning levels, from beginners to advanced practitioners.
- Lead the educational team in creating engaging and practical AI courses, workshops, and certification programs.
- Collaborate with key partners such as Uplimit, Yeshiva University Global, BSD Education, and Manning Publications to deliver high-quality AI education.
- Oversee the integration of the latest AI tools and applications into our educational offerings, ensuring they are practical and applicable in real-world scenarios.
- Manage and expand our digital presence through educational channels such as webinars, podcasts, and online courses.
- Foster relationships with educational institutions, tech companies, and other organizations aligned with our vision of AI education.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of educational programs and implement continuous improvement strategies.
- Support the company's commitment to diversity and inclusion by developing targeted educational initiatives and strategies.
- Stay abreast of the latest developments in AI and incorporate them into our educational materials and teaching methodologies.

**Qualifications:**
- A minimum of a Bachelor's degree in Education, Computer Science, AI, or a related field. Advanced degrees are preferred.
- Proven experience in educational program development and management, preferably in the field of AI or technology.
- Strong understanding of AI concepts, tools, and industry trends.
- Excellent leadership and team management skills.
- Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams and external partners.
- Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex AI concepts in an accessible manner.
- Experience with digital education platforms and content creation tools.
- Commitment to fostering an inclusive learning environment.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- An opportunity to be at the forefront of AI education and make a significant impact in the field.
- A collaborative and innovative company culture that values continuous learning and adaptation.
- Access to the latest AI technologies and educational tools.
