**Job Title:** UX/UI Designer at Synthminds

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI company that has carved a niche in developing tailored AI applications and is now strategically shifting towards AI education and responsible integration. With a commitment to our core values – collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit – we aim to demystify AI and make it accessible to a diverse audience. Our team is composed of leading strategists, speakers, instructors, creators, and developers within the AI space, and we are looking for a UX/UI Designer to join our dynamic team.

**Job Description:**
As a UX/UI Designer at Synthminds, you will be instrumental in shaping the user experience and visual design of our digital products, including educational platforms, AI tools, and company websites. You will work closely with our technical team, educational experts, and marketing team to create intuitive, user-friendly, and aesthetically pleasing interfaces that align with our mission to make AI accessible and understandable to all.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design and iterate on user interfaces for our digital products, ensuring they are intuitive, engaging, and aligned with our brand identity.
- Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into design solutions.
- Conduct user research and usability testing to gather feedback and inform design decisions.
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs for a variety of platforms, including web and mobile.
- Ensure a consistent design language across all digital touchpoints, maintaining a design system that facilitates scalability and collaboration.
- Work with developers to implement designs, providing guidance and ensuring design integrity throughout the development process.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest design trends, techniques, and technologies in the AI and education sectors.

**Qualifications:**
- A portfolio showcasing strong design skills and a user-centered design approach.
- Proficiency in design tools such as Sketch, Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, or equivalent.
- Experience with prototyping tools and an understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Strong understanding of UX principles, design frameworks, and best practices.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to articulate design decisions and iterate based on feedback.
- A passion for AI technology and education, with a desire to contribute to a team that is shaping the future of AI integration and learning.
- Bachelor’s degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

**Preferred Experience:**
- Experience designing for educational platforms or technology-focused products.
- Familiarity with AI concepts and the ability to design for complex, data-driven systems.
- Experience working in a fast-paced, startup environment.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A collaborative and innovative work environment where your contributions have a direct impact on the AI landscape.
- Opportunities for professional growth and development in the rapidly evolving field of AI.
- A commitment to diversity and inclusion, with initiatives to involve more women and people of color in AI.
