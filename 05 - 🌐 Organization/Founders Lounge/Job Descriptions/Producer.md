**Position Overview:**
We are seeking a dynamic and experienced Producer to join our Commercial Hub. This role requires a blend of creativity, organizational skills, and a passion for AI and technology.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Collaborate with our Chief Commercial Officer and marketing team to develop and execute content strategies that align with Synthminds' mission and goals.
- Manage the end-to-end production process for content creation, including planning, scheduling, budgeting, and resource allocation.
- Coordinate with internal teams, external partners, and influencers to ensure timely delivery of high-quality content.
- Oversee the technical aspects of content production, including filming, editing, sound design, and post-production.
- Support the marketing team in content distribution through Synthminds' digital channels, ensuring maximum reach and engagement.
- Monitor content performance and analytics to inform future content strategies and improvements.
- Maintain a content calendar and ensure all stakeholders are informed of production timelines and deliverables.

**Qualifications:**
- At least 1 year of experience in Communications, Media Production, Marketing, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in content production, preferably in a tech or AI-related industry.
- Strong project management skills with the ability to handle multiple projects simultaneously.
- Proficiency in content creation tools and software, including video editing, graphic design, and audio production.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams and external partners.
- A keen eye for detail and a commitment to producing high-quality, impactful content.
- Knowledge of AI and its applications in content creation is highly desirable.
- Ability to adapt to a fast-paced and evolving AI industry.
