**Job Title:** Instructional Designer

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI education and service provider, committed to demystifying AI and making it accessible to a diverse audience. With a strategic focus on AI education and responsible integration, we aim to empower individuals and organizations to understand, integrate, and ethically use AI in their workflows and daily lives. Our team is composed of industry leaders and educational experts dedicated to innovation, continuous improvement, and excellence in AI learning.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Collaborate with the Education manager, subject matter experts (SMEs) and the Chief Learning Officer to analyze course topics and develop relevant, engaging content that aligns with Synthminds' educational goals.
- Design and develop comprehensive asynchronous course modules for adult education, integrating multimedia elements and interactive components to enhance learning experiences.
- Create structured modules, and incorporate text content along with chatbots and multimedia that adheres to adult learning principles and engagement strategies.
- Work closely with the content team to produce instructional materials, including user guides, video tutorials, and demos.
- Apply instructional design theories and practices to ensure courses are effective, efficient, and appealing to learners.
- Provide insights on assessment methods and ensure that learning objectives are met through appropriate evaluation strategies.
- Stay up-to-date with current trends in AI and instructional design to ensure course content remains relevant and cutting-edge.
- Participate in the continuous improvement of course offerings by collecting and analyzing feedback for iterative development.

**Qualifications:**
- One year of experience in Instructional Design, Educational Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience in instructional design, particularly in developing courses for adult learners.
- Strong understanding of AI concepts and the ability to translate technical information into learner-friendly content.
- Proficiency in multimedia and e-learning technologies, as well as experience with learning management systems (LMS).
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with a remote team.
- Creative problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently in a fast-paced, evolving environment.

**What Synthminds Offers:**
- A remote and flexible work environment.
- Opportunities for professional development in the AI and educational technology space.
- A collaborative and inclusive company culture.
