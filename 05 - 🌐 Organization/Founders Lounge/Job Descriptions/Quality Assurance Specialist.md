**Job Title: Quality Assurance Specialist**

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**
Synthminds is a pioneering AI education and solution provider dedicated to demystifying artificial intelligence and making it accessible to a diverse audience. With a strategic focus on AI insights, training, and custom-built AI solutions, we are committed to empowering individuals and organizations to integrate and ethically use AI in their workflows and daily lives. Our team is composed of leading strategists, speakers, instructors, creators, and developers within the AI space.

**Job Description:**
We are seeking a meticulous and experienced Quality Assurance Specialist to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for ensuring the highest quality of our educational materials, including AI courses, workshops, webinars, and online content. As a QA Specialist, you will play a critical role in maintaining our reputation for excellence and reliability in AI education.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Conduct thorough reviews of educational content to ensure accuracy, clarity, and pedagogical soundness.
- Collaborate with content creators and instructional designers to identify and correct errors in text, graphics, and multimedia components.
- Develop and implement quality assurance protocols and checklists for consistent evaluation of educational materials.
- Provide detailed feedback and recommendations for content improvement to enhance learning outcomes.
- Ensure that all educational materials align with our core values, including collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit.
- Validate that course content meets the standards of our educational partners, such as Uplimit, Yeshiva University Global, BSD Education, and Manning Publications.
- Monitor and incorporate user feedback to continuously improve the quality and effectiveness of our educational offerings.
- Stay updated with the latest AI advancements and educational trends to ensure our content remains current and relevant.
- Work closely with the Chief Learning Officer and the educational team to uphold the integrity and quality of Synthminds' educational programs.

**Qualifications:**
- 1 year of experience in Instructional Design, Quality Assurance, or a related field.
- Proven experience in quality assurance within the educational content, e-learning industry or related field.
- Strong understanding of AI concepts and a passion for continuous learning in the field of artificial intelligence.
- Exceptional attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills to effectively work with a remote, multidisciplinary team.
- Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and meet tight deadlines.
- Proficiency in using digital tools and platforms for content management and quality control.
