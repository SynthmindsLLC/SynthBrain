**Location**: Remote / Various Locations as Required

With a history of innovation and a deep exploration into the potentials of artificial intelligence, we are now embarking on a strategic shift towards empowering individuals and organizations to understand, integrate, and ethically use AI. **Job Overview**:
We are seeking a passionate and knowledgeable AI Educator to join our Learning Lab team. As an AI Educator, you will be responsible for delivering engaging and informative AI educational content to a broad range of learners, from beginners to advanced practitioners. Your role will be pivotal in shaping the future of AI understanding and application, ensuring that our educational programs reflect the latest advancements and practical applications in the field.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Deliver comprehensive AI courses, workshops, and certification programs tailored to various skill levels.
- Stay abreast of the latest developments in AI to help continuously updating educational materials to ensure cutting-edge content.
- Utilize engaging teaching methodologies to deliver content in a manner that is both informative and enjoyable.
- Collaborate with our team of AI/ML Engineers, marketing experts, and entrepreneurs to integrate real-world applications into the curriculum.
- Provide coaching and training to adult learners, helping them to upskill and apply AI tools in their professional lives.
- Engage with the AI community through webinars, podcasts, and social media to promote our educational offerings and build a community around AI learning.
- Contribute to the creation of digital content, including online courses, webinars, and instructional videos, for platforms like Uplimit and our own channels.
- Participate in AI conferences and events as a representative of Synthminds, sharing insights and promoting our educational initiatives.

**Qualifications**:
- At least 1 year of experience educating about STEM..

- Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex information clearly and engagingly.
- Experience with digital education platforms and content creation tools.
