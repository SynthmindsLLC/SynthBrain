**Job Title:** Integration Manager at Synthminds

**Location:** Remote

**About Synthminds:**  
Synthminds is a pioneering AI company that specializes AI education and integration. We are recognized as leaders in AI strategy, development, and education, offering a suite of No-code AI courses and custom-built AI integration. Our mission is to empower clients with AI tools and knowledge, enabling transformative growth and productivity.

**Position Overview:**  
We are seeking a dynamic and experienced Integration Manager to lead the successful integration of AI solutions into our clients' business processes. The ideal candidate will be responsible for overseeing the implementation of AI tools and applications, ensuring seamless adoption and maximizing the value of AI within client organizations.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Collaborate with mid-sized businesses to assess AI needs and develop tailored integration strategies.
- Manage the end-to-end integration process, from initial planning to execution and post-implementation support.
- Coordinate with technical teams to customize AI integrations that align with client objectives and workflows.
- Facilitate training and knowledge transfer to client teams, ensuring they are equipped to leverage AI tools effectively.
- Monitor integration projects to ensure they are delivered on time, within scope, and budget.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for clients, maintaining strong relationships and providing expert guidance on AI integration.
- Work closely with the sales and marketing teams to identify integration opportunities and contribute to business development efforts.
- Stay abreast of the latest AI trends and technologies to continuously improve integration offerings.

**Qualifications:**

- 1 year of experience in software implementation, IT or support.
- Proven experience in managing AI or technology integration projects, with a strong understanding of AI applications and tools.
- Excellent project management skills, with the ability to lead cross-functional teams and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills, capable of conveying complex AI concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience in training and coaching teams on new technologies.
- A commitment to Synthminds' core values of collaboration, reliability, excellence, adaptability, transparency, and an explorative spirit.
- Familiarity with AI educational platforms and a passion for continuous learning and innovation in the AI space.

**What Synthminds Offers:**

- A collaborative and innovative work environment where your contributions have a direct impact on the company's success.
- Opportunities for professional growth and development in the rapidly evolving AI industry.
