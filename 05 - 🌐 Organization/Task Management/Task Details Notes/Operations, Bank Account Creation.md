Below is a task details, but taken from the internal chat, they are not in the task structure. Take the description below and format it nicely into tasks. First should be action items add them with a checkbox and tag a person resposnsible ("#PersonName thats how to tag people) and add a "#todo" (but without "") tag. Then you can break down the tasks. For your reference this is a note for a specific task in a kanban board:

## Task: Set Up a Multicurrency Business Bank Account

### Action Items:
- [x] #DanRosenbaum #todo Obtain EIN number for the business.
- [x] #UrosPesic #todo Research and identify potential business banks that offer multicurrency accounts.
- [x] #DanRosenbaum #todo Compare the fees and services of the shortlisted banks.
- [x] #UrosPesic #todo Check the requirements for opening a business bank account with the chosen bank.
- [x] #WilliamShields #todo Make a decision on which bank to proceed with based on the gathered information.

### Task Breakdown:

#### Obtain EIN Number
- Assigned to: #DanRosenbaum
- Description: Secure an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS, which is necessary for banking and tax purposes.

#### Research Multicurrency Business Banks
- Assigned to: #UrosPesic
- Description: Find banks that offer business accounts with multicurrency features, allowing us to hold cash in various currencies without incurring conversion fees.

#### Compare Bank Fees and Services
- Assigned to: #DanRosenbaum
- Description: Analyze the costs associated with each potential bank, including any service charges, conversion rates, and additional benefits they may provide.

#### Check Account Opening Requirements
- Assigned to: #UrosPesic
- Description: Determine what documents and information are required to open a business bank account with our preferred bank.

#### Decision Making
- Assigned to: #WilliamShields
- Description: Review all collected data regarding EIN acquisition, banking options, fees comparison, and account requirements to make an informed decision on where to open the business bank account.
