## Description

### Task Title: Preparation for Multicurrency Business Bank Account Setup

#### Task Description:

1. **Obtain EIN Number:**
   - Research the process for obtaining an Employer Identification Number (EIN).
   - Complete the necessary forms and submit them to the IRS.
   - Follow up on the application status and secure the EIN number.

2. **Identify Suitable Multicurrency Business Banks:**
   - Investigate banks that offer business accounts with multicurrency holding capabilities.
   - Compare fees, exchange rates, and services of potential banks.
   - Create a shortlist of banks that allow businesses to hold cash in multiple currencies without incurring conversion fees.

3. **Evaluate Bank Account Options:**
   - Assess each shortlisted bank's account features, including but not limited to:
     - Minimum balance requirements
     - Monthly maintenance fees
     - Online banking capabilities
     - Customer service quality
     - Additional benefits or services relevant to our business operations

4. **Prepare Documentation for Account Opening:**
   - Gather all necessary documents required by the chosen bank for opening a business account.
   - Ensure all documents are up-to-date and comply with banking regulations.

5. **Schedule Consultations with Banks (if necessary):**
   - Arrange meetings or calls with representatives from selected banks to discuss account options and negotiate terms.
   - Make informed decisions based on consultations and finalize the choice of bank.

#### Acceptance Criteria:

- EIN number is successfully obtained and documented.
- A comprehensive list of suitable multicurrency business banks is compiled.
- Detailed comparison of bank account options is conducted, highlighting pros and cons.
- All required documentation for opening an account is ready for submission.
- The best banking option for our needs is identified, potentially after consultations.

#### Notes:

- This task will enable us to streamline financial transactions involving multiple currencies, such as Euro payments received by team members like Goda, without incurring unnecessary conversion fees.

Please ensure all steps are completed thoroughly and keep the team updated on progress through the kanban board updates.
