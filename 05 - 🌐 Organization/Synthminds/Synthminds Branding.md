1. **Wordmarks (or Logotypes)**:
    - These are font-based logos that focus on the company's name alone. Think of Google or Coca-Cola. This style is great if you have a distinctive business name that can stand alone with strong typography.
2. **Lettermarks (or Monograms)**: **(NOT GOING FOR SM)** 
    - Similar to wordmarks, these logos are typically initials or abbreviations (e.g., IBM, HP). This type is useful for streamlining long or hard-to-pronounce names.
3. **Brandmarks (or Pictorial Marks)**: ("Exploration, journey, adventure") ("Compass") ("Sexton")
    - These are icon- or graphic-based logos. They are simple but powerful images that are emblematic of your company, like Apple's apple or Twitter's bird. They can be effective if you want an emblem to symbolically represent your brand.
4. **Abstract Logo Marks**:
    - Unlike brandmarks, which are recognizable images, abstract logos are geometric forms that don't represent something immediately recognizable (like Adidas's striped flower). They're great for conveying your brand's values or ethos without being tied to a specific image.
5. **Mascots**: ("Professor Synapse") ("Whip Master") ("Brain") ("TutorBot") ("**Astrolabe**") ("Queen") ("G")
    - Mascot logos are illustrated characters that represent your company. They are often colorful, cartoonish, and fun, like KFC’s Colonel or Michelin’s Bibendum. They're excellent for brands that want to create a wholesome, family-friendly atmosphere.
6. **Combination Marks**:
    - These logos combine a wordmark with a brandmark, abstract mark, or mascot. They offer versatility with both text and an icon or mascot, which can be used together or separately. Examples include Burger King and Lacoste.
7. **Emblems**: ("Approved by Synthminds") ("Certificates")
    - Emblems are usually more detailed and intricate. They include font inside a symbol or an icon, like badges, seals, or crests. Think of Starbucks or Harley-Davidson logos. They can give a traditional and classic feel.
8. **Dynamic Marks**: ("Evolvement of Brain ")
    - These are a newer type of logo design where the logo may change in appearance, showing different variations of the same theme. This type of logo is adaptable and can be a good fit for a brand that is diverse and multifaceted.
