## Org System
![[Synthminds Organism Chart.canvas|Synthminds Organism Chart]]
### Mission Control 🛰️
Our central nervous system - acts as the conduit to keep all the moving pieces organized, on track, and communication lanes flowing. 

### Commercial Hub 🎥
Responsible for increasing awareness about Synthminds and managing our brand.

### Business Generation 🤝
Responsible for building and strengthening client and partner relationship to drive revenue. 

### Learning Lab 🧪 
Responsible for creating and managing the education side of the business. 

### The Workshop 🛠️
Responsible for integrating or building AI enhanced solutions to client problems. 

## Tiers
### ![[Synthminds Structure.svg]]

### Tier 1
- Access to company tools and resources (e.g. Shared accounts, APIs, google workspace, notion access, etc.)
- Anything produced using these tools is considered company property unless otherwise discussed 
- Affiliate codes
- X# shares per year vested over 3 years
- Expectations
	- spending a # of your time on Synthminds work
	- educating yourself using our courses and resources
	- Producing SOMETHING that contributes value to the commons

### Tier 2
- Hourly pay for contract work
- Company-sponsored education and certifications (e.g., Mind Studio AI course, USA AI certifications)
- One-year agreement to work a certain number of hours per week in exchange for the education and certifications
- Additional equity

### Tier 3

- Part-time employment with the company
- Elimination of hourly billable work
- MORE SHARES

### Tier 4

- Full-time employment with the company
